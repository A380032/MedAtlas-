02 · Palpation

Apex beat, cardiac impulse and the “cat's purr”

First palpate approximately with the palm between the 3rd–6th ribs, then precisely with 2–3 fingertips. In women the breast is displaced upward and to the right.

Practice: where is the apex beat?

Choose a patient and click on the chest where the apex beat should be. The program tells you which intercostal space you chose and the distance relative to the midclavicular line.

A “cat's purr” is always a sign of an organic defect. To determine its phase, the pulse over the carotid artery is palpated at the same time: a thrill coinciding with the pulse is systolic, a thrill coinciding with the pause after the pulse is diastolic.

04 · Auscultation

Auscultation points and heart sounds

The heart is auscultated with the patient standing, supine and lying on the left side, in held inspiration and held expiration. The bell of the stethoscope is used for low-frequency sounds (S3, S4, the murmur of mitral stenosis), the diaphragm for high-frequency ones.

05 · Murmurs

Listen to the murmur and describe it

A murmur arises when blood flow is turbulent: when an orifice narrows, when blood flows backward through an incompetent valve, when blood viscosity falls, or when flow accelerates.

Plan for describing a murmur

  1. Phase: systolic or diastolic (compared with the carotid pulse)
  2. Relation to the sounds: does it start after the sound or “swallow” it
  3. Epicenter: the point where it is heard best
  4. Radiation: along the direction of blood flow
  5. Intensity: grade 1–6 on the Levine scale
  6. Shape: crescendo, decrescendo, diamond-shaped, plateau
  7. Timbre: harsh, sawing, blowing, rumbling, musical
  8. Dependence on position, breathing and exertion

07 · Arterial blood pressure

Measure blood pressure by the Korotkoff method

First, by the palpatory method, inflate until the pulse in the radial artery disappears, then add another 20–30 mm Hg. Deflate at 2–3 mm Hg/s. Press “Systolic” when the first sound appears and “Diastolic” when the sounds disappear completely.
