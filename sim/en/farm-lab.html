RememberDoses are written in grams (as a decimal fraction) or in milliliters: 0,5 (but not “500” or “.5”), 1,0 (but not “1”). Moving the decimal point one place changes the dose 10-fold. For officinal drugs (tincture, Validol) the concentration is not written.

04 · Calculations

Calculating percentages, mg/mL and a child's dose

Type the answer as a number (a decimal fraction with a comma or a point). The solution is shown step by step after each problem.

Formulas

Mass from percentage: substance (g) = total mass or volume × percentage / 100

mg/mL from percentage: multiply the percentage by 10: 1% = 10 mg/mL, 2.5% = 25 mg/mL, 0.1% = 1 mg/mL

Percentage from ratio: 1:1000 = 0.1%, 1:5000 = 0.02% (percentage = 100 / the second number of the ratio)

Volume: required volume (mL) = required dose (mg) / concentration (mg/mL)

Child's dose: dose = mg/kg × body weight (kg)

Steady state: Css is reached at about 4–5 × t½

05 · Pharmacokinetics laboratory

The drug in the blood: absorption, distribution, elimination

Choose the drug, route, dose and interval. The graph shows the concentration in the patient's blood. The green band is the therapeutic window, the red zone the toxic level. Move a finger or the mouse over the graph to see the concentration at any time.

About the modelThe simulator is based on a one-compartment pharmacokinetic model: average literature parameters for adults are used. For phenytoin, saturable (Michaelis–Menten) elimination is used. In a real patient the values vary widely, so this is a teaching tool: in treatment the dose is chosen from the instructions, clinical guidelines and measured blood concentrations.
