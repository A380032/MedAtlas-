## Nerv tizimi kasalliklari | Neurological diseases
Insult (stroke) | Bosh miya qon aylanishining o'tkir buzilishi tufayli miya to'qimasi zararlanishi; ishemik yoki gemorragik bo'ladi. | Stroke | Acute focal brain injury caused by disruption of cerebral blood supply, either ischemic or hemorrhagic.
Ishemik insult (ischemic stroke) | Miya arteriyasi tromb yoki emboli bilan to'silishi tufayli miya infarkti. | Ischemic stroke | Cerebral infarction caused by occlusion of a brain artery by a thrombus or embolus.
Gemorragik insult (hemorrhagic stroke) | Miya tomiri yorilishi tufayli miya to'qimasi yoki qorinchalarga qon quyilishi. | Hemorrhagic stroke | Bleeding into brain tissue or ventricles from a ruptured vessel.
Tranzitor ishemik hujum (transient ischemic attack) | Miya ishemiyasi tufayli bir necha daqiqa-soat davom etib, izsiz o'tadigan nevrologik buzilish. | Transient ischemic attack (TIA) | Brief episode of neurologic dysfunction from focal ischemia without permanent infarction.
Subaraxnoidal qon quyilish (subarachnoid hemorrhage) | Miya pardalari orasiga, ko'pincha anevrizma yorilishi tufayli qon quyilishi; "hayotdagi eng kuchli bosh og'rig'i". | Subarachnoid hemorrhage | Bleeding into the subarachnoid space, often from a ruptured aneurysm, presenting with sudden severe headache.
Epidural gematoma (epidural hematoma) | Bosh suyagi va qattiq parda orasida to'planadigan qon; bosh jarohati bilan bog'liq. | Epidural hematoma | Collection of blood between the skull and dura mater, usually after head trauma.
Subdural gematoma (subdural hematoma) | Qattiq va o'rgimchaksimon pardalar orasida qon to'planishi. | Subdural hematoma | Collection of blood between the dura and arachnoid membranes.
Epilepsiya (epilepsy) | Takroriy, sababsiz tutqanoq xurujlariga moyillik bilan kechadigan surunkali kasallik. | Epilepsy | Chronic disorder characterized by a predisposition to recurrent unprovoked seizures.
Epileptik status (status epilepticus) | 5 daqiqadan uzoq davom etuvchi yoki ongi tiklanmay takrorlanuvchi tutqanoq xurujlari; shoshilinch holat. | Status epilepticus | Seizure lasting more than 5 minutes or repeated seizures without recovery of consciousness; a medical emergency.
Isitma talvasasi (febrile seizure) | Bolalarda yuqori harorat fonida paydo bo'ladigan talvasa. | Febrile seizure | Seizure in a young child triggered by fever.
Meningit (meningitis) | Miya pardalarining yallig'lanishi; bosh og'rig'i, isitma va ensa mushaklari qotishi. | Meningitis | Inflammation of the meninges presenting with headache, fever, and neck stiffness.
Meningokokk infeksiyasi (meningococcal infection) | Neisseria meningitidis qo'zg'atadigan meningit yoki sepsis. | Meningococcal disease | Meningitis or sepsis caused by Neisseria meningitidis.
Ensefalit (encephalitis) | Miya to'qimasining yallig'lanishi, ko'pincha virusli. | Encephalitis | Inflammation of the brain parenchyma, often viral.
Migren (migraine) | Bir tomonlama, pulsatsiyalanuvchi, ko'ngil aynishi va yorug'likka sezuvchanlik bilan kechadigan takroriy bosh og'rig'i. | Migraine | Recurrent, often unilateral pulsating headache with nausea and photophobia.
Klaster bosh og'rig'i (cluster headache) | Ko'z atrofida juda kuchli bir tomonlama bosh og'rig'i xurujlari, ko'z yoshlanishi bilan. | Cluster headache | Severe unilateral periorbital headache attacks with lacrimation.
Parkinson kasalligi (Parkinson disease) | Substansiya nigrada dofamin hujayralari yo'qolishi tufayli tremor, qotish va harakat sekinlashuvi bilan kechadigan neyrodegenerativ kasallik. | Parkinson disease | Neurodegenerative disease with loss of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra, causing tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia.
Alsgeymer kasalligi (Alzheimer disease) | Xotira va fikrlashning progressiv buzilishi bilan kechadigan, demensiyaning eng ko'p sababi. | Alzheimer disease | Progressive neurodegenerative disorder with memory and cognitive decline and the most common cause of dementia.
Demensiya (dementia) | Kundalik hayotga xalaqit beradigan, orttirilgan kognitiv funksiyalarning progressiv pasayishi. | Dementia | Acquired progressive decline in cognitive function that interferes with daily living.
Tarqoq skleroz (multiple sclerosis) | Markaziy asab tizimida demielinizatsiya o'choqlari paydo bo'lishi bilan kechadigan autoimmun kasallik. | Multiple sclerosis | Autoimmune demyelinating disease of the central nervous system.
Amiotrofik lateral skleroz (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis) | Harakat neyronlarining progressiv yemirilishi; mushaklar zaifligi va atrofiyasi. | Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis | Progressive degeneration of motor neurons causing muscle weakness and atrophy.
Gilyen-Barre sindromi (Guillain-Barré syndrome) | Periferik nervlarning o'tkir autoimmun zararlanishi; yuqoriga ko'tariluvchi oyoq-qo'l zaifligi. | Guillain-Barré syndrome | Acute autoimmune polyneuropathy with ascending limb weakness.
Miasteniya (myasthenia gravis) | Atsetilxolin retseptorlariga antitanalar tufayli charchoqqa moyil mushak zaifligi. | Myasthenia gravis | Autoimmune disease with antibodies to acetylcholine receptors causing fatigable muscle weakness.
Nevralgiya (neuralgia) | Asab tarmog'i bo'ylab kuchli xurujsimon og'riq. | Neuralgia | Severe paroxysmal pain along the distribution of a nerve.
Uch shoxli nerv nevralgiyasi (trigeminal neuralgia) | Yuz sohasida elektr toki urgandek, qisqa muddatli kuchli og'riq xurujlari. | Trigeminal neuralgia | Brief, severe electric-shock-like facial pain in the trigeminal nerve distribution.
Yuz nervi nevriti (Bell falaji) (Bell palsy) | Yuz nervining o'tkir bir tomonlama zaiflashuvi, yuz mushaklarining falaji. | Bell palsy | Acute unilateral facial nerve paralysis.
Polinevropatiya (polyneuropathy) | Ko'p periferik nervlarning simmetrik zararlanishi. | Polyneuropathy | Symmetric damage to multiple peripheral nerves.
Gidrosefaliya (hydrocephalus) | Miya qorinchalarida orqa miya suyuqligining ortiqcha to'planishi. | Hydrocephalus | Abnormal accumulation of cerebrospinal fluid in the cerebral ventricles.
Bosh miya shikastlanishi (traumatic brain injury) | Tashqi kuch ta'sirida miya funksiyasining buzilishi. | Traumatic brain injury | Disruption of brain function caused by external mechanical force.
Miya chayqalishi (concussion) | Bosh jarohatidan keyin ong va xotiraning qisqa muddatli buzilishi. | Concussion | Transient disturbance of consciousness and memory following head trauma.
Koma (coma) | Tashqi ta'sirotlarga javobsiz chuqur ong yo'qolishi. | Coma | Deep unresponsive state of unconsciousness.
Afaziya (aphasia) | Til hosil qilish yoki tushunishning buzilishi. | Aphasia | Impairment of language production or comprehension.
Gemiparez (hemiparesis) | Tananing bir yarmidagi mushaklar kuchining pasayishi. | Hemiparesis | Weakness of one side of the body.
Gemiplegiya (hemiplegia) | Tananing bir yarmi mushaklarining to'liq falaji. | Hemiplegia | Complete paralysis of one side of the body.
Tremor (tremor) | Tananing biror qismining ritmik, beixtiyor titrashi. | Tremor | Involuntary rhythmic oscillation of a body part.
Ataksiya (ataxia) | Harakatlar muvofiqlashuvining buzilishi, muvozanat yo'qolishi. | Ataxia | Impaired coordination of movement with loss of balance.
Bosh aylanishi (vertigo) | O'zining yoki atrofning aylanayotgandek sezilishi. | Vertigo | Illusion of rotational movement of oneself or the surroundings.
Depressiya (depression) | Doimiy g'amginlik, qiziqishning yo'qolishi va energiya pasayishi bilan kechadigan ruhiy buzilish. | Depression | Mood disorder with persistent sadness, loss of interest, and low energy.
Shizofreniya (schizophrenia) | Gallyutsinatsiyalar, behuda fikrlar va tafakkur buzilishi bilan kechadigan surunkali psixotik kasallik. | Schizophrenia | Chronic psychotic disorder with hallucinations, delusions, and disorganized thinking.
Bipolyar buzilish (bipolar disorder) | Mania va depressiya davrlari almashinadigan kayfiyat buzilishi. | Bipolar disorder | Mood disorder with alternating episodes of mania and depression.
Tashvish buzilishi (anxiety disorder) | Haddan tashqari, nazoratsiz xavotir va uning jismoniy belgilari bilan kechadigan buzilishlar guruhi. | Anxiety disorder | Group of disorders with excessive, uncontrollable worry and associated physical symptoms.
Insomniya (insomnia) | Uyquga ketish yoki uyquni saqlashning surunkali qiyinligi. | Insomnia | Persistent difficulty falling or staying asleep.

## Yuqumli kasalliklar | Infectious diseases
Sil (tuberculosis) | Mycobacterium tuberculosis qo'zg'atadigan, o'pka va boshqa a'zolarni zararlaydigan surunkali infeksiya. | Tuberculosis | Chronic infection caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis, affecting the lungs and other organs.
Qizamiq (measles) | Yuqori darajada yuqumli virusli kasallik: isitma, yo'tal, kon'yunktivit va butun tanaga tarqaluvchi toshma. | Measles | Highly contagious viral illness with fever, cough, conjunctivitis, and a generalized rash.
Qizilcha (rubella) | Yengil kechadigan virusli toshmali kasallik; homilador ayollarda tug'ma nuqsonlarga olib keladi. | Rubella | Mild viral rash illness that causes congenital defects if acquired in pregnancy.
Parotit (mumps) | Quloq oldi so'lak bezlarining yallig'lanishi bilan kechadigan virusli kasallik. | Mumps | Viral illness with inflammation of the parotid salivary glands.
Suvchechak (varicella) | Varicella-zoster virusi qo'zg'atadigan, pufakchali toshma bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Varicella (chickenpox) | Varicella-zoster virus infection with an itchy vesicular rash.
Belbog'li temiratki (herpes zoster) | Varicella-zoster virusining qayta faollashuvi; nerv bo'ylab og'riqli pufakchali toshma. | Herpes zoster (shingles) | Reactivation of varicella-zoster virus causing a painful vesicular rash along a dermatome.
Poliomielit (poliomyelitis) | Poliovirus qo'zg'atadigan, orqa miya old shoxlari zararlanishi va bo'shashgan falaj bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Poliomyelitis | Poliovirus infection that can damage spinal anterior horn cells and cause flaccid paralysis.
Skarlatina (scarlet fever) | A guruhi streptokokk toksini qo'zg'atadigan, mayda nuqtali toshma va "malina tili" bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Scarlet fever | Group A streptococcal illness with a fine red rash and strawberry tongue.
Saramas (erysipelas) | Terining o'tkir streptokokk infeksiyasi, aniq chegarali qizarish va isitma bilan kechadi. | Erysipelas | Acute streptococcal skin infection with a sharply demarcated red plaque and fever.
Stafilokokk infeksiyasi (staphylococcal infection) | Staphylococcus aureus qo'zg'atadigan teri, yumshoq to'qima, o'pka, suyak va qon infeksiyalari. | Staphylococcal infection | Skin, soft tissue, lung, bone, and bloodstream infections caused by Staphylococcus aureus.
Qoqshol (tetanus) | Clostridium tetani toksini qo'zg'atadigan, mushak spazmlari va trizm bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Tetanus | Toxin-mediated disease caused by Clostridium tetani with muscle spasms and trismus.
Quturish (rabies) | Hayvon tishlashi orqali yuqadigan, deyarli doim o'limga olib keladigan virusli ensefalit. | Rabies | Viral encephalitis transmitted by animal bites that is almost invariably fatal once symptoms appear.
Brutsellyoz (brucellosis) | Chorva mollaridan yuqadigan, to'lqinsimon isitma va bo'g'im og'riqlari bilan kechadigan infeksiya. | Brucellosis | Zoonotic infection with undulant fever and joint pain acquired from livestock.
Bezgak (malaria) | Plasmodium parazitlari qo'zg'atadigan, anofeles chivini orqali yuqadigan, davriy isitma xurujlari bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Malaria | Mosquito-borne Plasmodium infection causing periodic fever paroxysms.
Virusli gemorragik isitmalar (viral hemorrhagic fever) | Isitma, qon ketish va ko'p a'zolar yetishmovchiligi bilan kechadigan og'ir virusli infeksiyalar guruhi. | Viral hemorrhagic fever | Group of severe viral illnesses causing fever, bleeding, and multi-organ failure.
Qrim-Kongo gemorragik isitmasi (Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever) | Shomil orqali yuqadigan virusli gemorragik isitma. | Crimean-Congo hemorrhagic fever | Tick-borne viral hemorrhagic fever.
COVID-19 (COVID-19) | SARS-CoV-2 virusi qo'zg'atadigan, nafas yo'llari va boshqa a'zolarni zararlaydigan infeksiya. | COVID-19 | Infection by SARS-CoV-2 affecting the respiratory tract and other organs.
Sifilis (syphilis) | Treponema pallidum qo'zg'atadigan, bosqichma-bosqich kechadigan jinsiy yo'l bilan yuqadigan infeksiya. | Syphilis | Sexually transmitted infection by Treponema pallidum that progresses through stages.
Gonoreya (gonorrhea) | Neisseria gonorrhoeae qo'zg'atadigan jinsiy yo'l bilan yuqadigan infeksiya. | Gonorrhea | Sexually transmitted infection caused by Neisseria gonorrhoeae.
Xlamidioz (chlamydia infection) | Chlamydia trachomatis qo'zg'atadigan, ko'pincha belgisiz kechadigan jinsiy yo'l bilan yuqadigan infeksiya. | Chlamydia infection | Frequently asymptomatic sexually transmitted infection caused by Chlamydia trachomatis.
Kandidoz (candidiasis) | Candida zamburug'lari qo'zg'atadigan teri va shilliq pardalar infeksiyasi. | Candidiasis | Fungal infection of skin and mucous membranes caused by Candida species.
Toksoplazmoz (toxoplasmosis) | Toxoplasma gondii qo'zg'atadigan, homilador ayollarda homilaga xavfli parazitar infeksiya. | Toxoplasmosis | Parasitic infection by Toxoplasma gondii, dangerous to the fetus during pregnancy.
Mononukleoz (infectious mononucleosis) | Epshteyn-Barr virusi qo'zg'atadigan, isitma, angina va limfa tugunlarining kattalashishi bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Infectious mononucleosis | Epstein-Barr virus illness with fever, pharyngitis, and lymphadenopathy.
Ichak infeksiyasi (intestinal infection) | Ovqat hazm qilish yo'lini zararlaydigan, ich ketishi va qusish bilan kechadigan infeksiyalar. | Intestinal infection | Infections of the digestive tract causing diarrhea and vomiting.
Bakteriya tashuvchilik (carrier state) | Kasallik belgilarisiz mikrobni organizmda saqlash va boshqalarga yuqtirish holati. | Carrier state | Harboring a pathogen without symptoms while being able to transmit it.
Epidemiya (epidemic) | Kasallik tarqalishining ma'lum hududda odatdagidan sezilarli oshishi. | Epidemic | Occurrence of disease clearly in excess of normal expectancy in a given population or region.
Pandemiya (pandemic) | Kasallikning bir necha mamlakat yoki qit'alarga keng tarqalishi. | Pandemic | Epidemic spreading across multiple countries or continents.

## Bolalar kasalliklari va akusherlik-ginekologiya | Pediatrics and obstetrics-gynecology
Prematur chaqaloq (premature infant) | Homiladorlikning 37 haftasidan oldin tug'ilgan chaqaloq. | Premature infant | Infant born before 37 completed weeks of gestation.
Chaqaloqlar sarig'i (neonatal jaundice) | Yangi tug'ilgan chaqaloqlarda bilirubin ortishi tufayli teri sarg'ayishi. | Neonatal jaundice | Yellow discoloration of the skin in newborns due to elevated bilirubin.
Chaqaloqlar gemolitik kasalligi (HDN) | Ona va homila qon guruhi (Rh yoki ABO) mos kelmasligi tufayli homila eritrotsitlari parchalanishi. | Hemolytic disease of the newborn | Destruction of fetal red cells by maternal antibodies due to Rh or ABO incompatibility.
Perinatal asfiksiya (perinatal asphyxia) | Tug'ruq davrida yoki yangi tug'ilgandan so'ng kislorod yetishmovchiligi. | Perinatal asphyxia | Oxygen deprivation of the fetus or newborn around the time of birth.
Bolalar serebral falaji (cerebral palsy) | Bolaning rivojlanayotgan miyasi zararlanishi tufayli harakat va holat buzilishi. | Cerebral palsy | Group of permanent movement and posture disorders resulting from damage to the developing brain.
Daun sindromi (Down syndrome) | 21-xromosoma trisomiyasi tufayli kelib chiqadigan, aqliy rivojlanish kechikishi va xarakterli tashqi belgilar bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Down syndrome | Trisomy 21 disorder with intellectual disability and characteristic physical features.
Surfaktant (surfactant) | Alveola sirt tarangligini kamaytirib, ularning qulashiga yo'l qo'ymaydigan fosfolipid-oqsil aralashmasi. | Surfactant | Phospholipid-protein mixture that lowers alveolar surface tension and prevents alveolar collapse.
Gipotrofiya (malnutrition, hypotrophy) | Bolalarda vazn yetishmovchiligi bilan kechadigan ovqatlanish buzilishi. | Malnutrition (hypotrophy) | Nutritional disorder in children with insufficient body weight for age.
Eksudativ diatez (exudative diathesis) | Bolalarda teri va shilliq pardalarning yallig'lanishga moyilligi. | Exudative diathesis | Tendency of children's skin and mucous membranes to inflammatory reactions.
Homiladorlik (pregnancy) | Urug'langan tuxum hujayraning bachadonda rivojlanishi (taxminan 40 hafta). | Pregnancy | Development of a fertilized ovum in the uterus over about 40 weeks.
Preeklampsiya (preeclampsia) | Homiladorlikning 20-haftasidan keyin gipertenziya va proteinuriya yoki a'zolar zararlanishi bilan kechadigan holat. | Preeclampsia | Pregnancy disorder after 20 weeks with hypertension and proteinuria or other organ dysfunction.
Eklampsiya (eclampsia) | Preeklampsiya fonida tutqanoq xurujlari rivojlanishi. | Eclampsia | Seizures occurring in a woman with preeclampsia.
Bachadondan tashqari homiladorlik (ectopic pregnancy) | Urug'langan tuxum hujayraning bachadon bo'shlig'idan tashqarida, ko'pincha naychada joylashishi. | Ectopic pregnancy | Implantation of a fertilized ovum outside the uterine cavity, most often in the fallopian tube.
Abort (abortion) | Homiladorlikning 22 haftagacha tugashi. | Abortion | Termination of pregnancy before viability, usually before 22 weeks.
Yo'ldosh oldi kelishi (placenta previa) | Yo'ldoshning bachadon ichki og'zi ustida joylashishi; og'riqsiz qon ketishi. | Placenta previa | Placenta located over the internal cervical os, causing painless bleeding.
Yo'ldosh ko'chishi (placental abruption) | Yo'ldoshning bachadon devoridan muddatidan oldin ajralishi; og'riqli qon ketishi. | Placental abruption | Premature separation of the placenta from the uterine wall, causing painful bleeding.
Tug'ruqdan keyingi qon ketish (postpartum hemorrhage) | Tug'ruqdan keyin 500 mL (kesar kesishda 1000 mL) dan ko'p qon yo'qotish. | Postpartum hemorrhage | Blood loss of more than 500 mL after vaginal delivery (1000 mL after cesarean) or hemodynamic compromise.
Endometrioz (endometriosis) | Bachadon ichki qavatiga o'xshash to'qimaning bachadondan tashqarida o'sishi; og'riqli hayz va bepushtlik bilan kechadi. | Endometriosis | Growth of endometrial-like tissue outside the uterus causing painful menses and infertility.
Mioma (uterine fibroids) | Bachadon mushak qavatining xavfsiz o'smasi. | Uterine fibroids (leiomyoma) | Benign smooth-muscle tumors of the uterus.
Tuxumdon kistasi (ovarian cyst) | Tuxumdonda suyuqlik bilan to'lgan pufak. | Ovarian cyst | Fluid-filled sac in the ovary.
Polikistoz tuxumdon sindromi (polycystic ovary syndrome) | Anovulyatsiya, androgenlar ortiqchaligi va hayz buzilishi bilan kechadigan endokrin buzilish. | Polycystic ovary syndrome | Endocrine disorder with anovulation, hyperandrogenism, and menstrual irregularity.
Bachadon bo'yni saratoni (cervical cancer) | Odam papillomavirusi (HPV) bilan bog'liq bachadon bo'yni xavfli o'smasi. | Cervical cancer | Malignant tumor of the cervix strongly associated with human papillomavirus (HPV).
Ko'krak bezi saratoni (breast cancer) | Sut bezi to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma, ayollarda eng ko'p uchraydigan saraton turlaridan biri. | Breast cancer | Malignant tumor of breast tissue and one of the most common cancers in women.
Mastit (mastitis) | Sut bezi yallig'lanishi, ko'pincha emizish davrida. | Mastitis | Inflammation of the breast, often during lactation.
Menopauza (menopause) | Tuxumdon funksiyasi to'xtashi tufayli hayzning butunlay to'xtashi. | Menopause | Permanent cessation of menstruation due to loss of ovarian function.
Bepushtlik (infertility) | Muntazam jinsiy hayot sharoitida 12 oy ichida homiladorlik yuz bermasligi. | Infertility | Failure to achieve pregnancy after 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse.
Amenoreya (amenorrhea) | Hayz ko'rishning bo'lmasligi: 15 yoshgacha boshlanmasligi (birlamchi) yoki to'xtab qolishi (ikkilamchi). | Amenorrhea | Absence of menstruation, either failure to begin by age 15 (primary) or cessation after onset (secondary).
Dismenoreya (dysmenorrhea) | Hayz davrida pastki qorin sohasidagi og'riqli spazmlar; birlamchi yoki boshqa kasalliklar tufayli ikkilamchi bo'ladi. | Dysmenorrhea | Painful cramps in the lower abdomen during menstruation, either primary or secondary to another condition.
