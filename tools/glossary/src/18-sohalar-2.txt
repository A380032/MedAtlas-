## Farmakologiya | Pharmacology
Biokiritilish (bioavailability) | Dorining ttizimli qon oqimiga tushadigan ulushi. | Bioavailability | Fraction of a drug reaching the systemic circulation.
Retseptor (receptor) | Dori yoki gormon bog'lanib ta'sir hosil qiluvchi hujayra oqsili. | Receptor | Cell protein to which a drug or hormone binds to produce an effect.
Qaramlik (dependence) | Dori yoki moddaning ruhiy yoki jismoniy ehtiyojga aylanishi. | Dependence | Psychological or physical need for a drug or substance.
Dorilar o'zaro ta'siri (drug interaction) | Bir dori boshqasining ta'sirini kuchaytirishi yoki susaytirishi. | Drug interaction | One drug increasing or reducing the effect of another.
Sinergizm (synergism) | Ikki dori birgalikdagi ta'sirining yig'indidan kuchliroq bo'lishi. | Synergism | Combined drug effect greater than the sum of each.
Teratogenlik (teratogenicity) | Dori yoki omilning homilada nuqson chaqirish xususiyati. | Teratogenicity | Ability of an agent to cause fetal malformations.
Antibiotik (antibiotic) | Bakteriyalarni yo'qotuvchi yoki o'sishini to'xtatuvchi dori. | Antibiotic | Drug killing bacteria or inhibiting their growth.
Sefalosporinlar (cephalosporins) | Keng spektrli beta-laktam antibiotiklar. | Cephalosporins | Broad-spectrum beta-lactam antibiotics.
Antiviral dorilar (antivirals) | Viruslar ko'payishini to'xtatuvchi dorilar. | Antivirals | Drugs inhibiting viral replication.
Antifungal dorilar (antifungals) | Zamburug' infeksiyalarini davolovchi dorilar. | Antifungals | Drugs treating fungal infections.
Gijjaga qarshi dorilar (anthelmintics) | Gelmintlarni yo'qotuvchi dorilar. | Anthelmintics | Drugs eliminating helminths.
Analgetiklar (analgesics) | Og'riq qoldiruvchi dorilar. | Analgesics | Pain-relieving drugs.
Opioidlar (opioids) | Opioid retseptorlariga ta'sir qiluvchi kuchli og'riq qoldiruvchilar. | Opioids | Strong analgesics acting on opioid receptors.
NYaQV (NSAIDs) | Yallig'lanishga qarshi nosteroid vositalar: ibuprofen, diklofenak. | NSAIDs | Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs such as ibuprofen and diclofenac.
Parasetamol (paracetamol) | Isitma tushiruvchi va og'riq qoldiruvchi dori. | Paracetamol | Antipyretic and analgesic drug.
Glyukokortikoidlar (glucocorticoids) | Yallig'lanishga va immunitetga qarshi buyrak usti bezi steroid gormonlari va ularning analoglari. | Glucocorticoids | Adrenal steroid hormones and analogs with anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive action.
Bronxodilatatorlar (bronchodilators) | Bronxlarni kengaytiruvchi dorilar. | Bronchodilators | Drugs that widen the bronchi.
Mukolitiklar (mucolytics) | Balg'amni suyultiruvchi dorilar. | Mucolytics | Drugs thinning sputum.
Antitussiv dorilar (antitussives) | Yo'talni bosuvchi dorilar. | Antitussives | Cough-suppressing drugs.
AFF ingibitorlari (ACE inhibitors) | Angiotenzin-konvertaza fermentini bloklab qon bosimini pasaytiruvchi dorilar. | ACE inhibitors | Drugs lowering blood pressure by blocking angiotensin-converting enzyme.
Kalsiy kanallari blokatorlari (calcium channel blockers) | Kalsiy kirishini bloklab tomirlarni kengaytiruvchi dorilar. | Calcium channel blockers | Drugs blocking calcium entry to dilate vessels.
Nitratlar (nitrates) | Tomirlarni kengaytirib stenokardiyada qo'llanadigan dorilar. | Nitrates | Vasodilating drugs used in angina.
Yurak glikozidlari (cardiac glycosides) | Yurak qisqarishini kuchaytiruvchi dorilar, masalan digoksin. | Cardiac glycosides | Drugs strengthening heart contraction, e.g., digoxin.
Antiaritmiklar (antiarrhythmics) | Yurak ritmi buzilishini davolovchi dorilar. | Antiarrhythmics | Drugs treating heart rhythm disorders.
Protonli nasos ingibitorlari (proton pump inhibitors) | Oshqozon kislotasi ajralishini kamaytiruvchi dorilar, omeprazol. | Proton pump inhibitors | Drugs reducing gastric acid secretion, e.g., omeprazole.
Surgilar (laxatives) | Ichak harakatini kuchaytiruvchi, ich yumshatuvchi dorilar. | Laxatives | Drugs promoting bowel movement.
Qusishga qarshi dorilar (antiemetics) | Ko'ngil aynishi va qusishni kamaytiruvchi dorilar. | Antiemetics | Drugs reducing nausea and vomiting.
Uyqu dorilari (hypnotics) | Uyquni chaqiruvchi yoki chuqurlashtiruvchi dorilar. | Hypnotics | Drugs inducing or deepening sleep.
Mahalliy og'riqsizlantirish (local anesthesia) | Muayyan sohada sezgini vaqtincha yo'qotish. | Local anesthesia | Temporary loss of sensation in a specific area.
Umumiy narkoz (general anesthesia) | Ong va og'riq sezgisini to'liq o'chirish. | General anesthesia | Complete loss of consciousness and pain sensation.
Mushak bo'shashtiruvchilar (muscle relaxants) | Skelet mushaklarini bo'shashtiruvchi dorilar. | Muscle relaxants | Drugs relaxing skeletal muscle.
Rp (prescription) | Shifokorning dori yozib berish retsepti. | Prescription | A physician's written order for medication.
Doza (dose) | Dorining bir marta yoki bir kunda qabul qilinadigan miqdori. | Dose | Amount of a drug taken once or per day.
Tomchi (drops) | Suyuq dori shakli, tomchilab qabul qilinadi. | Drops | Liquid dosage form taken by drops.
Malham (ointment) | Terida qo'llanadigan yog'li dori shakli. | Ointment | Greasy dosage form for use on the skin.
Krem (cream) | Teriga surtiladigan yarim qattiq emulsiya dori shakli. | Cream | Semisolid emulsion dosage form for the skin.
Eritma (solution) | Moddaning suyuqlikda erigan dori shakli. | Solution | Dosage form with a substance dissolved in liquid.
Sirop (syrup) | Shirin quyuq suyuq dori shakli. | Syrup | Sweet thick liquid dosage form.
Kapsula (capsule) | Qobiq ichiga solingan dori shakli. | Capsule | Dosage form enclosed in a shell.
Tabletka (tablet) | Presslangan qattiq dori shakli. | Tablet | Compressed solid dosage form.
Ukol (injectable) | Teri ostiga, mushak yoki tomirga yuboriladigan dori shakli. | Injectable | Dosage form given under the skin, into muscle, or into a vein.
