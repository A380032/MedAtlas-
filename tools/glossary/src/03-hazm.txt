## Hazm tizimi va jigar kasalliklari | Digestive and liver diseases
Gastroezofageal reflyuks kasalligi (GERD) | Oshqozon tarkibining qizilo'ngachga qaytishi tufayli jig'ildon qaynashi va qizilo'ngach shilliq qavatining zararlanishi bilan kechadigan surunkali kasallik. | Gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) | Chronic condition in which reflux of gastric contents into the esophagus causes heartburn and may damage the esophageal mucosa.
Reflyuks-ezofagit (reflux esophagitis) | Oshqozon kislotasining qizilo'ngachga qaytishi sabab bo'lgan qizilo'ngach shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi. | Reflux esophagitis | Inflammation of the esophageal mucosa caused by reflux of gastric acid.
Barrett qizilo'ngachi (Barrett esophagus) | Surunkali reflyuks natijasida qizilo'ngach pastki qismi yassi epiteliysining kolonsimon (ichak tipidagi) epiteliyga almashinuvi; adenokartsinoma xavfini oshiradi. | Barrett esophagus | Replacement of the distal esophageal squamous epithelium by columnar (intestinal-type) epithelium due to chronic reflux, raising the risk of adenocarcinoma.
Axalaziya (achalasia) | Qizilo'ngach pastki sfinkterining bo'shashmasligi va peristaltikaning yo'qolishi; disfagiya va ovqatning qaytishi bilan kechadi. | Achalasia | Failure of the lower esophageal sphincter to relax with loss of peristalsis, causing dysphagia and regurgitation.
Disfagiya (dysphagia) | Yutishning qiyinlashishi yoki ovqatning qizilo'ngach bo'ylab o'tishida to'siq sezish. | Dysphagia | Difficulty swallowing or sensation of obstructed passage of food.
Qizilo'ngach varikozi (esophageal varices) | Portal gipertenziyada qizilo'ngach pastki qismi venalarining kengayishi; yorilsa, hayot uchun xavfli qon ketishi mumkin. | Esophageal varices | Dilated submucosal veins in the lower esophagus due to portal hypertension that can rupture and cause life-threatening bleeding.
Gastrit (gastritis) | Oshqozon shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi. | Gastritis | Inflammation of the gastric mucosa.
Surunkali gastrit (chronic gastritis) | Oshqozon shilliq qavatining uzoq davom etuvchi yallig'lanishi; ko'pincha Helicobacter pylori sabab bo'ladi, atrofiyaga olib kelishi mumkin. | Chronic gastritis | Long-standing gastric mucosal inflammation, often caused by Helicobacter pylori and potentially leading to atrophy.
Atrofik gastrit (atrophic gastritis) | Oshqozon bezlarining yo'qolishi bilan o'tadigan surunkali gastrit; xlorid kislota ishlab chiqarilishi kamayadi, B12 tanqisligi mumkin. | Atrophic gastritis | Chronic gastritis with loss of gastric glands, reducing acid secretion and potentially causing vitamin B12 deficiency.
Helikobakter pilori infeksiyasi (Helicobacter pylori infection) | Oshqozon shilliq qavatini kolonizatsiya qiluvchi bakteriya; gastrit, yara kasalligi va oshqozon saratoni xavfi bilan bog'liq. | Helicobacter pylori infection | Infection of the gastric mucosa by H. pylori, linked to gastritis, peptic ulcer disease, and gastric cancer.
Peptik yara (peptic ulcer disease) | Oshqozon va o'n ikki barmoqli ichak yara kasalligi: Oshqozon yoki o'n ikki barmoqli ichak shilliq qavatining chuqur nuqsoni; asosiy sabablari H. pylori va nosteroid yallig'lanishga qarshi dorilar (NSAID) qabul qilishdir. | Peptic ulcer disease | Deep defects in the gastric or duodenal mucosa, mainly caused by H. pylori infection and NSAID use.
Yara perforatsiyasi (perforated ulcer) | Yaraning oshqozon yoki ichak devorini teshib o'tishi; xanjarsimon qorin og'rig'i va peritonit bilan kechadigan shoshilinch holat. | Perforated peptic ulcer | Ulcer penetrating the full thickness of the gastric or duodenal wall, causing sudden severe abdominal pain and peritonitis.
Zollinger-Ellison sindromi (Zollinger-Ellison syndrome) | Gastrin ishlab chiqaruvchi o'sma (gastrinoma) tufayli kislota ortiqcha ajralishi va qaytalanuvchi yaralar. | Zollinger-Ellison syndrome | Gastrin-secreting tumor causing excess gastric acid and recurrent, severe peptic ulcers.
Oshqozon saratoni (gastric cancer) | Oshqozon shilliq qavatidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma, ko'pincha adenokartsinoma. | Gastric cancer | Malignant tumor of the stomach, most often adenocarcinoma.
Funksional dispepsiya (functional dyspepsia) | Organik sabab topilmaydigan, qorin yuqori qismidagi og'riq, to'yinish hissi yoki to'lib ketish sezgilari. | Functional dyspepsia | Chronic upper abdominal pain, fullness, or early satiety without an identifiable organic cause.
Qorin og'rig'i (abdominal pain) | Qorin sohasida paydo bo'ladigan og'riq; ko'plab ichki a'zolar kasalliklari belgisi. | Abdominal pain | Pain felt in the abdomen, a symptom of many visceral disorders.
Ich ketishi (diarrhea) | Ich kelishining tez-tezlashishi va najas suyuqlashishi; odatda kuniga uch va undan ko'p marta suyuq najas. | Diarrhea | Increased frequency of loose or watery stools, usually three or more per day.
Qabziyat (constipation) | Najas chiqishining kamayishi yoki qiyinlashishi, najasning quruq va qattiq bo'lishi. | Constipation | Infrequent or difficult passage of hard stools.
Meteorizm (flatulence) | Ichakda gaz to'planishi sababli qorin dam bo'lishi. | Flatulence (bloating) | Excessive intestinal gas causing abdominal distension.
Malena (melena) | Yuqori hazm trakti qon ketishida qora, yopishqoq, o'ziga xos hidli najas. | Melena | Black, tarry, foul-smelling stool caused by upper gastrointestinal bleeding.
Qon qusish (hematemesis) | Qizil yoki "qahva quyqasi" rangidagi qon aralash qusish. | Hematemesis | Vomiting of red blood or coffee-ground material.
Ichak tutilishi (intestinal obstruction) | Ichak yo'lining mexanik yoki funksional to'silishi; qorin og'rig'i, qusish, qorin dam bo'lishi va gaz hamda najas chiqmasligi bilan kechadi. | Intestinal obstruction | Mechanical or functional blockage of the bowel causing colicky pain, vomiting, distension, and failure to pass stool or gas.
Appenditsit (appendicitis) | Ko'richak chuvalchangsimon o'simtasining yallig'lanishi; og'riq avval kindik atrofi yoki epigastriyda boshlanib, o'ng pastki qoringa ko'chadi. | Appendicitis | Inflammation of the vermiform appendix, with pain migrating from the periumbilical area to the right lower quadrant.
Peritonit (peritonitis) | Qorin pardasining yallig'lanishi; qorin devori taranglashishi va og'riq bilan kechadigan og'ir holat. | Peritonitis | Inflammation of the peritoneum causing abdominal rigidity and severe pain.
Yallig'lanishli ichak kasalliklari (inflammatory bowel disease) | Ichakning surunkali yallig'lanish kasalliklari: Kron kasalligi va yarali kolit. | Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) | Group of chronic intestinal inflammatory disorders comprising Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis.
Kron kasalligi (Crohn disease) | Hazm yo'lining har qanday qismini, ko'pincha yonbosh va yo'g'on ichakni zararlaydigan, transmural yallig'lanish bilan kechadigan surunkali kasallik. | Crohn disease | Chronic transmural inflammatory disease that can affect any part of the GI tract, most often the terminal ileum and colon.
Yarali kolit (ulcerative colitis) | Yo'g'on ichak shilliq qavatining to'g'ri ichakdan boshlanib, uzluksiz tarqaladigan yarali yallig'lanishi; qonli ich ketishi bilan kechadi. | Ulcerative colitis | Chronic mucosal inflammation and ulceration of the colon beginning in the rectum and extending continuously, causing bloody diarrhea.
Tirnash ichak sindromi (irritable bowel syndrome) | Qorin og'rig'i va ich kelishi o'zgarishi bilan kechadigan funksional ichak buzilishi; organik sabab topilmaydi. | Irritable bowel syndrome | Functional bowel disorder with abdominal pain and altered bowel habits without structural cause.
Seliakiya (celiac disease) | Glyutenga nisbatan autoimmun reaksiya natijasida ingichka ichak vorsinkalarining atrofiyasi va malabsorbsiya. | Celiac disease | Autoimmune reaction to gluten that causes villous atrophy of the small intestine and malabsorption.
Laktaza tanqisligi (lactase deficiency) | Laktaza fermenti yetishmasligi tufayli sut shakarining hazm bo'lmasligi; ich ketishi va meteorizm kuzatiladi. | Lactase deficiency | Insufficient lactase enzyme causing malabsorption of lactose, with diarrhea and bloating.
Malabsorbsiya sindromi (malabsorption syndrome) | Ingichka ichakda oziq moddalar so'rilishining buzilishi. | Malabsorption syndrome | Impaired intestinal absorption of nutrients.
Divertikulyoz (diverticulosis) | Yo'g'on ichak devorida kichik xaltachasimon bo'rtmalar hosil bo'lishi. | Diverticulosis | Presence of small outpouchings in the colonic wall.
Divertikulit (diverticulitis) | Divertikullarning yallig'lanishi; chap pastki qorin og'rig'i va isitma bilan kechadi. | Diverticulitis | Inflammation of colonic diverticula causing left lower quadrant pain and fever.
Yo'g'on ichak polipi (colonic polyp) | Yo'g'on ichak shilliq qavatidan ichak bo'shlig'iga o'sib chiqqan hosila; ba'zilari saratonga aylanishi mumkin. | Colonic polyp | Mucosal growth projecting into the colon lumen, some of which can become malignant.
Kolorektal saraton (colorectal cancer) | Yo'g'on yoki to'g'ri ichak shilliq qavatidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma, ko'pincha adenomatoz poliplardan rivojlanadi. | Colorectal cancer | Malignant tumor of the colon or rectum, often arising from adenomatous polyps.
Gemorroy (hemorrhoids) | To'g'ri ichak pastki qismi venalari tugunlarining kengayishi; qonash va og'riq bilan kechadi. | Hemorrhoids | Enlarged venous cushions of the anorectal region causing bleeding and discomfort.
Anal yoriq (anal fissure) | Orqa chiqaruv teshigi shilliq qavatining yirtilishi; defekatsiyada kuchli og'riq beradi. | Anal fissure | Tear in the anal mucosa causing severe pain with defecation.
Churra (hernia) | Ichki a'zoning qorin devori nuqsoni orqali tashqariga chiqishi. | Hernia | Protrusion of an organ or tissue through a defect in the abdominal wall.
O'tkir pankreatit (acute pancreatitis) | Oshqozon osti bezining o'tkir yallig'lanishi; kamarsimon qorin og'rig'i, qusish; sababi ko'pincha o't toshi yoki alkogol. | Acute pancreatitis | Acute inflammation of the pancreas presenting with severe upper abdominal pain radiating to the back, commonly due to gallstones or alcohol.
Surunkali pankreatit (chronic pancreatitis) | Oshqozon osti bezi to'qimasining qaytmas fibroz va funksiyasi buzilishi bilan o'tadigan surunkali yallig'lanishi. | Chronic pancreatitis | Long-standing pancreatic inflammation with irreversible fibrosis and loss of exocrine and endocrine function.
Oshqozon osti bezi saratoni (pancreatic cancer) | Oshqozon osti bezidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma, ko'pincha duktal adenokartsinoma; kech aniqlanadi. | Pancreatic cancer | Malignant pancreatic tumor, most commonly ductal adenocarcinoma, usually diagnosed late.
O't tosh kasalligi (cholelithiasis) | O't pufagi yoki o't yo'llarida toshlar hosil bo'lishi. | Cholelithiasis (gallstones) | Presence of stones in the gallbladder or bile ducts.
O't pufagi yallig'lanishi (xolesistit) (cholecystitis) | O't pufagi devorining yallig'lanishi, ko'pincha tosh bilan o'tning chiqishi to'silishi tufayli. | Cholecystitis | Inflammation of the gallbladder wall, usually due to obstruction of bile outflow by gallstones.
Xoledoxolitiaz (choledocholithiasis) | Umumiy o't yo'lida toshlar bo'lishi; sariqlik va xolangit xavfi. | Choledocholithiasis | Presence of stones in the common bile duct, with risk of jaundice and cholangitis.
Xolangit (cholangitis) | O't yo'llarining infeksion yallig'lanishi; isitma, sariqlik va o'ng qovurg'a ostidagi og'riq (Sharko uchligi). | Cholangitis | Infection of the bile ducts presenting with fever, jaundice, and right upper quadrant pain (Charcot triad).
Birlamchi sklerozlovchi xolangit (primary sclerosing cholangitis) | O't yo'llarining surunkali yallig'lanishi va fibrozi bilan o'tuvchi kasallik; yarali kolit bilan birga uchraydi. | Primary sclerosing cholangitis | Chronic inflammation and fibrosis of the bile ducts, frequently associated with ulcerative colitis.
Birlamchi biliar xolangit (primary biliary cholangitis) | Mayda o't yo'llarining autoimmun yallig'lanishi va yemirilishi; asosan o'rta yoshli ayollarda uchraydi. | Primary biliary cholangitis | Autoimmune destruction of small intrahepatic bile ducts, mainly in middle-aged women.
Sariqlik (jaundice) | Qonda bilirubin ortishi tufayli teri va shilliq pardalarning sariq tusga kirishi. | Jaundice | Yellow discoloration of skin and mucous membranes caused by elevated blood bilirubin.
Gepatit (hepatitis) | Jigar to'qimasining yallig'lanishi; virus, alkogol, dori yoki autoimmun sabablarga ko'ra yuzaga keladi. | Hepatitis | Inflammation of the liver caused by viruses, alcohol, drugs, or autoimmune mechanisms.
A virusli gepatit (hepatitis A) | Fekal-oral yo'l bilan yuqadigan, surunkali shaklga o'tmaydigan o'tkir virusli jigar infeksiyasi. | Hepatitis A | Acute viral liver infection transmitted by the fecal-oral route that does not become chronic.
B virusli gepatit (hepatitis B) | Qon va jinsiy yo'l orqali yuqadigan, surunkali gepatit, sirroz va jigar saratoniga olib kelishi mumkin bo'lgan virusli infeksiya. | Hepatitis B | Blood-borne and sexually transmitted viral infection that can lead to chronic hepatitis, cirrhosis, and hepatocellular carcinoma.
C virusli gepatit (hepatitis C) | Asosan qon orqali yuqadigan, ko'pincha surunkali kechadigan virusli gepatit; bevosita ta'sir qiluvchi antivirus dorilar bilan davolanadi. | Hepatitis C | Predominantly blood-borne viral hepatitis that often becomes chronic and is curable with direct-acting antivirals.
D virusli gepatit (hepatitis D) | Faqat B gepatit virusi mavjud bo'lganda ko'payadigan nuqsonli virus; kasallikni og'irlashtiradi. | Hepatitis D | Defective virus that replicates only in the presence of hepatitis B virus and worsens disease severity.
Alkogolli jigar kasalligi (alcoholic liver disease) | Alkogolni uzoq muddat iste'mol qilish natijasida jigarning yog'li o'zgarishi, gepatit va sirrozi. | Alcoholic liver disease | Spectrum of liver injury from chronic alcohol use, from steatosis to hepatitis and cirrhosis.
Metabolik bog'liq yog'li jigar kasalligi (MASLD) | Alkogolsiz, metabolik xavf omillari (semizlik, diabet) bilan bog'liq jigarning yog'li o'zgarishi. | Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) | Hepatic steatosis associated with metabolic risk factors such as obesity and diabetes in the absence of significant alcohol use.
Jigar sirrozi (liver cirrhosis) | Jigar to'qimasining qaytmas fibroz va tugunli o'zgarishi, funksiyasi buzilishi va portal gipertenziyaga olib keladi. | Liver cirrhosis | Irreversible fibrosis and nodular regeneration of the liver leading to impaired function and portal hypertension.
Portal gipertenziya (portal hypertension) | Darvoza venasi tizimida bosimning oshishi; assit, qizilo'ngach varikozi va splenomegaliya bilan kechadi. | Portal hypertension | Elevated pressure in the portal venous system causing ascites, esophageal varices, and splenomegaly.
Jigar ensefalopatiyasi (hepatic encephalopathy) | Jigar yetishmovchiligi tufayli markaziy asab tizimining buzilishi: xulq o'zgarishidan komagacha. | Hepatic encephalopathy | Neuropsychiatric disturbance from liver failure, ranging from altered behavior to coma.
Jigar yetishmovchiligi (liver failure) | Jigarning sintetik, zararsizlantiruvchi va boshqa funksiyalarining jiddiy buzilishi. | Liver failure | Severe impairment of hepatic synthetic and detoxifying functions.
Gepatotsellulyar karsinoma (hepatocellular carcinoma) | Jigar hujayralaridan chiqadigan birlamchi xavfli o'sma, ko'pincha sirroz fonida rivojlanadi. | Hepatocellular carcinoma | Primary malignant tumor of hepatocytes, usually arising in cirrhosis.
Gemoxromatoz (hemochromatosis) | Temirning ortiqcha so'rilishi va a'zolarda to'planishi bilan o'tadigan irsiy kasallik. | Hemochromatosis | Hereditary disorder of excess iron absorption and deposition in organs.
Vilson kasalligi (Wilson disease) | Mis almashinuvining irsiy buzilishi, misning jigar va miyada to'planishi. | Wilson disease | Inherited disorder of copper metabolism with copper accumulation in the liver and brain.
Gilbert sindromi (Gilbert syndrome) | Bilirubin konyugatsiyasining yengil irsiy buzilishi; stress yoki ochlikda vaqtinchalik sariqlik. | Gilbert syndrome | Mild inherited impairment of bilirubin conjugation causing intermittent jaundice during fasting or stress.
Gepatomegaliya (hepatomegaly) | Jigarning me'yordan ortiq kattalashishi; qovurg'a yoyi ostidan paypaslanishi mumkin. | Hepatomegaly | Enlargement of the liver beyond normal size, often palpable below the costal margin.
Splenomegaliya (splenomegaly) | Taloqning me'yordan ortiq kattalashishi; portal gipertenziya, qon va infeksion kasalliklarda uchraydi. | Splenomegaly | Enlargement of the spleen beyond normal size, seen in portal hypertension, blood disorders, and infections.
Dizenteriya (dysentery) | Yo'g'on ichakning yallig'lanishi bilan kechadigan qonli, shilimshiqli ich ketishi. | Dysentery | Intestinal infection with inflamed colon causing bloody, mucus-containing diarrhea.
Vabo (cholera) | Vibrio cholerae qo'zg'atadigan, juda ko'p suvsimon ich ketishi va suvsizlanish bilan kechadigan o'tkir ichak infeksiyasi. | Cholera | Acute intestinal infection by Vibrio cholerae causing profuse watery diarrhea and dehydration.
Qorin tifi (typhoid fever) | Salmonella Typhi qo'zg'atadigan, uzoq isitma va ichak shikastlanishi bilan kechadigan tizimli infeksiya. | Typhoid fever | Systemic infection by Salmonella Typhi with prolonged fever and intestinal involvement.
Salmonellyoz (salmonellosis) | Salmonella turkumi bakteriyalari qo'zg'atadigan oziq-ovqatdan zaharlanishga o'xshash ichak infeksiyasi. | Salmonellosis | Foodborne intestinal infection caused by Salmonella species.
Rotavirus infeksiyasi (rotavirus infection) | Bolalarda og'ir suvsimon ich ketishi va qusish keltirib chiqaradigan virusli gastroenterit. | Rotavirus infection | Viral gastroenteritis causing severe watery diarrhea and vomiting in young children.
Askaridoz (ascariasis) | Ascaris lumbricoides yumaloq gelmintlari qo'zg'atadigan kasallik. | Ascariasis | Infection with the roundworm Ascaris lumbricoides.
Enterobioz (enterobiasis) | Ostritsa (Enterobius vermicularis) qo'zg'atadigan, anus atrofidagi qichishish bilan kechadigan gelmintoz. | Enterobiasis (pinworm infection) | Helminthic infection by Enterobius vermicularis causing perianal itching.
Exinokokkoz (echinococcosis) | Echinococcus lichinkalari qo'zg'atadigan, jigar va o'pkada kistalar hosil bo'lishi bilan kechadigan parazitar kasallik. | Echinococcosis | Parasitic disease caused by Echinococcus larvae forming cysts, most often in the liver and lungs.
Giardioz (giardiasis) | Giardia lamblia qo'zg'atadigan ingichka ichak infeksiyasi; ich ketishi va meteorizm bilan kechadi. | Giardiasis | Small-intestine infection by Giardia lamblia causing diarrhea and bloating.
