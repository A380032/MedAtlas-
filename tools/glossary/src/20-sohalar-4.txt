## Qo'shimcha kasalliklar | Additional diseases
Umurtqa churrasi (disc herniation) | Umurtqalararo disk yadrosining kanal ichiga chiqib qolishi. | Disc herniation | Protrusion of the disc nucleus into the spinal canal.
Ishias (sciatica) | Dumba nervi bo'ylab beldan oyoqqa tarqaluvchi og'riq. | Sciatica | Pain radiating along the sciatic nerve from back to leg.
Tunnel sindromi (carpal tunnel syndrome) | Bilak o'rta nervining siqilishi, barmoqlarda uvishish. | Carpal tunnel syndrome | Compression of the median nerve with finger numbness.
Son bo'g'imi displaziyasi (hip dysplasia) | Chaqaloqda son bo'g'imi tuzilishining nuqsoni. | Hip dysplasia | Congenital defect of the hip joint structure.
Qiyshiq bo'yin (torticollis) | Bo'yin mushaklari qisqarishi tufayli boshning yon tomonga og'ishi. | Torticollis | Tilted head from contracted neck muscles.
Suyak sinishi (fracture) | Suyak yaxlitligining buzilishi. | Fracture | Break in the continuity of a bone.
Tortilish (sprain) | Bo'g'im boylamlarining cho'zilishi yoki yirtilishi. | Sprain | Stretching or tearing of joint ligaments.
Mushak yirtilishi (muscle strain) | Mushak tolalarining cho'zilishi yoki uzilishi. | Muscle strain | Stretching or tearing of muscle fibers.
Kontuziya (contusion) | Yumshoq to'qimalarning zarbdan ezilishi. | Contusion | Soft tissue bruising from impact.
Tamponada (cardiac tamponade) | Perikard bo'shlig'ida suyuqlik to'planib yurakning siqilishi. | Cardiac tamponade | Heart compression by fluid in the pericardial sac.
Disseminirlangan tomir ichi ivishi (disseminated intravascular coagulation) | Qonning tomirlar ichida yoyilib ivishi va qon ketishi. | Disseminated intravascular coagulation | Widespread intravascular clotting with bleeding.
Policitemiya (polycythemia) | Eritrotsitlar sonining haddan tashqari oshishi. | Polycythemia | Excessive increase in red blood cells.
Yo'g'on ichak saratoni (colorectal cancer) | Yo'g'on yoki to'g'ri ichakdan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma. | Colorectal cancer | Malignant tumor of the colon or rectum.
Jigar saratoni (hepatocellular carcinoma) | Jigar hujayralaridan chiqadigan birlamchi xavfli o'sma. | Hepatocellular carcinoma | Primary malignant tumor of liver cells.
Sut bezi saratoni (breast cancer) | Sut bezidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma. | Breast cancer | Malignant tumor of the breast.
Miomma bachadon (uterine fibroid) | Bachadon mushagidan chiqadigan yaxshi sifatli o'sma. | Uterine fibroid | Benign tumor of the uterine muscle.
Gestoz (gestosis) | Homiladorlikning kech toksikozi. | Gestosis | Late toxemia of pregnancy.
Homila gipoksiyasi (fetal hypoxia) | Homilaning kislorod yetishmovchiligi. | Fetal hypoxia | Oxygen deficiency in the fetus.
Rezus-nizo (Rh incompatibility) | Ona va homila rezus omillarining mos kelmasligi. | Rh incompatibility | Mismatch of maternal and fetal Rh factors.
Pufak surilishi (hydatidiform mole) | Yo'ldosh to'qimasining pufakchali o'zgarishi. | Hydatidiform mole | Vesicular degeneration of placental tissue.
Bachadon bo'yni yetishmovchiligi (cervical insufficiency) | Bachadon bo'ynining homilani ushlab tura olmasligi. | Cervical insufficiency | Inability of the cervix to retain the pregnancy.
Xurrak (snoring) | Uyqu paytida yuqori nafas yo'llari tebranishidan chiqadigan tovush. | Snoring | Sound from upper airway vibration during sleep.
Xavotir buzilishi (anxiety disorder) | Haddan ziyod doimiy tashvish bilan kechuvchi buzilish. | Anxiety disorder | Disorder with excessive persistent worry.
Travmadan keyingi stress buzilishi (post-traumatic stress disorder) | Og'ir voqeadan keyin paydo bo'ladigan xotira va qo'rquv buzilishi. | Post-traumatic stress disorder | Memory and fear disturbance after a severe event.
Diqqat tanqisligi sindromi (attention deficit hyperactivity disorder) | Diqqat jamlay olmaslik va giperaktivlik. | Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder | Inattention with hyperactivity.
Ishtaha yo'qolishi (loss of appetite) | Ishtahaning yo'qolishi. | Anorexia (loss of appetite) | Loss of appetite.
Giyohvandlik (drug addiction) | Giyohvand moddalarga patologik qaramlik. | Drug addiction | Pathological dependence on narcotic substances.
## Qo'shimcha belgilar va tekshiruvlar | Additional signs and tests
Kernig belgisi (Kernig sign) | Meningitda tizzani yozishdagi qarshilik va og'riq. | Kernig sign | Resistance and pain on knee extension in meningitis.
Brudzinski belgisi (Brudzinski sign) | Meningitda bo'yin bukilganda oyoqlarning o'z-o'zidan bukilishi. | Brudzinski sign | Reflex leg flexion on neck flexion in meningitis.
Murphy belgisi (Murphy sign) | O't pufagi yallig'lanishida chuqur nafasda o'ng qovurg'a ostidagi og'riq. | Murphy sign | Pain under the right ribs on deep breath in cholecystitis.
Shchetkin-Blyumberg belgisi (rebound tenderness) | Qorinni bosib qo'yib yuborganda og'riqning kuchayishi, peritonit belgisi. | Rebound tenderness | Pain increasing on release of abdominal pressure, a sign of peritonitis.
Ortner belgisi (Ortner sign) | O'ng qovurg'a yoyiga urganda o'ng qovurg'a ostidagi og'riq. | Ortner sign | Pain on tapping the right costal arch in cholecystitis.
Pasternatskiy belgisi (costovertebral angle tenderness) | Bel sohasiga urilganda buyrak sohasidagi og'riq. | Costovertebral angle tenderness | Pain on tapping over the kidney area.
Lasegue belgisi (Lasègue sign) | Ko'tarilgan to'g'ri oyoqda sciatica og'rig'ining paydo bo'lishi. | Lasègue sign | Sciatic pain on raising the straight leg.
Romberg holati (Romberg test) | Ko'zni yumib turganda muvozanatning buzilishini aniqlash sinovi. | Romberg test | Test for loss of balance with eyes closed.
Koplik dog'lari (Koplik spots) | Qizamiq oldidan yonoq shilliq qavatidagi mayda oq dog'lar. | Koplik spots | Small white spots on the cheek mucosa in early measles.
Trusso belgisi (Trousseau sign) | Gipokalsemiyada qo'l mushaklarining tortishishi. | Trousseau sign | Hand spasm in hypocalcemia.
Xvostek belgisi (Chvostek sign) | Yuz nervi urilganda yuz mushaklarining qisqarishi, gipokalsemiya belgisi. | Chvostek sign | Facial twitch on tapping the facial nerve in hypocalcemia.
Kussmaul nafasi (Kussmaul breathing) | Atsidozda chuqur va shovqinli nafas. | Kussmaul breathing | Deep noisy breathing in acidosis.
Cheyn-Stoks nafasi (Cheyne–Stokes respiration) | Chuqurlashib va sayozlashib boruvchi, apnoe bilan almashuvchi nafas. | Cheyne–Stokes respiration | Cycles of deepening and shallowing breaths with apnea.
Nog'ora tayoqchasi (clubbing) | Barmoq uchi qalinlashib, tirnoqning gumbazsimon bo'lishi. | Clubbing | Thickened fingertips with dome-shaped nails.
Akrosianoz (acrocyanosis) | Qo'l-oyoq uchlarining ko'karishi. | Acrocyanosis | Bluish discoloration of the hands and feet.
Urtikariya (urticaria) | Qichishuvchi, tez o'tuvchi yuza shishlar. | Urticaria | Itchy, transient raised swellings of the skin.
Angionevrotik shish (angioedema) | Teri osti va shilliq qavatning chuqur shishishi. | Angioedema | Deep swelling of subcutaneous tissue and mucosa.
Ekximoz (ecchymosis) | Terida katta qon quyilish dog'i. | Ecchymosis | Large area of skin bruising.
Xolestaz (cholestasis) | O't oqimining buzilishi. | Cholestasis | Impaired bile flow.
Kardiomegaliya (cardiomegaly) | Yurak o'lchamining kattalashishi. | Cardiomegaly | Enlargement of the heart.
Bo'yin venalarining shishishi (jugular venous distension) | O'ng yurak bosimi oshganda bo'yin venalarining tarang bo'lishi. | Jugular venous distension | Distended neck veins from raised right heart pressure.
Ko'krakdagi og'riq (chest pain) | Ko'krak qafasi sohasidagi og'riq, yurak va boshqa sabablardan. | Chest pain | Pain in the chest from cardiac and other causes.
Hansirash (breathlessness) | Nafas yetishmaslik hissi. | Breathlessness | Sensation of insufficient air.
Hushdan ketish (syncope) | Miya qon ta'minoti kamayishidan qisqa muddatli ong yo'qolishi. | Syncope | Brief loss of consciousness from reduced cerebral blood flow.
Terlash (sweating) | Ter bezlarining ter ajratishi. | Sweating | Secretion of sweat by sweat glands.
Diareya (diarrhea) | Suyuq va tez-tez najas ajralishi. | Diarrhea | Frequent loose stools.
Steatoreya (steatorrhea) | Najasda yog' ko'payishi. | Steatorrhea | Excess fat in stool.
Parenteral oziqlantirish (parenteral nutrition) | Oziq moddalarni tomir ichiga yuborish. | Parenteral nutrition | Giving nutrients intravenously.
Laboratoriya tahlili (laboratory test) | Qon, siydik yoki boshqa namunalarni tekshirish. | Laboratory test | Examination of blood, urine, or other samples.
Siydik umumiy tahlili (urinalysis) | Siydikning fizik, kimyoviy va mikroskopik tekshiruvi. | Urinalysis | Physical, chemical, and microscopic urine examination.
Nechiporenko sinovi (Nechiporenko test) | Siydikdagi hujayralarni 1 ml da hisoblash. | Nechiporenko test | Counting urinary cells per milliliter.
Zimnitskiy sinovi (Zimnitsky test) | Buyrakning siydik quyultirish funksiyasini baholash. | Zimnitsky test | Assessment of the kidney's concentrating ability.
S-reaktiv oqsil (C-reactive protein) | Yallig'lanishda jigar ishlab chiqaradigan o'tkir faza oqsili. | C-reactive protein | Acute-phase protein made by the liver in inflammation.
Prokaltsitonin (procalcitonin) | Bakterial infeksiya va sepsis ko'rsatkichi. | Procalcitonin | Marker of bacterial infection and sepsis.
D-dimer (D-dimer) | Qon laxtasi parchalanish mahsuloti, tromboz ko'rsatkichi. | D-dimer | Clot breakdown product, marker of thrombosis.
Revmatoid omil (rheumatoid factor) | Revmatoid artritda aniqlanadigan autoantitana. | Rheumatoid factor | Autoantibody found in rheumatoid arthritis.
Kalprotektin (calprotectin) | Ichak yallig'lanishini ko'rsatuvchi najas ko'rsatkichi. | Calprotectin | Stool marker of intestinal inflammation.
Onkomarker (tumor marker) | O'sma borligini ko'rsatuvchi qon moddasi. | Tumor marker | Blood substance indicating a tumor.

Diazepam (diazepam) | Tinchlantiruvchi va tutqanoqqa qarshi benzodiazepin. | Diazepam | Sedative and anticonvulsant benzodiazepine.

Glyukoza eritmasi (glucose solution) | Tomir ichiga yuboriladigan qand eritmasi. | Glucose solution | Intravenous sugar solution.

Regidratatsiya (rehydration) | Yo'qotilgan suyuqlikni tiklash. | Rehydration | Restoration of lost fluid.
Davolovchi jismoniy tarbiya (therapeutic exercise) | Kasallikni davolash uchun jismoniy mashqlar. | Therapeutic exercise | Physical exercises used to treat disease.

## Qo'shimcha | Extras
Sitomegalovirus (cytomegalovirus) | Gerpesvirus oilasiga mansub, homilada nuqson chaqirishi mumkin bo'lgan virus. | Cytomegalovirus | Herpesvirus that can cause fetal defects.
Epshteyn-Barr virusi (Epstein–Barr virus) | Yuqumli mononukleoz qo'zg'atuvchisi bo'lgan gerpesvirus. | Epstein–Barr virus | Herpesvirus causing infectious mononucleosis.
Yuqumli mononukleoz (infectious mononucleosis) | Isitma, angina va limfa tugunlari kattalashishi bilan kechuvchi virusli kasallik. | Infectious mononucleosis | Viral illness with fever, sore throat, and swollen lymph nodes.
Inson papillomavirusi (human papillomavirus) | So'gal va bachadon bo'yni saratoni bilan bog'liq virus. | Human papillomavirus | Virus linked to warts and cervical cancer.
Gripp (influenza) | Isitma, mushak og'rig'i va yo'tal bilan kechuvchi o'tkir respirator virusli kasallik. | Influenza | Acute respiratory viral illness with fever, myalgia, and cough.
Koronavirus infeksiyasi (coronavirus infection) | SARS-CoV-2 va boshqa koronaviruslar chaqiradigan nafas yo'llari infeksiyasi. | Coronavirus infection | Respiratory infection caused by SARS-CoV-2 and other coronaviruses.
Lyamblioz (giardiasis) | Giardia lamblia chaqiradigan ichak infeksiyasi. | Giardiasis | Intestinal infection caused by Giardia lamblia.
Taeniarinxoz (taeniasis) | Tasmasimon gijja bilan zararlanish. | Taeniasis | Infection with tapeworm.
Toksik shok sindromi (toxic shock syndrome) | Stafilokokk toksinlari chaqiradigan isitma, toshma va bosim tushishi sindromi. | Toxic shock syndrome | Syndrome of fever, rash, and hypotension from staphylococcal toxins.
Klebsiella (Klebsiella) | Pnevmoniya va siydik yo'li infeksiyasi chaqiruvchi gramma'nfiy bakteriya. | Klebsiella | Gram-negative bacterium causing pneumonia and urinary infections.
Sinegnoyniy tayoqcha (Pseudomonas aeruginosa) | Kasalxona infeksiyalari va kuyish yaralarida uchraydigan chidamli bakteriya. | Pseudomonas aeruginosa | Resistant bacterium found in hospital infections and burn wounds.
Klostridiya (Clostridium) | Qoqshol, gangrena va botulizm qo'zg'atuvchi spora hosil qiluvchi anaerob bakteriya. | Clostridium | Spore-forming anaerobe causing tetanus, gangrene, and botulism.
Lyme kasalligi (Lyme disease) | Kana orqali yuqadigan Borrelia infeksiyasi. | Lyme disease | Tick-borne Borrelia infection.
