## Nafas tizimi kasalliklari | Respiratory diseases
Pnevmoniya (pneumonia) | O'pka to'qimasining (alveolalar va interstitsiyning) o'tkir yallig'lanishi; asosan infeksion sabab bilan rivojlanadi, isitma, yo'tal va nafas qisilishi bilan kechadi. | Pneumonia | Acute inflammation of the lung parenchyma (alveoli and interstitium), usually infectious, presenting with fever, cough, and shortness of breath.
Krupoz pnevmoniya (lobar pneumonia) | O'pkaning butun bir bo'lagini egallaydigan pnevmoniya; ko'pincha pnevmokokk qo'zg'atadi, 4 bosqich (to'lib kelish, qizil va kulrang gepatizatsiya, yechilish) bilan o'tadi. | Lobar pneumonia | Pneumonia involving an entire lung lobe, most often caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae and passing through four stages: congestion, red hepatization, gray hepatization, and resolution.
O'choqli pnevmoniya (bronxopnevmoniya) | Bronxlar atrofidagi alohida o'choqlar shaklida tarqaladigan pnevmoniya; bronxit asorati sifatida ko'pincha keksalarda va bolalarda uchraydi. | Bronchopneumonia | Patchy pneumonia centered on the bronchi and bronchioles with scattered foci of consolidation; common in the elderly and young children.
Jamoatda orttirilgan pnevmoniya (community-acquired pneumonia) | Shifoxonadan tashqarida yoki shifoxonaga yotqizilgandan keyingi dastlabki 48 soat ichida paydo bo'lgan pnevmoniya. | Community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) | Pneumonia acquired outside the hospital or diagnosed within the first 48 hours of admission.
Nozokomial pnevmoniya (hospital-acquired pneumonia) | Shifoxonaga yotqizilgandan 48 soat va undan keyin rivojlangan pnevmoniya; ko'pincha dori-darmonga chidamli mikroblar qo'zg'atadi. | Hospital-acquired pneumonia (HAP) | Pneumonia that develops 48 hours or more after hospital admission, often caused by antibiotic-resistant organisms.
Aspiratsion pnevmoniya (aspiration pneumonia) | Oshqozon tarkibi yoki og'iz bo'shlig'i sekretining nafas yo'llariga tushishi tufayli yuzaga keladigan pnevmoniya. | Aspiration pneumonia | Pneumonia caused by inhalation of gastric contents or oropharyngeal secretions into the lower airways.
Pnevmotsistli pnevmoniya (Pneumocystis pneumonia) | Pneumocystis jirovecii qo'zg'atadigan pnevmoniya; asosan OIV/OITS bilan kasallangan va immuniteti susaygan odamlarda uchraydi. | Pneumocystis pneumonia | Pneumonia caused by Pneumocystis jirovecii, seen mainly in people with HIV/AIDS and other forms of immunosuppression.
Surunkali bronxit (chronic bronchitis) | Bronxlar shilliq pardasining surunkali yallig'lanishi; kamida ketma-ket ikki yil davomida yiliga 3 oydan ko'p balg'amli yo'tal bilan tavsiflanadi. | Chronic bronchitis | Chronic inflammation of the bronchi defined by a productive cough lasting at least 3 months per year for two consecutive years.
O'tkir bronxit (acute bronchitis) | Bronxlar shilliq qavatining odatda virusli, qisqa muddatli yallig'lanishi; yo'tal bilan kechadi va ko'pincha 2-3 hafta ichida o'tib ketadi. | Acute bronchitis | Short-lived inflammation of the bronchial mucosa, usually viral, causing cough that typically resolves within 2-3 weeks.
Bronxial astma (bronchial asthma) | Nafas yo'llarining surunkali yallig'lanish kasalligi: qaytalanuvchi xirillash, nafas qisilishi va yo'tal bilan, bronxlar qaytar torayishi (obstruksiya) va giperreaktivligi bilan namoyon bo'ladi. | Bronchial asthma | Chronic inflammatory airway disease with recurrent wheeze, breathlessness, and cough caused by reversible airflow obstruction and bronchial hyperresponsiveness.
Astmatik status (status asthmaticus) | Odatdagi davoga javob bermaydigan og'ir, uzoq davom etgan astma xuruji; nafas yetishmovchiligi va hayot uchun xavf tug'diradi. | Status asthmaticus | Severe, prolonged asthma attack unresponsive to standard bronchodilator therapy and threatening respiratory failure.
Surunkali obstruktiv o'pka kasalligi (SOO'K, COPD) | Havo oqimining qisman qaytar yoki qaytmas cheklanishi bilan tavsiflanadigan, asosan chekish sabab bo'ladigan kasallik; surunkali bronxit va emfizemani o'z ichiga oladi. | Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) | Progressive disease with persistent, not fully reversible airflow limitation, mainly caused by smoking and encompassing chronic bronchitis and emphysema.
Emfizema (emphysema) | Distal bronxiolalardan keyingi havo bo'shliqlarining kengayishi va alveola devorlarining buzilishi; o'pka elastikligi pasayib, nafas qisiladi. | Emphysema | Permanent enlargement of airspaces distal to the terminal bronchioles with destruction of alveolar walls, reducing lung elasticity and causing dyspnea.
Bronxoektaz kasalligi (bronchiectasis) | Bronxlarning qaytmas kengayishi va yiringli balg'am bilan surunkali yo'tal bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Bronchiectasis | Irreversible dilation of the bronchi causing chronic cough with copious purulent sputum and recurrent infections.
Kistoz fibroz (cystic fibrosis) | CFTR geni mutatsiyasi tufayli ekzokrin bezlar sekretining quyuqlashishi bilan o'tadigan irsiy kasallik; o'pka, oshqozon osti bezi va terlashni zararlaydi. | Cystic fibrosis | Autosomal recessive disease caused by CFTR gene mutations that thicken exocrine secretions, affecting the lungs, pancreas, and sweat glands.
O'pka abssessi (lung abscess) | O'pka to'qimasining yiringli yemirilishi natijasida hosil bo'lgan, devori bilan o'ralgan bo'shliq. | Lung abscess | Localized, necrotic cavity of pus within the lung parenchyma, usually following aspiration or necrotizing infection.
Gangrena (o'pka gangrenasi) | O'pka to'qimasining chegarasiz, chirituvchi mikroblar ta'sirida keng nekrozi; juda og'ir kechadi. | Pulmonary gangrene | Extensive, poorly demarcated necrosis of lung tissue caused by putrefactive organisms, with a severe clinical course.
Plevrit (pleurisy) | Plevra varaqlarining yallig'lanishi; ko'krak qafasida nafas olganda kuchayadigan og'riq bilan kechadi, suyuqlik to'planishi (ekssudativ) yoki yo'q (quruq) bo'lishi mumkin. | Pleurisy | Inflammation of the pleural layers causing sharp chest pain worsened by breathing, either dry or with fluid accumulation (effusion).
Plevral ekssudat (pleural effusion) | Plevra bo'shlig'ida suyuqlikning ortiqcha to'planishi; transsudat yoki ekssudat bo'lishi mumkin. | Pleural effusion | Abnormal accumulation of fluid in the pleural space, classified as transudate or exudate.
Empiyema (pleural empyema) | Plevra bo'shlig'ida yiring to'planishi; pnevmoniya yoki abssess asorati sifatida rivojlanadi. | Empyema | Collection of pus in the pleural space, usually as a complication of pneumonia or lung abscess.
Pnevmotoraks (pneumothorax) | Plevra bo'shlig'iga havo tushishi va o'pkaning qisman yoki to'liq tushib qolishi; to'satdan ko'krak og'rig'i va nafas qisilishi bilan boshlanadi. | Pneumothorax | Presence of air in the pleural space causing partial or complete lung collapse, presenting with sudden chest pain and dyspnea.
Taranglashgan pnevmotoraks (tension pneumothorax) | Plevra bo'shlig'ida bosim ortib, o'pka va o'rta chegara (mediastinum) siqilishi; shoshilinch dekompressiyani talab qiladi. | Tension pneumothorax | Life-threatening pneumothorax in which trapped air under pressure compresses the lung and shifts the mediastinum, requiring immediate decompression.
Gidrotoraks (hydrothorax) | Plevra bo'shlig'ida yallig'lanishsiz suyuqlik (transsudat) to'planishi; yurak yoki buyrak yetishmovchiligida uchraydi. | Hydrothorax | Accumulation of non-inflammatory transudative fluid in the pleural cavity, seen in heart failure, cirrhosis, or nephrotic syndrome.
Gemotoraks (hemothorax) | Plevra bo'shlig'ida qon to'planishi, odatda ko'krak qafasi jarohati natijasida. | Hemothorax | Accumulation of blood in the pleural space, usually from chest trauma.
Atelektaz (atelectasis) | O'pkaning bir qismi yoki bo'lagining havosizlanib, tushib qolishi. | Atelectasis | Collapse or incomplete expansion of part or all of a lung with loss of aerated volume.
O'pka sili (pulmonary tuberculosis) | Mycobacterium tuberculosis qo'zg'atadigan, o'pkani eng ko'p zararlaydigan yuqumli kasallik; uzoq davom etgan yo'tal, kechki terlash va ozish bilan kechadi. | Pulmonary tuberculosis | Infectious disease caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis that mainly affects the lungs, causing prolonged cough, night sweats, and weight loss.
Sarkoidoz (sarcoidosis) | Noma'lum sababli, to'qimalarda kazeoz nekrozsiz granulemalar hosil bo'ladigan tizimli kasallik; ko'pincha o'pka va ko'krak ichi limfa tugunlarini zararlaydi. | Sarcoidosis | Multisystem disease of unknown cause marked by non-caseating granulomas, most often affecting the lungs and hilar lymph nodes.
Idiopatik o'pka fibrozi (idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis) | Sababi noma'lum, o'pka interstitsiysining progressiv chandiqlanishi bilan o'tadigan, nafas qisilishi va quruq yo'talni keltirib chiqaradigan kasallik. | Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis | Progressive scarring of the lung interstitium of unknown cause, leading to worsening dyspnea and dry cough.
Pnevmokonioz (pneumoconiosis) | Changning o'pkada to'planishi va fibroz bilan kechadigan kasbiy kasalliklar guruhi. | Pneumoconiosis | Group of occupational lung diseases caused by inhalation of mineral dust with resulting fibrosis.
Silikoz (silicosis) | Kremniy dioksidi changini uzoq muddat nafas olish tufayli rivojlanadigan pnevmokonioz; konchilar va tosh ishlovchilarda uchraydi. | Silicosis | Pneumoconiosis caused by prolonged inhalation of crystalline silica dust, seen in miners and stone workers.
Asbestoz (asbestosis) | Asbest tolalarini nafas olish natijasidagi o'pka fibrozi; mezotelioma va o'pka saratoni xavfini oshiradi. | Asbestosis | Pulmonary fibrosis caused by inhaling asbestos fibers, increasing the risk of mesothelioma and lung cancer.
Ekzogen allergik alveolit (hypersensitivity pneumonitis) | Organik changdagi antigenlarga immun javob tufayli alveola va interstitsiyning yallig'lanishi (masalan, "fermer o'pkasi"). | Hypersensitivity pneumonitis | Immune-mediated inflammation of the alveoli and interstitium in response to inhaled organic antigens, e.g., farmer's lung.
O'pka tromboemboliyasi (pulmonary embolism) | O'pka arteriyasi yoki uning tarmoqlarining tromb (odatda chuqur venalardan) bilan to'silishi; to'satdan nafas qisilishi va ko'krak og'rig'i bilan kechadi. | Pulmonary embolism | Obstruction of the pulmonary artery or its branches by a thrombus, usually from deep veins, causing sudden dyspnea and pleuritic chest pain.
O'pka infarkti (pulmonary infarction) | O'pka arteriyasi tarmog'i to'silishi tufayli o'pka to'qimasining qon bilan ta'minlanmay, nekrozga uchrashi. | Pulmonary infarction | Necrosis of lung tissue resulting from occlusion of a pulmonary arterial branch.
O'pka gipertenziyasi (pulmonary hypertension) | O'pka arteriyalarida o'rtacha bosimning 20 mm sim. ust. dan oshishi; o'ng qorincha yuklamasini oshiradi. | Pulmonary hypertension | Elevated mean pulmonary arterial pressure above 20 mm Hg that increases right ventricular workload.
O'pka yuragi (cor pulmonale) | O'pka kasalliklari yoki o'pka gipertenziyasi tufayli o'ng qorinchaning kengayishi va gipertrofiyasi. | Cor pulmonale | Enlargement and hypertrophy of the right ventricle caused by lung disease or pulmonary hypertension.
O'pka shishi (pulmonary edema) | Alveola va interstitsiyda suyuqlik to'planishi; tez nafas qisilishi, ko'pikli pushti balg'am bilan kechadi. | Pulmonary edema | Accumulation of fluid in the alveoli and interstitium causing severe dyspnea and frothy pink sputum.
O'tkir respirator distress-sindrom (ARDS) | O'pka kapillyarlari o'tkazuvchanligining oshishi tufayli yuzaga keladigan, og'ir gipoksemiya va ikki tomonlama infiltratlar bilan kechadigan o'tkir nafas yetishmovchiligi. | Acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) | Acute respiratory failure from increased alveolar-capillary permeability, causing severe hypoxemia and bilateral lung infiltrates.
Nafas yetishmovchiligi (respiratory failure) | O'pkaning qonni kislorod bilan yetarli ta'minlay olmasligi yoki karbonat angidridni chiqara olmasligi. | Respiratory failure | Inability of the lungs to adequately oxygenate the blood or eliminate carbon dioxide.
Uyqudagi apnoe sindromi (obstructive sleep apnea) | Uyqu paytida yuqori nafas yo'llarining takroriy to'silishi, bunda nafas to'xtab, kunduzgi uyquchanlik va xo'rrak otish kuzatiladi. | Obstructive sleep apnea | Recurrent collapse of the upper airway during sleep causing pauses in breathing, snoring, and daytime sleepiness.
Laringit (laryngitis) | Hiqildoq shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi; ovoz bo'g'ilishi bilan kechadi. | Laryngitis | Inflammation of the laryngeal mucosa that causes hoarseness.
Krup (croup) | Bolalarda virus qo'zg'atadigan, hiqildoq va traxeyaning yallig'lanishi; hapshirib yo'talish ("hurish") va inspirator stridor bilan kechadi. | Croup | Viral infection of the larynx and trachea in young children causing barking cough and inspiratory stridor.
Epiglottit (epiglottitis) | Hiqildoq usti tog'ayining o'tkir yallig'lanishi; nafas yo'llarini to'sib qo'yishi mumkin bo'lgan shoshilinch holat. | Epiglottitis | Acute inflammation of the epiglottis that can rapidly obstruct the airway.
Sinusit (sinusitis) | Burun yondosh bo'shliqlari shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi. | Sinusitis | Inflammation of the paranasal sinus mucosa.
Rinit (rhinitis) | Burun shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi; burun bitishi, shilliq oqishi bilan kechadi. | Rhinitis | Inflammation of the nasal mucosa causing congestion and rhinorrhea.
Allergik rinit (allergic rhinitis) | Allergenlarga javoban burun shilliq qavatida IgE vositasidagi yallig'lanish; hapshirish va burundan suv oqishi bilan namoyon bo'ladi. | Allergic rhinitis | IgE-mediated inflammation of the nasal mucosa triggered by allergens, causing sneezing and watery rhinorrhea.
Faringit (pharyngitis) | Halqum shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi; tomoq og'rig'i bilan kechadi. | Pharyngitis | Inflammation of the pharynx causing sore throat.
Tonzillit (angina, tonsillitis) | Bodomsimon bezlarning yallig'lanishi; streptokokk sabab bo'lsa, yurak va buyrak asoratlari xavfi bor. | Tonsillitis | Inflammation of the palatine tonsils; group A streptococcal tonsillitis can lead to cardiac and renal complications.
Grippa (influenza) | Gripp virusi qo'zg'atadigan o'tkir nafas yo'llari infeksiyasi; yuqori isitma, mushak og'rig'i va yo'tal bilan boshlanadi. | Influenza | Acute respiratory infection caused by influenza viruses, with high fever, myalgia, and cough.
Ko'kyo'tal (pertussis, whooping cough) | Bordetella pertussis qo'zg'atadigan yuqumli kasallik; xurujsimon spazmatik yo'tal va nafas olishdagi "xirillash" bilan kechadi. | Pertussis (whooping cough) | Highly contagious infection caused by Bordetella pertussis, with paroxysmal coughing fits ending in a whooping inspiration.
Difteriya (diphtheria) | Corynebacterium diphtheriae toksini qo'zg'atadigan kasallik; halqumda zich plyonka hosil bo'lishi va yurak hamda asab asoratlari bilan kechadi. | Diphtheria | Toxin-mediated infection by Corynebacterium diphtheriae causing a thick pharyngeal pseudomembrane and cardiac and neurologic complications.
Legionelloz (Legionnaires' disease) | Legionella pneumophila qo'zg'atadigan og'ir pnevmoniya; kondensioner va suv tizimlari orqali yuqadi. | Legionnaires' disease | Severe pneumonia caused by Legionella pneumophila, spread through contaminated water systems and air conditioning.
Mikoplazmoz (Mycoplasma pneumonia) | Mycoplasma pneumoniae qo'zg'atadigan "atipik" pnevmoniya; yosh kattalar va maktab yoshidagi bolalarda quruq yo'tal bilan o'tadi. | Mycoplasma pneumonia | "Atypical" pneumonia caused by Mycoplasma pneumoniae, with dry cough in school-age children and young adults.
Bronxiolit (bronchiolitis) | Mayda bronxiolalarning yallig'lanishi, ko'pincha 2 yoshgacha bolalarda RS-virus sabab bo'ladi; xirillash va nafas qisilishi bilan kechadi. | Bronchiolitis | Inflammation of the small bronchioles, usually due to respiratory syncytial virus in children under 2, causing wheeze and respiratory distress.
Bronxopulmonar displaziya (bronchopulmonary dysplasia) | Chala tug'ilgan chaqaloqlarda uzoq vaqt kislorod va sun'iy ventilyatsiya natijasida rivojlanadigan surunkali o'pka kasalligi. | Bronchopulmonary dysplasia | Chronic lung disease of premature infants resulting from prolonged oxygen therapy and mechanical ventilation.
Chaqaloqlar respirator distress-sindromi (neonatal RDS) | Chala tug'ilgan chaqaloqlarda surfaktant tanqisligi tufayli alveolalarning tushib qolishi va nafas qiyinlashuvi. | Neonatal respiratory distress syndrome | Breathing difficulty in premature infants caused by surfactant deficiency and alveolar collapse.
O'pka saratoni (lung cancer) | Bronxlar yoki o'pka to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma; mayda hujayrali va mayda hujayrali bo'lmagan turlarga bo'linadi, asosiy xavf omili chekishdir. | Lung cancer | Malignant tumor of the bronchi or lung tissue, divided into small cell and non-small cell types, with smoking as the main risk factor.
Mezotelioma (mesothelioma) | Plevra yoki boshqa seroz pardalardan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma; asbest bilan bog'liq. | Mesothelioma | Malignant tumor of the pleura or other serous membranes, strongly associated with asbestos exposure.
Gemoptizi (hemoptysis) | Yo'talganda balg'am bilan qon ajralishi yoki qon tupurish. | Hemoptysis | Coughing up blood or blood-stained sputum from the respiratory tract.
Dispnoe (dyspnea) | Nafas qisilishi: havo yetishmasligining sub'ektiv, noxush sezgisi. | Dyspnea | Subjective sensation of breathlessness or difficulty breathing.
Siyanoz (cyanosis) | Qonda kislorodning kamayishi tufayli teri va shilliq pardalarning ko'kimtir tusga kirishi. | Cyanosis | Bluish discoloration of the skin and mucous membranes due to increased deoxygenated hemoglobin.
Stridor (stridor) | Yuqori nafas yo'llari torayganda eshitiladigan baland tovushli, qo'pol nafas shovqini. | Stridor | High-pitched, harsh breath sound produced by turbulent airflow through a narrowed upper airway.
