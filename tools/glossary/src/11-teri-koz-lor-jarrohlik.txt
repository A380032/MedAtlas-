## Teri, ko'z, quloq-burun-tomoq | Skin, eye, and ENT
Psoriaz (psoriasis) | Tangachali yaltiroq blyashkalar bilan kechadigan surunkali immun vositali teri kasalligi. | Psoriasis | Chronic immune-mediated skin disease with scaly erythematous plaques.
Ekzema (eczema) | Qichishish, qizarish va pufakchalar bilan kechadigan teri yallig'lanishi. | Eczema | Inflammatory skin condition with itching, redness, and vesicles.
Dermatit (dermatitis) | Terining yallig'lanishi, kontakt yoki allergik sabablarga ko'ra. | Dermatitis | Inflammation of the skin, due to contact or allergic causes.
Akne (acne) | Yog' bezlari va soch follikulalarining surunkali yallig'lanishi. | Acne | Chronic inflammation of sebaceous glands and hair follicles.
Qo'tir (scabies) | Sarcoptes scabiei kana qo'zg'atadigan, kechasi kuchayadigan qichishish bilan kechadigan teri kasalligi. | Scabies | Skin infestation by the mite Sarcoptes scabiei causing intense itching worse at night.
Pedikulyoz (pediculosis) | Bitlar tufayli kelib chiqadigan teri va sochli qism parazitar kasalligi. | Pediculosis | Parasitic infestation of the skin and hair by lice.
Dermatomikoz (dermatomycosis) | Teri, soch va tirnoqlarning zamburug'li infeksiyasi. | Dermatomycosis | Fungal infection of skin, hair, and nails.
Pemfigus (pemphigus) | Teri va shilliq pardalarda pufaklar hosil bo'lishi bilan kechadigan autoimmun kasallik. | Pemphigus | Autoimmune blistering disease of skin and mucous membranes.
Vitiligo (vitiligo) | Melanotsitlar yo'qolishi tufayli teri qismlarining oqarishi. | Vitiligo | Loss of melanocytes causing depigmented patches of skin.
Kuyish (burn) | Issiqlik, kimyoviy yoki elektr ta'siridan to'qima shikastlanishi; chuqurligi bo'yicha I-IV darajaga bo'linadi. | Burn | Tissue injury from heat, chemicals, or electricity, graded by depth.
Toshma (rash) | Teri yuzasida paydo bo'ladigan o'zgarishlar: dog', tugunchalar, pufakchalar. | Rash | Visible skin eruption such as macules, papules, or vesicles.
Qichishish (pruritus) | Tirnashga undaydigan teri sezgisi. | Pruritus | Itching sensation that provokes scratching.
Konyunktivit (conjunctivitis) | Ko'z kon'yunktivasining yallig'lanishi; qizarish va ajralma bilan kechadi. | Conjunctivitis | Inflammation of the conjunctiva with redness and discharge.
Glaukoma (glaucoma) | Ko'z ichki bosimi oshishi bilan ko'rish nervining zararlanishi. | Glaucoma | Optic nerve damage associated with raised intraocular pressure.
Katarakta (cataract) | Ko'z gavharining xiralashishi. | Cataract | Clouding of the lens of the eye.
Miopiya (myopia) | Uzoqdagi narsalarni yaxshi ko'rmaslik (yaqindan ko'ruvchanlik). | Myopia | Nearsightedness, with difficulty seeing distant objects.
Gipermetropiya (hyperopia) | Yaqindagi narsalarni yaxshi ko'rmaslik (uzoqdan ko'ruvchanlik). | Hyperopia | Farsightedness, with difficulty seeing near objects.
G'ilaylik (strabismus) | Ko'z o'qlarining parallel bo'lmasligi: ko'zlar turli tomonga qarab turishi. | Strabismus | Misalignment of the eyes, in which they do not point in the same direction.
To'r parda ko'chishi (retinal detachment) | To'r pardaning tagidagi qatlamdan ajralishi; ko'rish yo'qolishiga olib kelishi mumkin. | Retinal detachment | Separation of the retina from underlying layers that may cause vision loss.
Otit (otitis) | Quloq (tashqi, o'rta yoki ichki) yallig'lanishi; og'riq va eshitish pasayishi bilan kechadi. | Otitis | Inflammation of the outer, middle, or inner ear causing pain and hearing loss.
O'rta otit (otitis media) | O'rta quloq bo'shlig'ining yallig'lanishi, bolalarda tez-tez uchraydi. | Otitis media | Inflammation of the middle ear, common in children.
Eshitish pasayishi (hearing loss) | Tovushlarni eshitish qobiliyatining kamayishi. | Hearing loss | Reduced ability to perceive sound.
Quloqda shovqin (tinnitus) | Tashqi manbasiz quloqda jaranglash yoki shovqin sezish. | Tinnitus | Perception of ringing or noise in the ear without external source.
Burundan qon ketishi (epistaxis) | Burun bo'shlig'idan qon oqishi. | Epistaxis | Bleeding from the nasal cavity.
Adenoidlar (adenoid hypertrophy) | Burun-halqum bodomsimon bezining kattalashishi, burun orqali nafas olishni qiyinlashtiradi. | Adenoid hypertrophy | Enlargement of the pharyngeal tonsil obstructing nasal breathing.

## Jarrohlik, onkologiya va shoshilinch holatlar | Surgery, oncology, and emergencies
Jarohat (wound) | To'qima yaxlitligining buzilishi. | Wound | Disruption of tissue integrity.
Singan suyak (fracture) | Suyak yaxlitligining buzilishi. | Fracture | Break in the continuity of a bone.
Chiqish (dislocation) | Bo'g'im yuzalarining bir-biriga nisbatan joyidan siljishi. | Dislocation | Displacement of joint surfaces from their normal position.
Burilish (sprain) | Bo'g'im boylamlarining cho'zilishi yoki qisman uzilishi. | Sprain | Stretching or partial tearing of joint ligaments.
Ochiq sinish (open fracture) | Suyak sinishi bilan teri yaxlitligining buzilishi va sinish joyining tashqi muhit bilan aloqasi. | Open fracture | Fracture with a wound communicating with the outside environment.
Kompartment sindromi (compartment syndrome) | Fassiya bo'shlig'ida bosim ortishi natijasida mushak va nervlarning qon ta'minoti buzilishi. | Compartment syndrome | Raised pressure within a fascial compartment compromising blood supply to muscle and nerves.
Jarrohlik gemostazi (surgical hemostasis) | Jarrohlikda qon ketishini to'xtatish usullari majmuasi. | Surgical hemostasis | Methods used to stop bleeding in surgery.
Jarrohlik infeksiyasi (surgical site infection) | Operatsiyadan keyin jarrohlik joyida paydo bo'ladigan infeksiya. | Surgical site infection | Infection occurring at the site of a surgical procedure.
Operatsiyadan keyingi davr (postoperative period) | Operatsiya tugagandan so'ng bemor tiklanguniga qadar bo'lgan davr. | Postoperative period | Period after surgery until patient recovery.
Anesteziya (anesthesia) | Og'riq sezgisining yo'qotilishi, umumiy yoki mahalliy bo'lishi mumkin. | Anesthesia | Loss of sensation, either general or local.
O'tkir qorin (acute abdomen) | Qorin bo'shlig'idagi kasalliklar tufayli to'satdan paydo bo'lgan kuchli og'riq, shoshilinch jarrohlik yordamini talab qiluvchi holat. | Acute abdomen | Sudden severe abdominal pain from intra-abdominal disease, often requiring urgent surgery.
Xolesistektomiya (cholecystectomy) | O't pufagini jarrohlik yo'li bilan olib tashlash. | Cholecystectomy | Surgical removal of the gallbladder.
Appendektomiya (appendectomy) | Chuvalchangsimon o'simtani jarrohlik yo'li bilan olib tashlash. | Appendectomy | Surgical removal of the appendix.
Laparoskopiya (laparoscopy) | Qorin devoridagi kichik kesmalar orqali kamera yordamida o'tkaziladigan minimal invaziv usul. | Laparoscopy | Minimally invasive technique using a camera inserted through small abdominal incisions.
Stent (stent) | Tomir yoki boshqa kanalni ochiq tutuvchi ichi kovak tuzilma. | Stent | Tubular device that keeps a vessel or duct open.
Koronar angioplastika (coronary angioplasty) | Torayib qolgan koronar arteriyani balon va stent yordamida kengaytirish. | Coronary angioplasty | Widening a narrowed coronary artery using a balloon and stent.
Koronar shuntlash (coronary artery bypass grafting) | Torayib qolgan koronar arteriya o'rniga tomir transplantati bilan aylanma yo'l yaratish. | Coronary artery bypass grafting | Surgery creating a graft bypass around a blocked coronary artery.
Kardiostimulyator (pacemaker) | Yurak ritmini boshqarish uchun elektr impulslar yuboruvchi qurilma. | Pacemaker | Device that delivers electrical impulses to regulate heart rhythm.
Defibrillyatsiya (defibrillation) | Qorincha fibrillyatsiyasini bartaraf etish uchun yurakka elektr zaryad berish. | Defibrillation | Delivery of an electric shock to terminate ventricular fibrillation.
Yurak-o'pka reanimatsiyasi (cardiopulmonary resuscitation) | Yurak to'xtaganda ko'krak qafasi massaji va sun'iy nafas bilan qon aylanishini tiklash. | Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) | Chest compressions and rescue breathing to restore circulation after cardiac arrest.
Intubatsiya (intubation) | Nafas yo'llarini ta'minlash uchun traxeyaga naycha kiritish. | Intubation | Insertion of a tube into the trachea to secure the airway.
Sun'iy o'pka ventilyatsiyasi (mechanical ventilation) | Nafasni mashina yordamida ta'minlash. | Mechanical ventilation | Support of breathing by a machine.
Traxeostomiya (tracheostomy) | Nafas yo'llarini ta'minlash uchun bo'yindan traxeyaga teshik ochish. | Tracheostomy | Surgical opening in the neck into the trachea to secure an airway.
Infuzion terapiya (infusion therapy) | Tomir ichiga suyuqlik va dorilar yuborish orqali davolash. | Infusion therapy | Treatment by intravenous administration of fluids and drugs.
Qon quyish (blood transfusion) | Donor qonini yoki uning tarkibiy qismlarini bemorga yuborish. | Blood transfusion | Administration of donor blood or blood components to a patient.
Qon guruhi (blood group) | Eritrotsitlardagi antigenlarga ko'ra qonning tasnifi (ABO, rezus). | Blood group | Classification of blood by red cell antigens (ABO, Rh).
Rezus-omil (Rh factor) | Eritrotsitlardagi D antigeni; bor bo'lsa rezus-musbat deyiladi. | Rh factor | D antigen on red cells; its presence is termed Rh-positive.
Triaj (triage) | Bemorlarni holat og'irligi va shoshilinchligiga qarab saralash. | Triage | Sorting of patients by severity and urgency of condition.
Birinchi yordam (first aid) | Baxtsiz hodisa yoki kasallikda tibbiy yordam kelguncha ko'rsatiladigan dastlabki yordam. | First aid | Initial care given before professional medical help arrives.
Travmatik shok (traumatic shock) | Og'ir jarohat tufayli qon yo'qotish va og'riq oqibatida to'qimalar qon ta'minotining buzilishi. | Traumatic shock | Circulatory failure from severe trauma, blood loss, and pain.
Gipovolemik shok (hypovolemic shock) | Qon yoki suyuqlik hajmi keskin kamayishi tufayli rivojlanadigan shok. | Hypovolemic shock | Shock caused by severe loss of blood or fluid volume.
Elektr toki urishi (electrical injury) | Elektr toki ta'sirida to'qimalar, yurak va nerv tizimining shikastlanishi. | Electrical injury | Tissue, cardiac, and neurologic injury caused by electric current.
Cho'kish (drowning) | Suyuqlikka cho'mganda nafas yo'llariga suv kirishi tufayli kislorod yetishmovchiligi. | Drowning | Respiratory impairment from submersion or immersion in liquid.
Issiq urishi (heat stroke) | Tana harorati juda ko'tarilib, markaziy asab tizimi buzilishi bilan kechadigan hayot uchun xavfli holat. | Heat stroke | Life-threatening hyperthermia with central nervous system dysfunction.
Sovuq urishi (frostbite) | Past harorat ta'sirida to'qimalarning muzlashi. | Frostbite | Freezing injury of tissues from cold exposure.
Ilon chaqishi (snakebite) | Zaharli ilon chaqishi natijasida zaharlanish. | Snakebite | Envenomation caused by the bite of a venomous snake.
Kimyoterapiya (chemotherapy) | O'sma hujayralarini yo'q qiluvchi dorilar bilan davolash. | Chemotherapy | Treatment with drugs that destroy tumor cells.
Nur terapiyasi (radiotherapy) | Ionlashtiruvchi nurlanish yordamida o'smani davolash. | Radiotherapy | Treatment of tumors with ionizing radiation.
Immunoterapiya (immunotherapy) | Immun tizimini faollashtirish yordamida o'smalar va boshqa kasalliklarni davolash. | Immunotherapy | Treatment that harnesses the immune system against tumors and other diseases.
Remissiya (remission) | Kasallik belgilarining kamayishi yoki yo'qolishi. | Remission | Decrease or disappearance of signs and symptoms of disease.
Residiv (relapse) | Remissiyadan keyin kasallik belgilarining qaytishi. | Relapse | Return of disease signs after remission.
Asorat (complication) | Asosiy kasallik yoki davolash fonida paydo bo'lgan qo'shimcha holat. | Complication | Additional condition arising during the course of a disease or its treatment.
Surunkali kasallik (chronic disease) | Uzoq davom etuvchi, odatda 3 oydan ortiq kechadigan kasallik. | Chronic disease | Long-lasting condition, typically persisting more than three months.
O'tkir kasallik (acute disease) | Tez boshlanadigan va nisbatan qisqa davom etadigan kasallik. | Acute disease | Condition with rapid onset and relatively short duration.
Palliativ yordam (palliative care) | Davolab bo'lmaydigan kasallikda og'riq va azoblarni kamaytirishga qaratilgan yordam. | Palliative care | Care that relieves pain and suffering in incurable disease.
