## Qo'shimcha atamalar (Z–M) | Additional terms (Z–M)
Qorachiq reaksiyasi (pupillary reflex) | Yorug'likka qorachiqning torayishi, qorong'ida kengayishi. | Pupillary reflex | Constriction of the pupil in light and dilation in darkness.
Qorachiq (pupil) | Rangdor pardadagi yorug'lik o'tadigan markaziy teshik. | Pupil | The central opening of the iris through which light passes.
Ko'rish (vision) | Ko'z orqali yorug'likni qabul qilib, narsalarni idrok qilish qobiliyati. | Vision | The ability to perceive objects through light received by the eye.
Ko'rish nervi (optic nerve) | To'r pardadan ko'rish impulslarini miyaga olib boruvchi II juft bosh miya nervi. | Optic nerve | Cranial nerve II carrying visual signals from the retina to the brain.

Tishsimon tuzilma (dentate) | Tish yoki arra tishiga o'xshash shaklli tuzilma. | Dentate | Structure shaped like a tooth or serrated edge.
Tish suyagi (ebur dentis) | Tishning emal ostidagi asosiy moddasi, dentin. | Dentin (ebur) | The main substance of the tooth beneath the enamel.
Iatrogeniya (iatrogenesis) | Shifokorning harakati yoki so'zi sabab bo'lgan kasallik yoki asorat. | Iatrogenesis | Disease or complication caused by a physician's action or words.
Igna tutqich (needle holder) | Jarrohlikda tikuv ignasini ushlab turuvchi asbob. | Needle holder | Surgical instrument that grips the suturing needle.
Idiopatik (idiopathic) | Sababi noma'lum, o'z-o'zidan paydo bo'ladigan. | Idiopathic | Of unknown cause, arising spontaneously.
Idiosinkraziya (idiosyncrasy) | Ayrim moddalarga tug'ma yuqori yoki g'ayrioddiy sezgirlik. | Idiosyncrasy | Unusual individual sensitivity to certain substances.
Idiotiya (idiocy) | Aqliy zaiflikning eng og'ir darajasi. | Idiocy | The most severe degree of intellectual disability.
Eyakulyatsiya (ejaculation) | Jinsiy aloqa vaqtida urug'ning chiqishi. | Ejaculation | Expulsion of semen during sexual climax.
Egat (gyrus) | Bosh miya po'stlog'idagi burmalar orasidagi tizma. | Gyrus | Ridge between folds on the surface of the cerebral cortex.
Ta'm buzilishi (dysgeusia) | Ta'm sezish sifatining buzilishi. | Dysgeusia | Distortion of the sense of taste.
Uyqu inversiyasi (sleep inversion) | Kunduzi uxlab, tunda uyg'oq bo'lish holati. | Sleep inversion | Sleeping by day and being awake at night.
Egilma (flexure) | A'zoning bukilgan joyi, masalan, yo'g'on ichak egilmalari. | Flexure | A bend in an organ, such as the colonic flexures.
Detrusor (detrusor) | Siydik pufagi devorining siydikni chiqaruvchi silliq mushagi. | Detrusor muscle | Smooth muscle of the bladder wall that expels urine.
Jig'ildon qaynashi (heartburn, pyrosis) | To'sh orqasidagi achishuvchi og'riq, oshqozon kislotasining qizilo'ngachga qaytishidan. | Pyrosis (heartburn) | Burning behind the breastbone from acid reflux into the esophagus.
Tuzalish (recovery) | Kasallikning tugab, sog'liqning tiklanishi. | Recovery | End of illness with restoration of health.
Efuziya (effusion) | Suyuqlikning tabiiy bo'shliqqa yoki to'qimaga to'planishi. | Effusion | Escape of fluid into a body cavity or tissue.
O'zgaruvchanlik (variability) | Organizmlar belgilarining bir-biridan farq qilishi. | Variability | Differences in traits between organisms.
Tos konyugatalari (pelvic conjugates) | Tos kirish qismining old-orqa o'lchamlari, tug'ruqda muhim. | Pelvic conjugates | Anteroposterior diameters of the pelvic inlet, important in labor.
Izokoriya (isocoria) | Ikkala ko'z qorachig'ining bir xil kattalikda bo'lishi. | Isocoria | Equal size of both pupils.
Izolyatsiya (isolation) | Yuqumli bemorni boshqalardan ajratib qo'yish. | Isolation | Separation of an infectious patient from others.
Izotop (isotope) | Atom soni bir xil, massasi har xil bo'lgan element turi. | Isotope | Atoms of one element with different mass numbers.

Boldir mushagi (calf) | Boldirning orqa qismidagi mushak guruhi. | Calf (sura) | The muscular back part of the lower leg.

Impuls (impulse) | Nerv bo'ylab tarqaladigan elektr signal. | Impulse | Electrical signal propagated along a nerve.
