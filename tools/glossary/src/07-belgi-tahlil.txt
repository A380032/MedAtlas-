## Belgilar, tekshiruv va tahlillar | Signs, examinations, and tests
Anamnez (anamnesis) | Bemorning kasallik tarixi va hayoti haqidagi, uning o'zi yoki yaqinlaridan olingan ma'lumotlar. | Anamnesis (medical history) | Information about the patient's illness and life history obtained from the patient or relatives.
Simptom (symptom) | Bemor o'zi his qiladigan, kasallikka xos sub'ektiv shikoyat. | Symptom | Subjective complaint reported by the patient that is associated with disease.
Belgi (klinik belgi) (sign) | Shifokor tekshiruvda aniqlaydigan ob'ektiv o'zgarish. | Clinical sign | Objective finding detected by the examiner.
Sindrom (syndrome) | O'zaro patogenetik bog'liq simptomlar va belgilar majmuasi. | Syndrome | Set of symptoms and signs that occur together and share a common pathogenesis.
Diagnoz (diagnosis) | Bemorning kasalligi yoki holatining shifokor tomonidan aniqlangan nomi va tavsifi. | Diagnosis | Identification of the patient's disease or condition by the clinician.
Differensial diagnostika (differential diagnosis) | O'xshash belgili kasalliklarni bir-biridan ajratish orqali to'g'ri tashxisga kelish jarayoni. | Differential diagnosis | Process of distinguishing between diseases with similar presentations to reach the correct diagnosis.
Prognoz (prognosis) | Kasallikning kutilayotgan kechishi va oqibati. | Prognosis | Expected course and outcome of a disease.
Etiologiya (etiology) | Kasallikning kelib chiqish sababi. | Etiology | The cause or set of causes of a disease.
Patogenez (pathogenesis) | Kasallik rivojlanish mexanizmi. | Pathogenesis | The mechanism by which a disease develops.
Palpatsiya (palpation) | A'zolarni qo'l bilan paypaslab tekshirish usuli. | Palpation | Examination by touch using the hands.
Perkussiya (percussion) | A'zolarni chertib, hosil bo'ladigan tovush orqali tekshirish usuli. | Percussion | Examination method of tapping on the body surface and interpreting the resulting sound.
Auskultatsiya (auscultation) | A'zolar tovushlarini quloq yoki fonendoskop bilan tinglash usuli. | Auscultation | Listening to internal body sounds, usually with a stethoscope.
Inspeksiya (inspection) | Bemorni ko'zdan kechirish orqali tekshirish usuli. | Inspection | Examination by visual observation.
Krepitatsiya (crepitation) | O'pka auskultatsiyasida alveolalar ochilishi bilan bog'liq mayda quruq shitirlash tovushi. | Crepitations (crackles) | Fine popping sounds heard over the lungs when alveoli open.
Xirillash (wheezing) | Bronxlar torayganda eshitiladigan musiqiy, hushtaksimon nafas tovushi. | Wheeze | High-pitched musical whistling sound produced by narrowed airways.
Plevra ishqalanish shovqini (pleural friction rub) | Yallig'langan plevra varaqlari bir-biriga ishqalanganda eshitiladigan qirsillash. | Pleural friction rub | Creaking sound heard when inflamed pleural layers rub together.
Yurak shovqini (cardiac murmur) | Yurak bo'shliqlari yoki qopqoqlarida qon oqimi turbulentligidan paydo bo'ladigan qo'shimcha tovush. | Cardiac murmur | Extra sound caused by turbulent blood flow within the heart or across valves.
Galop ritmi (gallop rhythm) | Qo'shimcha III yoki IV yurak tovushi bilan taqillagan ot yugurishini eslatuvchi uch bo'g'inli ritm. | Gallop rhythm | Three-beat cardiac rhythm caused by an extra third or fourth heart sound.
Puls (pulse) | Arteriya devorining yurak qisqarishi bilan hamohang ritmik tebranishi. | Pulse | Rhythmic expansion of an artery wall with each heartbeat.
Arterial qon bosimi (blood pressure) | Qonning arteriya devoriga ko'rsatadigan bosimi; sistolik va diastolik qiymatlar bilan ifodalanadi. | Blood pressure | Pressure exerted by blood on arterial walls, expressed as systolic and diastolic values.
Nafas olish chastotasi (respiratory rate) | Bir daqiqadagi nafas soni; kattalarda me'yor taxminan 12-20. | Respiratory rate | Number of breaths per minute, normally about 12-20 in adults.
Tana harorati (body temperature) | Organizm issiqligining o'lchovi; me'yor taxminan 36,6 °C. | Body temperature | Measure of the body's heat; normal is approximately 36.6-37 °C.
Isitma (fever) | Tana haroratining 37,5 °C dan oshishi; infeksiya yoki yallig'lanishga javob. | Fever | Elevated body temperature, usually as a response to infection or inflammation.
Gipotermiya (hypothermia) | Tana haroratining 35 °C dan pastga tushishi. | Hypothermia | Core body temperature below 35 °C.
Taxipnoe (tachypnea) | Nafas chastotasining me'yordan oshishi (kattalarda daqiqasiga 20 dan ortiq). | Tachypnea | Abnormally rapid breathing, in adults above about 20 breaths per minute.
Bradikardiya (bradycardia) | Yurak urish chastotasining daqiqasiga 60 dan kamayishi. | Bradycardia | Heart rate below 60 beats per minute.
Taxikardiya (tachycardia) | Yurak urish chastotasining daqiqasiga 100 dan oshishi. | Tachycardia | Heart rate above 100 beats per minute.
Shish (edema) | To'qimalarda ortiqcha suyuqlik to'planishi. | Edema | Excess fluid accumulation in the tissues.
Anasarka (anasarca) | Butun tana bo'ylab tarqalgan keng shish. | Anasarca | Generalized, severe edema throughout the body.
Bo'yin vena kengayishi (jugular venous distension) | Bo'yin venalarining to'lib turishi; o'ng yurak bosimining oshganini ko'rsatadi. | Jugular venous distension | Visible fullness of the neck veins indicating raised right heart pressure.
Rangparlik (pallor) | Teri va shilliq pardalarning oqarishi; anemiyada uchraydi. | Pallor | Abnormal paleness of the skin and mucous membranes, as in anemia.
Akrosiyanoz (acrocyanosis) | Qo'l-oyoq uchlarining ko'karishi. | Acrocyanosis | Bluish discoloration of the hands and feet.
Barmoqlar "nog'ora tayoqchasi" ko'rinishi (digital clubbing) | Barmoq uchlarining kengayishi va tirnoqlarning "soat oynasi" shaklida bo'rtishi; surunkali o'pka va yurak kasalliklarida uchraydi. | Digital clubbing | Bulbous enlargement of the fingertips with convex nails, seen in chronic lung and heart disease.
Limfadenopatiya (lymphadenopathy) | Limfa tugunlarining kattalashishi; infeksiya, yallig'lanish yoki o'smalarda uchraydi. | Lymphadenopathy | Enlargement of lymph nodes, seen in infection, inflammation, or malignancy.
Rigidlik (rigidity) | Mushaklarning doimiy tarangligi va passiv harakatlarga qarshiligi. | Rigidity | Constant muscle stiffness resisting passive movement.
Spastiklik (spasticity) | Markaziy motoneyron zararlanishida mushak tonusining oshishi. | Spasticity | Velocity-dependent increase in muscle tone due to upper motor neuron lesion.
Refleks (reflex) | Retseptor qo'zg'alishiga markaziy asab tizimi orqali beixtiyor javob reaksiyasi. | Reflex | Involuntary response to a stimulus mediated by the nervous system.
Babinskiy refleksi (Babinski sign) | Tovon tashqi chetini chizganda bosh barmoqning yuqoriga ko'tarilishi; piramidal yo'l zararlanishi belgisi. | Babinski sign | Upward movement of the great toe on plantar stimulation, indicating a pyramidal tract lesion.
Meningeal belgilar (meningeal signs) | Ensa mushaklari qotishi, Kernig va Brudzinskiy belgilari; meningit belgisi. | Meningeal signs | Neck stiffness and Kernig and Brudzinski signs indicating meningeal irritation.
Umumiy qon tahlili (complete blood count) | Qonning hujayra tarkibi: eritrotsitlar, gemoglobin, leykotsitlar, trombotsitlar va EChT ni aniqlaydigan tekshiruv. | Complete blood count (CBC) | Test measuring blood cell components: red cells, hemoglobin, white cells, and platelets.
Eritrotsitlar cho'kish tezligi (ECHT) (erythrocyte sedimentation rate) | Eritrotsitlarning bir soatda cho'kish tezligi; yallig'lanish nospetsifik ko'rsatkichi. | Erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR) | Rate at which red cells settle in an hour, a nonspecific marker of inflammation.
C-reaktiv oqsil (C-reactive protein) | Yallig'lanish va to'qima shikastlanishida jigarda sintezlanadigan o'tkir faza oqsili. | C-reactive protein (CRP) | Acute-phase protein synthesized by the liver in response to inflammation.
Biokimyoviy qon tahlili (blood chemistry panel) | Qondagi glyukoza, kreatinin, bilirubin, fermentlar va elektrolitlarni aniqlaydigan tekshiruv. | Blood chemistry panel | Test measuring glucose, creatinine, bilirubin, enzymes, and electrolytes.
Glikozillangan gemoglobin (HbA1c) (glycated hemoglobin) | Oxirgi 2-3 oy ichidagi o'rtacha qon glyukozasini aks ettiruvchi ko'rsatkich; 6,5% va undan yuqori diabetni bildiradi. | Glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) | Marker of average blood glucose over the preceding 2-3 months; a value of 6.5% or higher indicates diabetes.
Kreatinin (creatinine) | Mushak almashinuvi mahsuloti; buyrak orqali chiqariladi, buyrak funksiyasi ko'rsatkichi. | Creatinine | Muscle metabolism by-product excreted by the kidneys and used to assess kidney function.
Koptokchalar filtratsiya tezligi (KFT) (glomerular filtration rate) | Buyrak koptokchalarida bir daqiqada filtrlanadigan plazma hajmi; buyrak funksiyasining asosiy ko'rsatkichi. | Glomerular filtration rate (GFR) | Volume of plasma filtered by the glomeruli per minute, the main measure of kidney function.
Bilirubin (bilirubin) | Gem parchalanishidan hosil bo'ladigan pigment; jigarda konyugatsiyalanib o't bilan chiqadi. | Bilirubin | Pigment produced by heme breakdown, conjugated in the liver and excreted in bile.
Transaminazalar (ALT, AST) (transaminases) | Jigar hujayralari zararlanganda qonda ko'payadigan fermentlar. | Transaminases (ALT, AST) | Liver enzymes that rise in the blood when hepatocytes are damaged.
Troponin (troponin) | Yurak mushagi shikastlanishida qonga chiqadigan oqsil; miokard infarktining asosiy biomarkeri. | Troponin | Cardiac regulatory protein released into the blood with myocardial injury, the key marker of myocardial infarction.
Elektrokardiogramma (EKG) (electrocardiogram) | Yurakning elektr faolligini qayd qiluvchi tekshiruv. | Electrocardiogram (ECG) | Recording of the heart's electrical activity.
Exokardiografiya (echocardiography) | Yurak tuzilishi va faoliyatini ultratovush yordamida baholash. | Echocardiography | Ultrasound assessment of cardiac structure and function.
Ultratovush tekshiruvi (ultrasonography) | Ultratovush to'lqinlari yordamida ichki a'zolar tasvirini olish. | Ultrasonography | Imaging of internal organs using ultrasound waves.
Kompyuter tomografiyasi (computed tomography) | Rentgen nurlari yordamida a'zolarning qatlamli tasvirini olish usuli. | Computed tomography (CT) | Cross-sectional imaging using X-rays.
Magnit-rezonans tomografiya (magnetic resonance imaging) | Magnit maydon va radio to'lqinlar yordamida yumshoq to'qimalarning batafsil tasvirini olish usuli. | Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) | Detailed imaging of soft tissues using magnetic fields and radio waves.
Rentgenografiya (radiography) | Rentgen nurlari yordamida a'zolarning tasvirini olish. | Radiography | Imaging of internal structures using X-rays.
Endoskopiya (endoscopy) | Ichi kovak a'zolarni maxsus optik asbob yordamida ko'rib tekshirish. | Endoscopy | Visual examination of hollow organs with an optical instrument.
Biopsiya (biopsy) | To'qima namunasini olib, mikroskopda tekshirish. | Biopsy | Removal of a tissue sample for microscopic examination.
Spirometriya (spirometry) | O'pka hajmlari va havo oqimi tezligini o'lchaydigan tekshiruv. | Spirometry | Test measuring lung volumes and airflow rates.
Pulsoksimetriya (pulse oximetry) | Qon kislorod to'yinganligini (SpO2) invaziv bo'lmagan usulda o'lchash. | Pulse oximetry | Noninvasive measurement of arterial oxygen saturation (SpO2).
Lumbal punksiya (lumbar puncture) | Orqa miya suyuqligini olish uchun bel sohasidan igna kiritish. | Lumbar puncture | Insertion of a needle in the lower back to obtain cerebrospinal fluid.
Dializ (dialysis) | Buyrak yetishmovchiligida qonni zaharli moddalardan sun'iy tozalash usuli. | Dialysis | Artificial blood purification used in kidney failure.
Transplantatsiya (transplantation) | A'zo yoki to'qimani donordan qabul qiluvchiga ko'chirib o'tkazish. | Transplantation | Transfer of an organ or tissue from a donor to a recipient.
Vaksina (vaccine) | Maxsus immunitet hosil qilish uchun yuboriladigan zaiflashtirilgan yoki o'ldirilgan mikrob yoxud uning antigeni. | Vaccine | Preparation of an attenuated or inactivated organism or its antigen given to induce specific immunity.
Profilaktika (prevention) | Kasallikning oldini olishga qaratilgan tadbirlar majmuasi. | Prevention | Measures aimed at preventing disease.
Reabilitatsiya (rehabilitation) | Kasallikdan keyin buzilgan funksiyalarni tiklashga qaratilgan chora-tadbirlar. | Rehabilitation | Measures to restore function impaired by illness or injury.
