## Farmakologiya | Pharmacology
Farmakokinetika (pharmacokinetics) | Dori moddasining organizmda so'rilishi, taqsimlanishi, metabolizmi va chiqarilishi (ADME). | Pharmacokinetics | What the body does to a drug: absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME).
Farmakodinamika (pharmacodynamics) | Dorining organizmga ta'siri, uning mexanizmi va effektlari. | Pharmacodynamics | What a drug does to the body: its mechanisms and effects.
Biokiraolishlik (bioavailability) | Qabul qilingan dori dozasining tizimli qon aylanishiga yetib boradigan qismi. | Bioavailability | Fraction of an administered dose that reaches the systemic circulation unchanged.
Yarim chiqarilish davri (half-life) | Dori plazmadagi miqdori ikki barobar kamayishi uchun ketadigan vaqt. | Half-life | Time required for the plasma concentration of a drug to fall by half.
Birinchi o'tish effekti (first-pass effect) | Og'iz orqali qabul qilingan dorining jigarda umumiy qonga o'tishdan oldin metabolizmga uchrashi. | First-pass effect | Metabolism of an oral drug in the liver before it reaches systemic circulation.
Terapevtik indeks (therapeutic index) | Toksik doza va samarali doza nisbati; dorining xavfsizlik ko'rsatkichi. | Therapeutic index | Ratio of toxic dose to effective dose, a measure of drug safety.
Agonist (agonist) | Retseptorni qo'zg'atib, biologik javob chaqiruvchi modda. | Agonist | Substance that binds a receptor and activates it to produce a response.
Antagonist (antagonist) | Retseptorga bog'lanib, agonist ta'sirini to'sadigan modda. | Antagonist | Substance that binds a receptor and blocks the action of an agonist.
Nojo'ya ta'sir (adverse effect) | Dorining davolash maqsadidan tashqari, istalmagan ta'siri. | Adverse effect | Unwanted effect of a drug beyond its therapeutic purpose.
Dori o'zaro ta'siri (drug interaction) | Bir vaqtda qabul qilingan dorilarning bir-birining ta'sirini o'zgartirishi. | Drug interaction | Change in the effect of one drug caused by another taken at the same time.
Tolerantlik (tolerance) | Dori uzoq qabul qilinganda uning ta'sirining kamayishi. | Tolerance | Reduced response to a drug after repeated administration.
Retsept (prescription) | Shifokorning dori-darmon buyurishi haqidagi yozma ko'rsatmasi. | Prescription | Written order by a clinician for a medication.
Antibiotiklar (antibiotics) | Bakteriyalarni o'ldiruvchi yoki o'sishini to'xtatuvchi dorilar. | Antibiotics | Drugs that kill bacteria or inhibit their growth.
Penitsillinlar (penicillins) | Bakteriya hujayra devori sintezini buzuvchi beta-laktam antibiotiklar. | Penicillins | Beta-lactam antibiotics that inhibit bacterial cell wall synthesis.
Amoksitsillin (amoxicillin) | Keng spektrli aminopenitsillin guruhidagi antibiotik. | Amoxicillin | Broad-spectrum aminopenicillin antibiotic.
Tsefalosporinlar (cephalosporins) | Beta-laktam guruhiga mansub, avlodlarga bo'linadigan antibiotiklar. | Cephalosporins | Beta-lactam antibiotics divided into generations.
Seftriakson (ceftriaxone) | Uchinchi avlod tsefalosporin, og'ir infeksiyalarda parenteral qo'llaniladi. | Ceftriaxone | Third-generation parenteral cephalosporin used for serious infections.
Karbapenemlar (carbapenems) | Juda keng spektrli zaxira beta-laktam antibiotiklar (masalan, meropenem). | Carbapenems | Very broad-spectrum reserve beta-lactam antibiotics, such as meropenem.
Makrolidlar (macrolides) | Bakteriya ribosomasi 50S bo'lagiga ta'sir qiluvchi antibiotiklar (azitromitsin, klaritromitsin). | Macrolides | Antibiotics acting on the 50S ribosomal subunit, such as azithromycin and clarithromycin.
Ftorxinolonlar (fluoroquinolones) | DNK-giraza va topoizomerazani to'suvchi antibiotiklar (tsiprofloksatsin, levofloksatsin). | Fluoroquinolones | Antibiotics inhibiting DNA gyrase and topoisomerase, such as ciprofloxacin and levofloxacin.
Aminoglikozidlar (aminoglycosides) | Bakteriya oqsil sintezini buzuvchi, oto- va nefrotoksik antibiotiklar (gentamitsin). | Aminoglycosides | Protein synthesis inhibitors, such as gentamicin, with ototoxic and nephrotoxic potential.
Tetratsiklinlar (tetracyclines) | Bakteriya oqsil sintezini 30S bo'lakda to'suvchi antibiotiklar (doksitsiklin). | Tetracyclines | Antibiotics inhibiting protein synthesis at the 30S subunit, such as doxycycline.
Vankomitsin (vancomycin) | Metitsillinga chidamli stafilokokklarga qarshi ishlatiladigan glikopeptid antibiotik. | Vancomycin | Glycopeptide antibiotic used against methicillin-resistant staphylococci.
Metronidazol (metronidazole) | Anaerob bakteriyalar va ba'zi sodda hayvonlarga qarshi dori. | Metronidazole | Drug active against anaerobic bacteria and some protozoa.
Antibiotiklarga chidamlilik (antibiotic resistance) | Mikroblarning antibiotiklar ta'siriga qarshilik qobiliyatini orttirishi. | Antibiotic resistance | Acquired ability of microbes to withstand the effects of antibiotics.
Antivirus dorilar (antiviral drugs) | Viruslar ko'payishini to'xtatuvchi dorilar. | Antiviral drugs | Drugs that inhibit viral replication.
Asiklovir (acyclovir) | Gerpes viruslariga qarshi nukleozid analogi. | Acyclovir | Nucleoside analog active against herpes viruses.
Zamburug'larga qarshi dorilar (antifungal drugs) | Zamburug'li infeksiyalarni davolaydigan preparatlar (flukonazol, amfotersin B). | Antifungal drugs | Agents used for fungal infections, such as fluconazole and amphotericin B.
Silga qarshi dorilar (antituberculosis drugs) | Sil davolashda ishlatiladigan izoniazid, rifampitsin, pirazinamid, etambutol. | Antituberculosis drugs | Drugs for tuberculosis including isoniazid, rifampin, pyrazinamide, and ethambutol.
Antimalyariya dorilari (antimalarial drugs) | Bezgakni davolash va oldini olish uchun dorilar (xlorokin, artemizinin). | Antimalarial drugs | Drugs for treating and preventing malaria, such as chloroquine and artemisinin derivatives.
NSAID (nosteroid yallig'lanishga qarshi dorilar) | Siklooksigenazani to'suvchi, og'riq, isitma va yallig'lanishni kamaytiruvchi dorilar. | Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) | Cyclooxygenase inhibitors that relieve pain, fever, and inflammation.
Ibuprofen (ibuprofen) | Og'riq va isitmani kamaytiruvchi nosteroid yallig'lanishga qarshi dori. | Ibuprofen | NSAID used for pain, fever, and inflammation.
Atsetilsalitsil kislotasi (aspirin) | Siklooksigenazani qaytmas to'sib, trombotsitlar agregatsiyasini kamaytiruvchi NSAID. | Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) | NSAID that irreversibly inhibits cyclooxygenase and reduces platelet aggregation.
Paratsetamol (paracetamol) | Og'riq va isitmaga qarshi dori; yallig'lanishga deyarli ta'sir qilmaydi, ortiqcha dozada jigarni zararlaydi. | Paracetamol (acetaminophen) | Analgesic and antipyretic with little anti-inflammatory effect, hepatotoxic in overdose.
Opioid analgetiklar (opioid analgesics) | Opioid retseptorlariga ta'sir qiluvchi kuchli og'riq qoldiruvchi dorilar (morfin, fentanil). | Opioid analgesics | Strong analgesics acting on opioid receptors, such as morphine and fentanyl.
Naloksan (naloxone) | Opioid retseptorlari antagonisti, opioid dozasi oshib ketganda antidot. | Naloxone | Opioid receptor antagonist used as an antidote for opioid overdose.
Glyukokortikosteroidlar (glucocorticoids) | Yallig'lanishni va immunitetni susaytiruvchi steroid dorilar (prednizolon, deksametazon). | Glucocorticoids | Steroid drugs that suppress inflammation and immunity, such as prednisolone and dexamethasone.
Antigistamin dorilar (antihistamines) | Gistamin retseptorlarini to'suvchi allergiyaga qarshi dorilar. | Antihistamines | Drugs that block histamine receptors, used in allergies.
Beta-agonistlar (beta-agonists) | Beta-adrenoretseptorlarni qo'zg'atuvchi dorilar; salbutamol bronxlarni kengaytiradi. | Beta-agonists | Drugs stimulating beta-adrenergic receptors; salbutamol dilates the bronchi.
Salbutamol (salbutamol, albuterol) | Qisqa ta'sirli beta-2 agonist, astma xurujini bartaraf etadi. | Albuterol (salbutamol) | Short-acting beta-2 agonist used to relieve asthma attacks.
Beta-blokatorlar (beta-blockers) | Beta-adrenoretseptorlarni to'suvchi, yurak urishi va qon bosimini pasaytiruvchi dorilar (metoprolol, bisoprolol). | Beta-blockers | Drugs blocking beta-adrenergic receptors to lower heart rate and blood pressure, such as metoprolol and bisoprolol.
AO'F ingibitorlari (ACE inhibitors) | Angiotenzinni o'zgartiruvchi fermentni to'suvchi dorilar (enalapril, lizinopril); qon bosimini pasaytiradi. | ACE inhibitors | Drugs inhibiting angiotensin-converting enzyme to lower blood pressure, such as enalapril and lisinopril.
Angiotenzin retseptorlari blokatorlari (angiotensin receptor blockers) | AT1 retseptorlarini to'suvchi gipertenziyaga qarshi dorilar (losartan, valsartan). | Angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs) | Antihypertensives blocking AT1 receptors, such as losartan and valsartan.
Kaltsiy kanallari blokatorlari (calcium channel blockers) | Kaltsiy kanallarini to'suvchi dorilar (amlodipin, verapamil). | Calcium channel blockers | Drugs blocking calcium channels, such as amlodipine and verapamil.
Siydik haydovchi dorilar (diuretics) | Buyrak orqali suv va tuz chiqarilishini oshiruvchi dorilar. | Diuretics | Drugs that increase renal excretion of water and salt.
Furosemid (furosemide) | Genle halqasida ta'sir qiluvchi kuchli "halqa" diuretigi. | Furosemide | Potent loop diuretic acting on the loop of Henle.
Spironolakton (spironolactone) | Aldosteron antagonisti, kaliy saqlovchi siydik haydovchi dori. | Spironolactone | Aldosterone antagonist and potassium-sparing diuretic.
Gidroxlorotiazid (hydrochlorothiazide) | Distal naychalarda ta'sir qiluvchi tiazid diuretik. | Hydrochlorothiazide | Thiazide diuretic acting on the distal tubule.
Nitrogliserin (nitroglycerin) | Tomirlarni kengaytiruvchi nitrat; stenokardiya xurujini tezda bartaraf etadi. | Nitroglycerin | Vasodilating nitrate that rapidly relieves angina attacks.
Statinlar (statins) | HMG-KoA-reduktazani to'suvchi, xolesterinni pasaytiruvchi dorilar (atorvastatin). | Statins | HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors that lower cholesterol, such as atorvastatin.
Antikoagulyantlar (anticoagulants) | Qon ivishini susaytiruvchi dorilar. | Anticoagulants | Drugs that reduce blood clotting.
Geparin (heparin) | Antitrombin III ni faollashtiruvchi tezkor antikoagulyant. | Heparin | Fast-acting anticoagulant that activates antithrombin III.
Varfarin (warfarin) | K vitaminiga bog'liq ivish omillarini kamaytiruvchi og'iz orqali olinadigan antikoagulyant. | Warfarin | Oral anticoagulant that reduces vitamin K-dependent clotting factors.
Antiagregantlar (antiplatelet drugs) | Trombotsitlar yopishishini kamaytiruvchi dorilar (aspirin, klopidogrel). | Antiplatelet drugs | Drugs that reduce platelet aggregation, such as aspirin and clopidogrel.
Trombolitiklar (thrombolytics) | Qon laxtasini eritadigan dorilar (alteplaza). | Thrombolytics | Drugs that dissolve blood clots, such as alteplase.
Digoksin (digoxin) | Yurak glikozidi; yurak qisqarishini kuchaytiradi va AV o'tkazuvchanlikni sekinlashtiradi. | Digoxin | Cardiac glycoside that strengthens contraction and slows AV conduction.
Amiodaron (amiodarone) | Keng ta'sirli antiaritmik dori. | Amiodarone | Broad-spectrum antiarrhythmic drug.
Insulin preparatlari (insulin preparations) | Qandli diabetni davolashda qo'llaniladigan qisqa, o'rta va uzoq ta'sirli insulinlar. | Insulin preparations | Short-, intermediate-, and long-acting insulins used to treat diabetes.
Metformin (metformin) | 2-tip diabetda birinchi tanlov dori; jigarda glyukoza ishlab chiqarilishini kamaytiradi va insulin sezuvchanligini oshiradi. | Metformin | First-line drug for type 2 diabetes that reduces hepatic glucose production and improves insulin sensitivity.
Sulfonilmochevina preparatlari (sulfonylureas) | Oshqozon osti bezidan insulin ajralishini kuchaytiruvchi dorilar (glimepirid). | Sulfonylureas | Drugs stimulating pancreatic insulin secretion, such as glimepiride.
Levotiroksin (levothyroxine) | Gipotireozda qo'llaniladigan sintetik tiroksin. | Levothyroxine | Synthetic thyroxine used in hypothyroidism.
Proton pompasi ingibitorlari (proton pump inhibitors) | Oshqozon kislotasi ishlab chiqarilishini kuchli kamaytiruvchi dorilar (omeprazol). | Proton pump inhibitors | Drugs that strongly reduce gastric acid secretion, such as omeprazole.
Antatsidlar (antacids) | Oshqozon kislotasini neytrallovchi dorilar. | Antacids | Drugs that neutralize gastric acid.
Laksativlar (laxatives) | Ichni yumshatuvchi yoki surgi dorilar. | Laxatives | Drugs that promote bowel evacuation.
Antiemetiklar (antiemetics) | Ko'ngil aynishi va qusishni kamaytiruvchi dorilar (ondansetron, metoklopramid). | Antiemetics | Drugs that relieve nausea and vomiting, such as ondansetron and metoclopramide.
Yo'talga qarshi dorilar (antitussives) | Yo'tal refleksini bostiruvchi dorilar. | Antitussives | Drugs that suppress the cough reflex.
Balg'am ko'chiruvchi dorilar (expectorants) | Balg'amni suyultirib, ajralishini yaxshilovchi dorilar. | Expectorants | Drugs that thin sputum and aid its clearance.
Antidepressantlar (antidepressants) | Depressiyani davolovchi dorilar (sertralin, amitriptilin). | Antidepressants | Drugs for depression, such as sertraline and amitriptyline.
Antipsixotiklar (antipsychotics) | Psixoz va shizofreniyani davolovchi dorilar (galoperidol, risperidon). | Antipsychotics | Drugs for psychosis and schizophrenia, such as haloperidol and risperidone.
Benzodiazepinlar (benzodiazepines) | GABA-A retseptorlari orqali tinchlantiruvchi va talvasaga qarshi ta'sir qiluvchi dorilar (diazepam). | Benzodiazepines | Drugs acting via GABA-A receptors with sedative and anticonvulsant effects, such as diazepam.
Antikonvulsantlar (anticonvulsants) | Tutqanoq xurujlarini oldini oluvchi dorilar (karbamazepin, valproat). | Anticonvulsants | Drugs preventing seizures, such as carbamazepine and valproate.
Levodopa (levodopa) | Parkinson kasalligida dofamin tanqisligini to'ldiruvchi dori. | Levodopa | Dopamine precursor used in Parkinson disease.
Umumiy behushlik vositalari (general anesthetics) | Ongni va og'riq sezgisini vaqtincha o'chiruvchi dorilar. | General anesthetics | Drugs that cause reversible loss of consciousness and pain sensation.
Mahalliy behushlik vositalari (local anesthetics) | Muayyan sohada og'riq sezgisini o'chiruvchi dorilar (lidokain). | Local anesthetics | Drugs that block pain sensation in a limited area, such as lidocaine.
Atropin (atropine) | Muskarin retseptorlarini to'suvchi dori; yurak urishini tezlashtiradi, bezlar sekretsiyasini kamaytiradi. | Atropine | Muscarinic receptor blocker that increases heart rate and reduces glandular secretion.
Kontratseptivlar (contraceptives) | Homiladorlikning oldini oluvchi vositalar va dorilar. | Contraceptives | Agents and drugs used to prevent pregnancy.
Vitaminlar (vitamins) | Organizm uchun oz miqdorda zarur, ko'pincha tashqaridan olinadigan organik moddalar. | Vitamins | Organic compounds needed in small amounts, usually obtained from food.
Antidot (antidote) | Zaharlanishda zaharning ta'sirini bartaraf etuvchi modda. | Antidote | Agent that counteracts the effects of a poison.
Zaharlanish (poisoning) | Zaharli modda ta'sirida organizm funksiyalarining buzilishi. | Poisoning (intoxication) | Impairment of body functions due to a toxic substance.
