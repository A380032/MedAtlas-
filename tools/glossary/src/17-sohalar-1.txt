Golji apparati (Golgi apparatus) | Moddalarni o'zgartirib, hujayradan chiqarishga tayyorlovchi organella. | Golgi apparatus | Organelle that modifies and packages substances for secretion.
Lizosoma (lysosome) | Hazm fermentlariga ega, hujayra ichi parchalanishini ta'minlovchi organella. | Lysosome | Organelle with digestive enzymes for intracellular breakdown.
Hujayra pardasi (cell membrane) | Hujayrani o'rab, moddalar o'tishini boshqaruvchi lipid-oqsil parda. | Cell membrane | Lipid-protein membrane regulating passage into the cell.
Differensiatsiya (differentiation) | Hujayralarning ixtisoslashib, maxsus funksiya olishi. | Differentiation | Specialization of cells for specific functions.
O'zak hujayra (stem cell) | Cheksiz bo'linish va ixtisoslashgan hujayralarga aylanish qobiliyatiga ega hujayra. | Stem cell | Cell able to divide indefinitely and differentiate.
Epiteliy to'qimasi (epithelial tissue) | Yuza va bo'shliqlarni qoplovchi, bezlar hosil qiluvchi to'qima. | Epithelial tissue | Tissue lining surfaces and cavities and forming glands.
Biriktiruvchi to'qima (connective tissue) | Tayanch, trofik va himoya vazifali, hujayra oralig'i moddasi ko'p to'qima. | Connective tissue | Supportive tissue with abundant extracellular matrix.
Tog'ay to'qimasi (cartilage) | Elastik tayanch to'qima, qon tomirsiz. | Cartilage | Elastic supportive tissue without blood vessels.
Suyak to'qimasi (bone tissue) | Minerallashgan zich tayanch to'qima. | Bone tissue | Mineralized dense supportive tissue.
Nerv to'qimasi (nervous tissue) | Neyronlar va neyrogliyadan iborat, qo'zg'alishni o'tkazuvchi to'qima. | Nervous tissue | Tissue of neurons and glia that conducts excitation.
Akson (axon) | Neyron impulsini boshqa hujayraga uzatuvchi uzun o'simta. | Axon | Long process carrying neuron impulses to other cells.
Mielin (myelin) | Nerv tolasini o'rab tezlikni oshiruvchi yog'simon qobiq. | Myelin | Fatty sheath around nerve fibers increasing conduction speed.
Silliq mushak to'qimasi (smooth muscle) | Ichki a'zolar devoridagi, ixtiyorsiz qisqaruvchi mushak. | Smooth muscle | Involuntary muscle in the walls of internal organs.
Ko'ndalang-targ'il mushak (striated muscle) | Skelet mushaklari, ixtiyoriy qisqaradigan mushak turi. | Striated muscle | Skeletal muscle type under voluntary control.
Sarkomer (sarcomere) | Mushak tolasining qisqaruvchi tuzilmaviy birligi. | Sarcomere | The contractile structural unit of muscle fiber.
Fibroblast (fibroblast) | Biriktiruvchi to'qima moddasini ishlab chiqaruvchi hujayra. | Fibroblast | Cell producing connective tissue matrix.
Kollagen (collagen) | Biriktiruvchi to'qimaning asosiy mustahkam oqsili. | Collagen | Main strength-giving protein of connective tissue.
Plazmatsit (plasma cell) | Antitana ishlab chiqaruvchi B-limfotsitlardan hosil bo'lgan hujayra. | Plasma cell | Antibody-producing cell derived from B lymphocytes.
Bazofil (basophil) | Gistamin ajratuvchi, allergik reaksiyalarda ishtirok etuvchi leykotsit. | Basophil | Histamine-releasing leukocyte in allergic reactions.
Retikulotsit (reticulocyte) | Yosh eritrotsit, ko'mikdan yangi chiqqan. | Reticulocyte | Immature red cell recently released from bone marrow.
Qizil ko'mik (red bone marrow) | Qon hujayralari hosil bo'ladigan suyak ichidagi to'qima. | Red bone marrow | Bone tissue where blood cells are formed.
Sinovial parda (synovial membrane) | Bo'g'im ichini qoplab sinovial suyuqlik ishlab chiqaradigan parda. | Synovial membrane | Joint lining that secretes synovial fluid.
Ichak vorsinkalari (intestinal villi) | Ingichka ichak shilliq qavatidagi so'rilish yuzasini oshiruvchi o'simtalar. | Intestinal villi | Mucosal projections increasing absorptive surface area.
Gepatotsit (hepatocyte) | Jigarning asosiy ishchi hujayrasi. | Hepatocyte | The main functional cell of the liver.
## Psixiatriya | Psychiatry
Panika xuruji (panic attack) | To'satdan paydo bo'ladigan kuchli qo'rquv, yurak urishi va nafas qisilishi. | Panic attack | Sudden intense fear with palpitations and shortness of breath.
Obsessiv-kompulsiv buzilish (obsessive-compulsive disorder) | Takrorlanuvchi bezovta qiluvchi fikrlar va ularni bartaraf etuvchi majburiy harakatlar. | Obsessive-compulsive disorder | Recurrent intrusive thoughts and compulsive acts to relieve them.
Posttravmatik stress buzilishi (post-traumatic stress disorder) | Og'ir shokdan keyin takroriy xotiralar va qo'rquv bilan kechuvchi holat. | Post-traumatic stress disorder | Condition of recurrent memories and fear after severe trauma.
Anoreksiya (anorexia nervosa) | Vazn ortishidan qo'rqish bilan ovqatni keskin cheklash. | Anorexia nervosa | Severe food restriction driven by fear of weight gain.
Bulimiya (bulimia) | Ortiqcha yeb, so'ng qusish yoki boshqa usullar bilan tozalanish. | Bulimia | Binge eating followed by purging.
Altsgeymer kasalligi (Alzheimer disease) | Demensiyaning eng ko'p sababi, miya hujayralarining progressiv yo'qolishi. | Alzheimer disease | The most common cause of dementia with progressive neuronal loss.
Psixoz (psychosis) | Voqelikni idrok qilishning keskin buzilishi: gallyutsinatsiya va bema'ni fikrlar. | Psychosis | Marked loss of contact with reality with hallucinations and delusions.
Paranoyya (paranoia) | Shubhali, ta'qib qilinish haqidagi turg'un g'oyalar. | Paranoia | Persistent suspicious ideas of persecution.
Katatoniya (catatonia) | Harakat sustligi yoki qotib qolish, mutizm bilan kechadigan sindrom. | Catatonia | Syndrome of motor immobility or stupor with mutism.
Autizm (autism) | Muloqot va ijtimoiy o'zaro aloqa qiyinchiligi, takrorlanuvchi xulq bilan. | Autism spectrum disorder | Difficulty in communication and social interaction with repetitive behavior.
Dikkat tanqisligi (attention deficit hyperactivity disorder) | E'tibor jamlay olmaslik, giperfaollik va shoshqaloqlik bilan kechadigan buzilish. | ADHD | Disorder with inattention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity.
Narkomaniya (drug addiction) | Giyohvand moddalarga qaramlik. | Drug addiction | Dependence on narcotic substances.
Psixoterapiya (psychotherapy) | Ruhiy kasallik va buzilishlarni so'z orqali davolash. | Psychotherapy | Treatment of mental disorders through verbal methods.
Psixofarmakologiya (psychopharmacology) | Ruhiyatga ta'sir qiluvchi dori vositalar haqidagi fan. | Psychopharmacology | Science of drugs affecting the mind.
Trankvilizatorlar (tranquilizers) | Xavotirni kamaytiruvchi, tinchlantiruvchi dorilar. | Tranquilizers | Calming drugs reducing anxiety.
Uyqu apnoesi (sleep apnea) | Uyquda nafasning takroriy to'xtashi. | Sleep apnea | Repeated pauses in breathing during sleep.
Narkolepsiya (narcolepsy) | Kunduzi to'satdan uxlab qolish xurujlari. | Narcolepsy | Sudden attacks of sleep during the day.
Somnambulizm (sleepwalking) | Uyqu paytida beixtiyor yurish. | Sleepwalking | Walking during sleep without awareness.
Depersonalizatsiya (depersonalization) | O'z shaxsiga yoki tanasiga begonalik hissi. | Depersonalization | Feeling of detachment from one's self or body.
Derealizatsiya (derealization) | Atrofdagi dunyoning noreal tuyulishi. | Derealization | Feeling that the surroundings are unreal.
Konversiya (conversion disorder) | Ruhiy to'qnashuvning jismoniy, masalan nevrologik belgilar shaklida namoyon bo'lishi. | Conversion disorder | Psychological conflict expressed as physical, e.g., neurological, symptoms.
Neyrasteniya (neurasthenia) | Tez charchash, ta'sirchanlik va uyqu buzilishi bilan kechadigan holat. | Neurasthenia | Condition of fatigability, irritability, and sleep disturbance.
Psixopatiya (psychopathy) | Shaxs xarakterining barqaror buzilishi va ijtimoiy moslashuv qiyinligi. | Psychopathy | Stable character disorder with social maladjustment.
Agorafobiya (agoraphobia) | Ochiq yoki gavjum joylardan qo'rqish. | Agoraphobia | Fear of open or crowded places.
Klaustrofobiya (claustrophobia) | Yopiq joylardan qo'rqish. | Claustrophobia | Fear of enclosed spaces.
Ijtimoiy fobiya (social phobia) | Odamlar oldida baholanishdan qo'rqish. | Social phobia | Fear of being judged in front of others.
Gipoxondrik buzilish (hypochondriasis) | Jiddiy kasallik borligiga asossiz ishonch. | Hypochondriasis | Unfounded conviction of having a serious illness.
## Toksikologiya va favqulodda holatlar | Toxicology and emergencies
Zahar (poison) | Organizmga kirib sog'liqqa zarar yetkazuvchi modda. | Poison | A substance that harms health on entering the body.
Oshqozonni yuvish (gastric lavage) | Zaharlanishda oshqozonni zond orqali yuvish. | Gastric lavage | Washing out the stomach via a tube in poisoning.
Faollangan ko'mir (activated charcoal) | Zaharlarni ichakda adsorbsiya qiluvchi sorbent. | Activated charcoal | Sorbent adsorbing poisons in the gut.
Spirt bilan zaharlanish (alcohol poisoning) | Etanolning ortiqcha miqdori tufayli markaziy nerv tizimining bosilishi. | Alcohol poisoning | CNS depression from excess ethanol.
Metanol bilan zaharlanish (methanol poisoning) | Metil spirti tufayli ko'rishning yo'qolishi va atsidoz. | Methanol poisoning | Vision loss and acidosis from methyl alcohol.
Qo'rg'oshin bilan zaharlanish (lead poisoning) | Qo'rg'oshin to'planishidan nerv, qon va buyrak zararlanishi. | Lead poisoning | Nerve, blood, and kidney damage from lead accumulation.
Simob bilan zaharlanish (mercury poisoning) | Simob birikmalaridan nerv va buyrak zararlanishi. | Mercury poisoning | Nerve and kidney damage from mercury compounds.
Fosfororganik zaharlanish (organophosphate poisoning) | Xolinesterazani bloklovchi pestitsidlar bilan zaharlanish. | Organophosphate poisoning | Poisoning by cholinesterase-blocking pesticides.
Kvinke shishi (angioedema) | Teri va shilliq qavatning to'satdan chuqur shishishi. | Angioedema | Sudden deep swelling of skin and mucosa.
Qichima (urticaria) | Terida qichishuvchi, tez o'tuvchi pufakchali toshma. | Urticaria | Itchy transient wheals on the skin.
Klinik o'lim (clinical death) | Nafas va yurak faoliyatining to'xtashi, qaytarish mumkin bo'lgan qisqa davr. | Clinical death | Brief reversible period of cardiac and respiratory arrest.
Sun'iy nafas oldirish (artificial ventilation) | Nafas yo'llariga havo yuborib, nafasni majburan ta'minlash. | Artificial ventilation | Forced delivery of air to the lungs.
Glazgo koma shkalasi (Glasgow coma scale) | Ong holatini ko'z, nutq va harakat javobi bo'yicha baholash shkalasi. | Glasgow Coma Scale | Scale rating consciousness by eye, verbal, and motor response.
Elektr jarohati (electrical injury) | Elektr toki ta'sirida to'qima va yurak ritmi shikastlanishi. | Electrical injury | Tissue and cardiac rhythm damage from electric current.
Hasharot chaqishi (insect sting) | Ari yoki boshqa hasharot chaqishidan mahalliy yoki allergik reaksiya. | Insect sting | Local or allergic reaction to a bee or other insect sting.

Relaps (relapse) | Remissiyadan keyin kasallikning qaytalanishi. | Relapse | Return of disease after remission.

## Epidemiologiya va profilaktika | Epidemiology and prevention
Epidemik jarayon (epidemic process) | Qo'zg'atuvchi, yuqish yo'li va moyil aholidan iborat infeksiya tarqalish jarayoni. | Epidemic process | Spread of infection via source, transmission route, and susceptible population.
Endemiya (endemic) | Muayyan hududda doimiy mavjud kasallik. | Endemic | Disease constantly present in a certain area.
Sporadik kasallanish (sporadic cases) | Alohida, o'zaro bog'liq bo'lmagan kasallik holatlari. | Sporadic cases | Isolated unrelated cases of disease.
Epidemik o'choq (epidemic focus) | Infeksiya manbai joylashgan va tarqalish xavfi bor joy. | Epidemic focus | Place with an infection source and risk of spread.
Infeksiya manbai (source of infection) | Qo'zg'atuvchi tarqaladigan bemor, tashuvchi yoki hayvon. | Source of infection | Patient, carrier, or animal from which the pathogen spreads.
Bakteriya tashuvchi (carrier) | Kasallik belgilari yo'q, ammo qo'zg'atuvchini ajratib turuvchi shaxs. | Carrier | Person shedding a pathogen without symptoms.
Yuqish yo'li (route of transmission) | Qo'zg'atuvchining manbadan sog'lom odamga o'tish yo'li. | Transmission route | Path by which a pathogen passes from source to a healthy person.
Fekal-oral yo'l (fecal-oral route) | Najas bilan ifloslangan suv, ovqat orqali yuqish. | Fecal-oral route | Spread via food or water contaminated with feces.
Havo-tomchi yo'li (airborne droplet route) | Yo'talganda va aksirganda havo orqali yuqish. | Droplet route | Spread through air by coughing and sneezing.
Transmissiv yo'l (vector-borne route) | Qon so'ruvchi hasharotlar orqali yuqish. | Vector-borne route | Transmission via blood-sucking insects.
Vertikal yuqish (vertical transmission) | Onadan bolaga homiladorlik, tug'ruq yoki emizish davrida yuqishi. | Vertical transmission | Passage from mother to child during pregnancy, birth, or breastfeeding.
Emlash (vaccination) | Immunitet hosil qilish uchun vaksina yuborish. | Vaccination | Administering a vaccine to induce immunity.
BCG (BCG) | Silga qarshi tug'ruqxonada yuboriladigan vaksina. | BCG | Anti-tuberculosis vaccine given in the maternity ward.
Emlash taqvimi (immunization schedule) | Emlashlarning belgilangan muddat va ketma-ketligi. | Immunization schedule | The set timing and sequence of vaccinations.
Kollektiv immunitet (herd immunity) | Aholining katta qismi immun bo'lganda kasallik tarqalishining to'xtashi. | Herd immunity | Halting of spread when most of a population is immune.
Karantin (quarantine) | Xavfli infeksiya tarqalishining oldini olish uchun cheklov rejimi. | Quarantine | Restriction regime to prevent spread of dangerous infections.
Skrining (screening) | Kasallikni dastlabki belgilarsiz bosqichda aniqlash uchun ommaviy tekshiruv. | Screening | Mass testing to detect disease at an asymptomatic stage.
Dispanserizatsiya (health surveillance) | Aholini muntazam tibbiy kuzatuv ostida tekshirish va davolash. | Dispensary follow-up | Regular medical surveillance and treatment of the population.
Birlamchi profilaktika (primary prevention) | Kasallik paydo bo'lishining oldini olish. | Primary prevention | Preventing the onset of disease.
Ikkilamchi profilaktika (secondary prevention) | Kasallikni erta aniqlash va asoratlarni oldini olish. | Secondary prevention | Early detection and prevention of complications.
O'lim ko'rsatkichi (mortality rate) | Aholining ma'lum davrdagi o'lim darajasi. | Mortality rate | Death rate in a population over a period.
Kasallanish ko'rsatkichi (incidence) | Ma'lum davrda yangi kasallik holatlari soni. | Incidence | Number of new disease cases in a period.
Tarqalganlik (prevalence) | Aholida ma'lum paytda mavjud kasallik holatlari ulushi. | Prevalence | Proportion of existing disease cases in a population at a time.
Xavf omili (risk factor) | Kasallik ehtimolini oshiruvchi omil. | Risk factor | Factor that raises the likelihood of disease.
Dalillarga asoslangan tibbiyot (evidence-based medicine) | Qarorlarni ishonchli tadqiqot dalillariga tayanib qabul qilish. | Evidence-based medicine | Making decisions based on reliable research evidence.
