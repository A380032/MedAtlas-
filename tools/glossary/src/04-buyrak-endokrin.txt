## Buyrak va siydik yo'llari kasalliklari | Kidney and urinary diseases
Glomerulonefrit (glomerulonephritis) | Buyrak koptokchalarining (glomerulalarning) yallig'lanishi, ko'pincha immun mexanizmli; gematuriya, proteinuriya va gipertenziya bilan kechadi. | Glomerulonephritis | Inflammation of the renal glomeruli, usually immune-mediated, presenting with hematuria, proteinuria, and hypertension.
O'tkir poststreptokokk glomerulonefrit (APSGN) | A guruhi streptokokk infeksiyasidan 1-3 hafta o'tgach rivojlanadigan, nefritik sindrom bilan kechadigan glomerulonefrit. | Acute poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis | Glomerulonephritis developing 1-3 weeks after group A streptococcal infection and presenting as nephritic syndrome.
Nefritik sindrom (nephritic syndrome) | Gematuriya, gipertenziya, shish va buyrak funksiyasining pasayishi bilan namoyon bo'ladigan sindrom. | Nephritic syndrome | Syndrome of hematuria, hypertension, edema, and reduced renal function.
Nefrotik sindrom (nephrotic syndrome) | Katta proteinuriya (kuniga 3,5 g dan ortiq), gipoalbuminemiya, shishlar va giperlipidemiya bilan kechadigan sindrom. | Nephrotic syndrome | Syndrome of heavy proteinuria (more than 3.5 g/day), hypoalbuminemia, edema, and hyperlipidemia.
Minimal o'zgarishli kasallik (minimal change disease) | Bolalarda nefrotik sindromning eng ko'p sababi; yorug'lik mikroskopida o'zgarish yo'q, steroidlarga yaxshi javob beradi. | Minimal change disease | Most common cause of nephrotic syndrome in children, with normal light microscopy and good response to corticosteroids.
IgA nefropatiyasi (IgA nephropathy) | Glomerulalar mezangiumda IgA to'planishi bilan kechadigan, eng ko'p uchraydigan birlamchi glomerulonefrit; yuqori nafas yo'llari infeksiyasidan keyin gematuriya bilan boshlanadi. | IgA nephropathy | The most common primary glomerulonephritis, with mesangial IgA deposition and episodic hematuria often after upper respiratory infections.
Diabetik nefropatiya (diabetic nephropathy) | Qandli diabetda buyrak mayda tomirlari zararlanishi; albuminuriya va buyrak funksiyasi pasayishi bilan boradi. | Diabetic nephropathy | Kidney damage from diabetic microvascular disease marked by albuminuria and declining renal function.
Pielonefrit (pyelonephritis) | Buyrak jomi va to'qimasining bakterial yallig'lanishi; isitma, bel og'rig'i va dizuriya bilan kechadi. | Pyelonephritis | Bacterial infection of the renal pelvis and parenchyma causing fever, flank pain, and dysuria.
Sistit (cystitis) | Siydik pufagi shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi; tez-tez og'riqli siyish bilan kechadi. | Cystitis | Inflammation of the bladder mucosa causing frequent, painful urination.
Uretrit (urethritis) | Siydik chiqarish kanali shilliq qavatining yallig'lanishi; dizuriya va ajralmalar bilan kechadi. | Urethritis | Inflammation of the urethral mucosa, presenting with dysuria and discharge.
Siydik yo'llari infeksiyasi (urinary tract infection) | Siydik yo'llarining istalgan qismidagi bakterial infeksiya, eng ko'p Escherichia coli sabab bo'ladi. | Urinary tract infection (UTI) | Bacterial infection of any part of the urinary tract, most often caused by Escherichia coli.
Siydik-tosh kasalligi (urolithiasis) | Siydik yo'llarida toshlar hosil bo'lishi; buyrak sanchig'i bilan kechadi. | Urolithiasis (kidney stones) | Formation of stones in the urinary tract, causing renal colic.
Buyrak sanchig'i (renal colic) | Siydik yo'lining toshdan to'silishi sabab kelib chiqadigan, bel sohasidan chov tomon tarqaladigan kuchli to'lg'oqsimon og'riq. | Renal colic | Severe colicky flank pain radiating to the groin, caused by obstruction of the ureter, usually by a stone.
Gidronefroz (hydronephrosis) | Siydik chiqishi qiyinlashishi tufayli buyrak jomi va kosachalarining kengayishi. | Hydronephrosis | Dilation of the renal pelvis and calyces due to obstructed urine outflow.
O'tkir buyrak shikastlanishi (acute kidney injury) | Buyrak funksiyasining soatlar yoki kunlar ichida keskin pasayishi; kreatinin ortadi, diurez kamayishi mumkin. | Acute kidney injury (AKI) | Rapid decline in kidney function over hours to days with rising creatinine and possibly reduced urine output.
Surunkali buyrak kasalligi (chronic kidney disease) | Buyrak tuzilishi yoki funksiyasining kamida 3 oy davom etuvchi buzilishi (KFT 60 mL/min dan past yoki buyrak zararlanish belgilari). | Chronic kidney disease (CKD) | Abnormality of kidney structure or function lasting at least 3 months, e.g., GFR below 60 mL/min/1.73 m2 or markers of kidney damage.
Uremiya (uremia) | Buyrak yetishmovchiligida azot almashinuvi mahsulotlarining to'planishi bilan kechadigan zaharlanish sindromi. | Uremia | Syndrome of toxicity caused by accumulation of nitrogenous waste in kidney failure.
Azotemiya (azotemia) | Qonda mochevina va kreatinin kabi azotli moddalarning ko'payishi. | Azotemia | Elevated blood levels of nitrogenous waste such as urea and creatinine.
Poliuriya (polyuria) | Kunlik siydik hajmining ortishi (odatda 2,5-3 litrdan ko'p). | Polyuria | Excessive urine output, generally more than 2.5-3 L per day.
Oliguriya (oliguria) | Kunlik siydik miqdorining kamayishi (taxminan 400-500 mL dan kam). | Oliguria | Reduced urine output, roughly below 400-500 mL per day.
Anuriya (anuria) | Siydik ajralishining deyarli to'liq to'xtashi (kuniga 100 mL dan kam). | Anuria | Absence of urine production, below about 100 mL per day.
Gematuriya (hematuria) | Siydikda qon bo'lishi; ko'z bilan ko'rinadigan (makro) yoki faqat mikroskopda aniqlanadigan (mikro) bo'ladi. | Hematuria | Presence of blood in the urine, either visible (gross) or detected only microscopically.
Proteinuriya (proteinuria) | Siydikda oqsil ajralishining oshishi. | Proteinuria | Excess protein excretion in the urine.
Dizuriya (dysuria) | Siyish paytidagi og'riq yoki achishish; siydik yo'llari infeksiyasining tipik belgisi. | Dysuria | Pain or burning during urination, a typical symptom of urinary tract infection.
Polikistoz buyrak kasalligi (polycystic kidney disease) | Buyraklarda ko'p sonli kistalar hosil bo'lishi bilan kechadigan, irsiy kasallik. | Polycystic kidney disease | Inherited disorder characterized by numerous cysts in the kidneys.
Buyrak hujayrali karsinomasi (renal cell carcinoma) | Buyrak naychalari epiteliyidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma; gematuriya, bel og'rig'i va o'sma paypaslanishi mumkin. | Renal cell carcinoma | Malignant tumor of the renal tubular epithelium that may present with hematuria, flank pain, and a palpable mass.
Prostata giperplaziyasi (benign prostatic hyperplasia) | Prostata bezining xavfsiz kattalashishi, siyish qiyinlashuvi bilan kechadi. | Benign prostatic hyperplasia | Non-cancerous enlargement of the prostate causing urinary obstruction symptoms.
Prostatit (prostatitis) | Prostata bezining yallig'lanishi. | Prostatitis | Inflammation of the prostate gland.
Prostata saratoni (prostate cancer) | Prostata bezidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma, odatda adenokartsinoma. | Prostate cancer | Malignant tumor of the prostate, usually adenocarcinoma.
Siydik pufagi saratoni (bladder cancer) | Siydik pufagi epiteliyidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma; og'riqsiz gematuriya asosiy belgisi. | Bladder cancer | Malignant tumor of the bladder urothelium, typically presenting with painless hematuria.

## Endokrin va modda almashinuvi kasalliklari | Endocrine and metabolic diseases
Qandli diabet (diabetes mellitus) | Insulin ajralishi yoki ta'sirining buzilishi tufayli giperglikemiya bilan kechadigan surunkali modda almashinuvi kasalligi. | Diabetes mellitus | Chronic metabolic disorder characterized by hyperglycemia due to defective insulin secretion or action.
Qandli diabet, 1-tip (type 1 diabetes mellitus) | Oshqozon osti bezi beta-hujayralarining autoimmun yemirilishi tufayli insulin mutlaq tanqisligi; odatda yoshlikda boshlanadi. | Type 1 diabetes mellitus | Autoimmune destruction of pancreatic beta cells causing absolute insulin deficiency, typically beginning in youth.
Qandli diabet, 2-tip (type 2 diabetes mellitus) | Insulin qarshiligi va nisbiy insulin tanqisligi bilan kechadigan, semizlik bilan bog'liq eng ko'p uchraydigan diabet turi. | Type 2 diabetes mellitus | Most common form of diabetes, caused by insulin resistance and relative insulin deficiency, strongly linked to obesity.
Homiladorlar diabeti (gestational diabetes) | Homiladorlik davrida birinchi marta aniqlangan glyukoza bardoshligi buzilishi. | Gestational diabetes mellitus | Glucose intolerance first recognized during pregnancy.
Prediabet (prediabetes) | Qondagi glyukoza me'yordan yuqori, ammo diabet mezoniga yetmagan holat; diabet xavfini oshiradi. | Prediabetes | Elevated blood glucose that does not meet diabetes criteria but raises diabetes risk.
Diabetik ketoatsidoz (diabetic ketoacidosis) | Insulin tanqisligi tufayli giperglikemiya, ketonlar to'planishi va metabolik atsidoz bilan kechadigan o'tkir asorat. | Diabetic ketoacidosis | Acute complication of insulin deficiency with hyperglycemia, ketone accumulation, and metabolic acidosis.
Giperosmolyar holat (hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state) | 2-tip diabetda juda yuqori glyukoza va suvsizlanish bilan kechadigan, ketoatsidozsiz o'tkir asorat. | Hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state | Acute complication of type 2 diabetes marked by extreme hyperglycemia and dehydration without significant ketoacidosis.
Gipoglikemiya (hypoglycemia) | Qondagi glyukoza miqdorining pasayishi; terlash, titroq, ochlik hissi va hushdan ketish bilan kechadi. | Hypoglycemia | Low blood glucose causing sweating, tremor, hunger, and loss of consciousness.
Diabetik retinopatiya (diabetic retinopathy) | Diabet tufayli to'r pardasi mayda tomirlarining zararlanishi; ko'rlikning asosiy sabablaridan biri. | Diabetic retinopathy | Damage to retinal small vessels caused by diabetes and a leading cause of blindness.
Diabetik neyropatiya (diabetic neuropathy) | Diabetda periferik nervlarning zararlanishi; oyoq-qo'llarda uvishish, og'riq va sezuvchanlik pasayishi. | Diabetic neuropathy | Peripheral nerve damage in diabetes causing numbness, pain, and loss of sensation.
Diabetik oyoq sindromi (diabetic foot) | Diabetda neyropatiya va tomir zararlanishi tufayli oyoqda yara, infeksiya va gangrena rivojlanishi. | Diabetic foot | Foot ulceration, infection, and gangrene in diabetes caused by neuropathy and vascular disease.
Tireotoksikoz (thyrotoxicosis) | Qonda qalqonsimon bez gormonlarining ortiqchaligi tufayli yuzaga keladigan klinik holat. | Thyrotoxicosis | Clinical state caused by excess circulating thyroid hormones.
Diffuz toksik bo'qoq (Greyvs kasalligi) (Graves disease) | TTG retseptorlariga antitanalar tufayli qalqonsimon bezning ortiqcha ishlashi; ekzoftalm va tireotoksikoz bilan kechadi. | Graves disease | Autoimmune hyperthyroidism caused by TSH-receptor antibodies, with goiter and often exophthalmos.
Gipotireoz (hypothyroidism) | Qalqonsimon bez gormonlari yetishmasligi; holsizlik, sovuqqa chidamsizlik, bradikardiya va shishlar. | Hypothyroidism | Deficiency of thyroid hormones causing fatigue, cold intolerance, bradycardia, and myxedema.
Hashimoto tireoiditi (Hashimoto thyroiditis) | Qalqonsimon bezning autoimmun yallig'lanishi; gipotireozning eng ko'p sababi. | Hashimoto thyroiditis | Autoimmune thyroid inflammation and the most common cause of hypothyroidism.
Bo'qoq (goiter) | Qalqonsimon bezning kattalashishi. | Goiter | Enlargement of the thyroid gland.
Endemik bo'qoq (endemic goiter) | Yod tanqisligi tufayli ma'lum hududlarda keng tarqalgan bo'qoq. | Endemic goiter | Goiter prevalent in regions with iodine deficiency.
Kretinizm (cretinism) | Bolalik davridagi og'ir tug'ma gipotireoz natijasida jismoniy va aqliy rivojlanishning orqada qolishi. | Congenital hypothyroidism (cretinism) | Severe physical and intellectual developmental delay due to untreated congenital thyroid hormone deficiency.
Qalqonsimon bez saratoni (thyroid cancer) | Qalqonsimon bez to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma; papillyar shakli eng ko'p uchraydi. | Thyroid cancer | Malignant tumor of the thyroid gland, with papillary carcinoma the most common type.
Giperparatireoz (hyperparathyroidism) | Qalqonsimon oldi bezlari gormoni (PTG) ortiqchaligi, giperkalsiemiya va suyak zararlanishi bilan kechadi. | Hyperparathyroidism | Excess parathyroid hormone causing hypercalcemia and bone loss.
Gipoparatireoz (hypoparathyroidism) | PTG tanqisligi, gipokalsiemiya va tetaniya bilan kechadi. | Hypoparathyroidism | Parathyroid hormone deficiency causing hypocalcemia and tetany.
Itsenko-Kushing sindromi (Cushing syndrome) | Glyukokortikoidlarning uzoq muddat ortiqchaligi tufayli yuz va tana semirishi, striyalar va gipertenziya. | Cushing syndrome | Syndrome of prolonged glucocorticoid excess causing central obesity, striae, and hypertension.
Addison kasalligi (Addison disease) | Buyrak usti bezi po'stlog'i yetishmovchiligi; holsizlik, teri pigmentatsiyasi va gipotenziya bilan kechadi. | Addison disease | Primary adrenocortical insufficiency causing fatigue, skin hyperpigmentation, and hypotension.
Feoxromositoma (pheochromocytoma) | Buyrak usti bezi mag'iz qismidan chiqib, katexolaminlar ajratadigan o'sma; xurujsimon gipertenziya bilan kechadi. | Pheochromocytoma | Catecholamine-secreting tumor of the adrenal medulla causing paroxysmal hypertension.
Birlamchi giperaldosteronizm (primary hyperaldosteronism) | Aldosteronning ortiqcha ajralishi tufayli gipertenziya va gipokaliemiya (Kon sindromi). | Primary hyperaldosteronism | Excess aldosterone production causing hypertension and hypokalemia (Conn syndrome).
Akromegaliya (acromegaly) | Kattalarda o'sish gormoni ortiqchaligi tufayli qo'l, oyoq va yuz suyaklarining kattalashishi. | Acromegaly | Enlargement of hands, feet, and facial features due to excess growth hormone in adults.
Gigantizm (gigantism) | Bolalik davrida o'sish gormoni ortiqchaligi tufayli bo'yning me'yordan ortiq o'sishi. | Gigantism | Excessive linear growth caused by growth hormone excess before epiphyseal closure.
Gipofiz mitti bo'yligi (pituitary dwarfism) | Bolalikda o'sish gormoni tanqisligi tufayli bo'yning o'smay qolishi. | Growth hormone deficiency (pituitary dwarfism) | Short stature caused by deficiency of growth hormone in childhood.
Qandsiz diabet (diabetes insipidus) | Antidiuretik gormon (vazopressin) tanqisligi yoki unga buyrak sezgirligi pasayishi tufayli ko'p miqdorda suyultirilgan siydik ajralishi. | Diabetes insipidus | Deficiency of or renal resistance to antidiuretic hormone, causing large volumes of dilute urine.
Prolaktinoma (prolactinoma) | Gipofizning prolaktin ishlab chiqaruvchi xavfsiz o'smasi; galaktoreya va hayz buzilishiga sabab bo'ladi. | Prolactinoma | Benign prolactin-secreting pituitary tumor causing galactorrhea and menstrual disturbance.
Gipopituitarizm (hypopituitarism) | Gipofiz old bo'lagi gormonlarining bir yoki bir nechtasi yetishmovchiligi. | Hypopituitarism | Deficiency of one or more anterior pituitary hormones.
Semizlik (obesity) | Tana vazni indeksi (TVI) 30 kg/m2 va undan yuqori bo'lishi; yog' to'qimasining ortiqcha to'planishi. | Obesity | Excess accumulation of body fat, defined as a body mass index of 30 kg/m2 or higher.
Podagra (gout) | Siydik kislotasi kristallarining bo'g'imlarda cho'kishi tufayli takroriy o'tkir artrit xurujlari. | Gout | Recurrent acute arthritis caused by deposition of uric acid (monosodium urate) crystals in joints.
Giperurikemiya (hyperuricemia) | Qonda siydik kislotasi miqdorining oshishi; podagra va siydik tosh kasalligiga zamin yaratadi. | Hyperuricemia | Elevated serum uric acid, which predisposes to gout and kidney stones.
Osteoporoz (osteoporosis) | Suyak massasining kamayishi va mikroarxitekturasining buzilishi tufayli suyaklar mo'rtlashishi va sinish xavfi ortishi. | Osteoporosis | Loss of bone mass and microarchitectural deterioration that increases bone fragility and fracture risk.
Osteomalyatsiya (osteomalacia) | Kattalarda D vitamini tanqisligi sabab suyak mineralizatsiyasining buzilishi. | Osteomalacia | Impaired bone mineralization in adults, most often due to vitamin D deficiency.
Raxit (rickets) | Bolalarda D vitamini tanqisligi tufayli suyaklar mineralizatsiyasining buzilishi va deformatsiyasi. | Rickets | Impaired bone mineralization and deformity in children due to vitamin D deficiency.
Fenilketonuriya (phenylketonuria) | Fenilalanin gidroksilaza tanqisligi tufayli fenilalaninning to'planishi; davolanmasa aqliy rivojlanish orqada qoladi. | Phenylketonuria | Inherited deficiency of phenylalanine hydroxylase causing phenylalanine accumulation and intellectual disability if untreated.
Galaktozemiya (galactosemia) | Galaktoza almashinuvining irsiy buzilishi; chaqaloqlarda jigar zararlanishi va katarakta bilan kechadi. | Galactosemia | Inherited disorder of galactose metabolism causing liver damage and cataracts in infants.
Glikogenozlar (glycogen storage diseases) | Glikogen sintezi yoki parchalanishi fermentlarining irsiy tanqisligi. | Glycogen storage diseases | Inherited deficiencies of enzymes involved in glycogen synthesis or breakdown.
Amiloidoz (amyloidosis) | To'qimalarda noto'g'ri bukilgan oqsil (amiloid) to'planishi bilan a'zolar faoliyati buzilishi. | Amyloidosis | Extracellular deposition of misfolded amyloid protein that impairs organ function.
Dehidratatsiya (dehydration) | Organizmda suv va elektrolitlar yetishmovchiligi. | Dehydration | Deficit of body water and electrolytes.
Giperkaliemiya (hyperkalemia) | Qonda kaliy miqdorining oshishi; yurak ritmi buzilishiga olib kelishi mumkin. | Hyperkalemia | Elevated serum potassium that can cause cardiac arrhythmias.
Gipokaliemiya (hypokalemia) | Qonda kaliy miqdorining kamayishi; mushak zaifligi va aritmiyalarga sabab bo'ladi. | Hypokalemia | Low serum potassium causing muscle weakness and arrhythmias.
Giponatriemiya (hyponatremia) | Qonda natriy miqdorining kamayishi (135 mmol/L dan past). | Hyponatremia | Low serum sodium, below 135 mmol/L.
Gipernatriemiya (hypernatremia) | Qonda natriy miqdorining oshishi (145 mmol/L dan yuqori). | Hypernatremia | Elevated serum sodium, above 145 mmol/L.
Giperkalsiemiya (hypercalcemia) | Qonda kalsiy miqdorining oshishi; ko'pincha giperparatireoz yoki o'smalar sababli, holsizlik va qabziyat bilan kechadi. | Hypercalcemia | Elevated serum calcium, often from hyperparathyroidism or malignancy, causing weakness and constipation.
Gipokalsiemiya (hypocalcemia) | Qonda kalsiy miqdorining kamayishi; tetaniya va talvasalarga olib kelishi mumkin. | Hypocalcemia | Low serum calcium that can lead to tetany and seizures.
Metabolik atsidoz (metabolic acidosis) | Qonda bikarbonat kamayishi yoki kislotalar ortishi tufayli pH ning pasayishi. | Metabolic acidosis | Fall in blood pH due to decreased bicarbonate or accumulation of acids.
Metabolik alkaloz (metabolic alkalosis) | Qonda bikarbonat ortishi yoki kislotalar yo'qolishi tufayli pH ning ko'tarilishi. | Metabolic alkalosis | Rise in blood pH due to increased bicarbonate or loss of acid.
