## Yurak-qon tomir kasalliklari | Cardiovascular diseases
Arterial gipertenziya (arterial hypertension) | Arterial qon bosimining surunkali ko'tarilishi: kamida ikki marta o'lchashda sistolik bosim 140 mm sim. ust. va/yoki diastolik bosim 90 mm sim. ust. dan yuqori. | Arterial hypertension | Chronic elevation of arterial blood pressure, defined as systolic of 140 mm Hg or more and/or diastolic of 90 mm Hg or more on repeated measurements.
Essensial gipertenziya (essential hypertension) | Aniq sababi topilmaydigan birlamchi arterial gipertenziya; gipertoniya kasalligining asosiy turi. | Essential hypertension | Primary arterial hypertension without an identifiable cause, accounting for most hypertension cases.
Simptomatik gipertenziya (secondary hypertension) | Buyrak, endokrin yoki boshqa kasallik oqibatida rivojlanadigan ikkilamchi arterial gipertenziya. | Secondary hypertension | Hypertension caused by an identifiable underlying disorder such as renal or endocrine disease.
Gipertonik kriz (hypertensive crisis) | Qon bosimining keskin ko'tarilishi (odatda 180/120 mm sim. ust. dan yuqori); nishon a'zolar zararlanishi bilan murakkablashishi mumkin. | Hypertensive crisis | Severe acute rise in blood pressure, usually above 180/120 mm Hg, which may cause target-organ damage.
Ortostatik gipotenziya (orthostatic hypotension) | Yotgan holatdan turganda qon bosimining keskin pasayishi; bosh aylanishi va hushdan ketishga olib kelishi mumkin. | Orthostatic hypotension | Sustained fall in blood pressure upon standing, causing dizziness or fainting.
Yurakning ishemik kasalligi (YuIK) (ischemic heart disease) | Yurak mushagining qon bilan ta'minlanishi koronar arteriyalar torayishi tufayli talabga javob bermasligi. | Ischemic heart disease (IHD) | Impaired blood supply to the myocardium due to narrowed coronary arteries, so that supply does not meet demand.
Stenokardiya (angina pectoris) | Miokard ishemiyasi tufayli ko'krak orqasida siqilish yoki bosuvchi og'riq; jismoniy zo'riqishda paydo bo'ladi va dam olganda yoki nitroglitserindan o'tadi. | Angina pectoris | Retrosternal pressing or squeezing pain from myocardial ischemia, provoked by exertion and relieved by rest or nitroglycerin.
Beqaror stenokardiya (unstable angina) | Dam olish holatida yoki ozgina zo'riqishda paydo bo'ladigan, tobora kuchayuvchi stenokardiya; infarkt xavfi yuqori. | Unstable angina | Angina occurring at rest or with minimal exertion with increasing frequency or severity, carrying a high risk of myocardial infarction.
Prinsmetal stenokardiyasi (variant angina) | Koronar arteriya spazmi tufayli dam olishda, ko'pincha tunda paydo bo'ladigan stenokardiya; EKGda ST segmentining ko'tarilishi bilan kechadi. | Prinzmetal (variant) angina | Rest angina, often at night, caused by coronary artery spasm and accompanied by transient ST-segment elevation.
Miokard infarkti (myocardial infarction) | Koronar qon oqimi to'xtashi tufayli yurak mushagining bir qismi nekrozi; uzoq davom etuvchi ko'krak og'rig'i bilan boshlanadi. | Myocardial infarction | Necrosis of part of the heart muscle due to interrupted coronary blood flow, typically presenting with prolonged chest pain.
ST ko'tarilishli miokard infarkti (STEMI) | EKGda ST segmenti ko'tarilgan, koronar arteriya to'liq to'silishidan kelib chiqadigan infarkt; shoshilinch reperfuziyani talab qiladi. | ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) | Myocardial infarction with ST-segment elevation on ECG caused by complete coronary occlusion, requiring urgent reperfusion.
ST ko'tarilishsiz miokard infarkti (NSTEMI) | Troponinlar ko'tarilgan, ammo EKGda ST segmenti ko'tarilmagan infarkt; koronar arteriya qisman to'silgan bo'ladi. | Non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI) | Myocardial infarction with elevated troponin but no ST-segment elevation, typically from partial coronary occlusion.
O'tkir koronar sindrom (acute coronary syndrome) | Beqaror stenokardiya, NSTEMI va STEMI ni birlashtiruvchi, koronar blyashka yorilishi bilan bog'liq holatlar guruhi. | Acute coronary syndrome | Group of conditions, including unstable angina, NSTEMI, and STEMI, caused by acute coronary plaque rupture and thrombosis.
Infarktdan keyingi kardioskleroz (post-infarction cardiosclerosis) | Miokard infarkti o'rnida mushak to'qimasi o'rniga biriktiruvchi to'qima chandig'i hosil bo'lishi. | Post-infarction cardiosclerosis | Replacement of necrotic myocardium with connective tissue scar after myocardial infarction.
Yurak anevrizmasi (cardiac aneurysm) | Infarktdan keyin chandiqlangan qorincha devorining sirtga bo'rtib chiqishi. | Ventricular aneurysm | Outward bulging of a scarred ventricular wall following myocardial infarction.
Kardiogen shok (cardiogenic shock) | Yurakning nasos funksiyasi keskin pasayishi tufayli to'qimalarning qon bilan ta'minlanishi buzilishi; infarktning og'ir asorati. | Cardiogenic shock | Inadequate tissue perfusion caused by severe failure of the heart's pumping function, a serious complication of myocardial infarction.
Surunkali yurak yetishmovchiligi (chronic heart failure) | Yurak a'zolar talab qiladigan qon hajmini haydab bera olmasligi; nafas qisilishi, holsizlik va shishlar bilan kechadi. | Chronic heart failure | Inability of the heart to pump enough blood to meet tissue needs, presenting with dyspnea, fatigue, and edema.
O'tkir yurak yetishmovchiligi (acute heart failure) | Yurak nasos funksiyasining birdan buzilishi; yurak astmasi, o'pka shishi yoki kardiogen shok bilan namoyon bo'lishi mumkin. | Acute heart failure | Sudden deterioration of cardiac pumping function that may present as cardiac asthma, pulmonary edema, or cardiogenic shock.
Yurak astmasi (cardiac asthma) | Chap qorincha yetishmovchiligi tufayli, ko'pincha tunda paydo bo'ladigan keskin nafas qisilishi xuruji. | Cardiac asthma | Attack of acute breathlessness, often nocturnal, caused by left ventricular failure.
Kardiomiopatiya (cardiomyopathy) | Yurak mushagining qopqoq, koronar yoki gipertoniya bilan bog'liq bo'lmagan birlamchi kasalligi. | Cardiomyopathy | Primary disease of the heart muscle not caused by valvular, coronary, or hypertensive disease.
Kengaygan kardiomiopatiya (dilated cardiomyopathy) | Yurak bo'shliqlarining kengayishi va qisqarish kuchining pasayishi bilan kechadigan kardiomiopatiya. | Dilated cardiomyopathy | Cardiomyopathy marked by enlarged cardiac chambers and reduced contractile function.
Gipertrofik kardiomiopatiya (hypertrophic cardiomyopathy) | Ko'pincha irsiy, qorincha devori, ayniqsa qorinchalararo to'siq qalinlashuvi bilan kechadigan kardiomiopatiya; yosh sportchilarda to'satdan o'limning sababi bo'lishi mumkin. | Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy | Often inherited cardiomyopathy with thickening of the ventricular wall, especially the septum, and a cause of sudden death in young athletes.
Restriktiv kardiomiopatiya (restrictive cardiomyopathy) | Qorincha devori qattiqlashib, diastolik to'lishi buzilishi bilan kechadigan kardiomiopatiya. | Restrictive cardiomyopathy | Cardiomyopathy in which stiff ventricular walls impair diastolic filling.
Miokardit (myocarditis) | Yurak mushagining yallig'lanishi; ko'pincha virusli infeksiya orqasidan rivojlanadi. | Myocarditis | Inflammation of the myocardium, often following a viral infection.
Perikardit (pericarditis) | Yurak xaltasi (perikard) yallig'lanishi; ko'krak og'rig'i va perikard ishqalanish shovqini bilan kechadi. | Pericarditis | Inflammation of the pericardium causing chest pain and a pericardial friction rub.
Yurak tamponadasi (cardiac tamponade) | Perikard bo'shlig'ida suyuqlik yoki qon to'planib, yurak to'lishining buzilishi; shoshilinch perikardiotsentez talab qilinadi. | Cardiac tamponade | Compression of the heart by fluid or blood accumulating in the pericardial sac, impairing filling and requiring urgent drainage.
Infeksion endokardit (infective endocarditis) | Yurak ichki qavati va qopqoqlarining mikroblar sabab bo'ladigan yallig'lanishi; vegetatsiyalar hosil bo'ladi. | Infective endocarditis | Microbial infection of the endocardium and heart valves with formation of vegetations.
O'tkir revmatik isitma (acute rheumatic fever) | A guruhi β-gemolitik streptokokk infeksiyasidan keyin rivojlanadigan, yurak, bo'g'imlar va asab tizimini zararlaydigan yallig'lanish kasalligi. | Acute rheumatic fever | Inflammatory disease following group A streptococcal infection that affects the heart, joints, and nervous system.
Revmatik yurak kasalligi (rheumatic heart disease) | Revmatik isitmadan keyin qopqoqlarning surunkali zararlanishi, ayniqsa mitral stenoz. | Rheumatic heart disease | Chronic valvular damage, especially mitral stenosis, resulting from rheumatic fever.
Mitral stenoz (mitral stenosis) | Chap bo'lmachadan qorinchaga o'tuvchi mitral teshikning torayishi; chap bo'lmacha kengayadi va o'pka dimlanishi rivojlanadi. | Mitral stenosis | Narrowing of the mitral valve orifice that obstructs left atrial emptying, causing left atrial enlargement and pulmonary congestion.
Mitral yetishmovchilik (mitral regurgitation) | Mitral qopqoqning to'liq yopilmasligi tufayli qonning qorinchadan chap bo'lmachaga qaytishi. | Mitral regurgitation | Backflow of blood from the left ventricle into the left atrium due to incomplete closure of the mitral valve.
Mitral qopqoq prolapsi (mitral valve prolapse) | Sistola paytida mitral qopqoq varaqlarining chap bo'lmacha tomon bo'rtib chiqishi. | Mitral valve prolapse | Bulging of the mitral valve leaflets into the left atrium during systole.
Aorta stenozi (aortic stenosis) | Aorta qopqog'ining torayishi; stenokardiya, hushdan ketish va yurak yetishmovchiligi uchligi bilan kechadi. | Aortic stenosis | Narrowing of the aortic valve, classically causing angina, syncope, and heart failure.
Aorta yetishmovchiligi (aortic regurgitation) | Aorta qopqog'ining yopilmasligi tufayli diastolada qonning aortadan chap qorinchaga qaytishi. | Aortic regurgitation | Diastolic backflow of blood from the aorta into the left ventricle due to incompetence of the aortic valve.
Trikuspidal yetishmovchilik (tricuspid regurgitation) | Uch tavaqali qopqoq yetishmovchiligi tufayli qonning o'ng qorinchadan o'ng bo'lmachaga qaytishi. | Tricuspid regurgitation | Backflow of blood from the right ventricle to the right atrium due to tricuspid valve incompetence.
Tug'ma yurak nuqsonlari (congenital heart defects) | Homiladorlik davrida yurak va yirik tomirlar rivojlanishidagi anomaliyalar. | Congenital heart defects | Structural abnormalities of the heart and great vessels present from birth.
Bo'lmachalararo to'siq nuqsoni (atrial septal defect) | Chap va o'ng bo'lmachalar o'rtasidagi to'siqda teshik bo'lishi; chapdan o'ngga qon oqimi kuzatiladi. | Atrial septal defect | Opening in the septum between the atria, producing a left-to-right shunt.
Qorinchalararo to'siq nuqsoni (ventricular septal defect) | Qorinchalar o'rtasidagi to'siqda teshik bo'lishi; eng ko'p uchraydigan tug'ma yurak nuqsonlaridan biri. | Ventricular septal defect | Opening in the septum between the ventricles, one of the most common congenital heart defects.
Ochiq arterial yo'l (patent ductus arteriosus) | Tug'ilgandan keyin aorta va o'pka arteriyasi orasidagi yo'lning yopilmay qolishi. | Patent ductus arteriosus | Failure of the fetal ductus arteriosus between the aorta and pulmonary artery to close after birth.
Fallo tetradasi (tetralogy of Fallot) | To'rt belgidan iborat siyanotik nuqson: o'pka arteriyasi stenozi, qorinchalararo to'siq nuqsoni, aorta dekstrapozitsiyasi va o'ng qorincha gipertrofiyasi. | Tetralogy of Fallot | Cyanotic congenital defect with four features: pulmonary stenosis, ventricular septal defect, overriding aorta, and right ventricular hypertrophy.
Aorta koarktatsiyasi (coarctation of the aorta) | Aorta bo'ylab bir joyning tug'ma torayishi; yuqori oyoq-qo'llarda bosim yuqori, pastki oyoqlarda past bo'ladi. | Coarctation of the aorta | Congenital narrowing of the aorta causing higher blood pressure in the arms than in the legs.
Aritmiya (arrhythmia) | Yurak ritmi tezligi, muntazamligi yoki o'tkazuvchanligi buzilishi. | Arrhythmia | Abnormality in the rate, regularity, or conduction of the heartbeat.
Bo'lmachalar fibrillyatsiyasi (atrial fibrillation) | Bo'lmachalarning tartibsiz, tez qisqarishi bilan kechadigan eng ko'p uchraydigan aritmiya; insult xavfini oshiradi. | Atrial fibrillation | The most common sustained arrhythmia, with chaotic rapid atrial activity and irregularly irregular ventricular response, increasing stroke risk.
Bo'lmachalar pirpirashi (atrial flutter) | Bo'lmachalarning taxminan 300 martagacha tez va muntazam qisqarishi bilan kechadigan aritmiya. | Atrial flutter | Rapid, regular atrial arrhythmia at about 300 beats per minute with a sawtooth pattern on ECG.
Qorinchalar fibrillyatsiyasi (ventricular fibrillation) | Qorinchalarning tartibsiz qisqarishi, qon haydalishi to'xtashi; darhol defibrillyatsiya talab qiladigan yurak to'xtashi shakli. | Ventricular fibrillation | Chaotic ventricular electrical activity with no effective pumping, a form of cardiac arrest requiring immediate defibrillation.
Qorincha taxikardiyasi (ventricular tachycardia) | Qorinchalardan chiqadigan tez ritm (daqiqasiga 100 dan ortiq) bilan kechadigan aritmiya. | Ventricular tachycardia | Rapid rhythm (over 100 beats per minute) originating in the ventricles.
Supraventrikulyar taxikardiya (supraventricular tachycardia) | Qorinchalardan yuqorida, bo'lmachalar yoki AV tugunda paydo bo'ladigan tez va muntazam ritm. | Supraventricular tachycardia | Rapid, regular rhythm arising above the ventricles, in the atria or atrioventricular node.
Sinus taxikardiyasi (sinus tachycardia) | Sinus tugunidan chiqadigan, yurak urish tezligi daqiqasiga 100 dan oshishi. | Sinus tachycardia | Sinus-node rhythm with a heart rate above 100 beats per minute.
Sinus bradikardiyasi (sinus bradycardia) | Sinus tugunidan chiqadigan, yurak urish tezligi daqiqasiga 60 dan kam bo'lishi. | Sinus bradycardia | Sinus-node rhythm with a heart rate below 60 beats per minute.
Ekstrasistoliya (premature beat) | Yurakning navbatdan tashqari, muddatidan oldin qisqarishi. | Premature beat (extrasystole) | Heartbeat occurring earlier than expected in the normal cycle.
Atrioventrikulyar blokada (atrioventricular block) | Bo'lmachalardan qorinchalarga impuls o'tishining sekinlashishi yoki to'xtashi; I, II va III darajalarga bo'linadi. | Atrioventricular block | Delay or interruption of impulse conduction from the atria to the ventricles, graded I to III.
Volf-Parkinson-Uayt sindromi (Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome) | Bo'lmachalar va qorinchalar o'rtasida qo'shimcha o'tkazuvchi yo'l bo'lishi; qorinchalar erta qo'zg'aladi (delta to'lqini) va taxikardiya xurujlari kuzatiladi. | Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome | Presence of an accessory atrioventricular pathway causing early ventricular activation (delta wave) and episodes of tachycardia.
Uzaygan QT sindromi (long QT syndrome) | EKGda QT intervalining uzayishi, o'limga olib kelishi mumkin bo'lgan qorincha aritmiyalari xavfi bilan kechadigan holat. | Long QT syndrome | Prolongation of the QT interval on ECG that predisposes to life-threatening ventricular arrhythmias.
Sinus tugunining sustlik sindromi (sick sinus syndrome) | Sinus tugunining faoliyati buzilishi: bradikardiya va taxikardiya almashinuvi. | Sick sinus syndrome | Dysfunction of the sinus node causing alternating bradycardia and tachycardia.
Yurak to'xtashi (cardiac arrest) | Yurakning to'satdan samarali qisqarishdan to'xtashi, nafas va qon aylanishining to'xtashi. | Cardiac arrest | Sudden cessation of effective cardiac contraction with loss of circulation and breathing.
Sinkopa (syncope) | Hushdan ketish: bosh miya qon ta'minoti vaqtincha kamayishi tufayli qisqa muddatli ong yo'qolishi. | Syncope | Transient loss of consciousness due to temporary reduction of cerebral blood flow.
Ateroskleroz (atherosclerosis) | Arteriya devorida xolesterin va lipidlar to'planishi, ateromatoz blyashkalar hosil bo'lishi va tomir yo'li torayishi bilan o'tadigan kasallik. | Atherosclerosis | Disease in which cholesterol-rich plaques build up in arterial walls, narrowing the lumen.
Aorta anevrizmasi (aortic aneurysm) | Aorta devorining mahalliy kengayishi; yorilib ketish xavfi bor. | Aortic aneurysm | Localized dilation of the aortic wall with a risk of rupture.
Aorta disseksiyasi (aortic dissection) | Aorta ichki qavatining yirtilishi va qonning devor qatlamlari orasiga kirishi; kuchli yirtilib ketuvchi og'riq bilan boshlanadi. | Aortic dissection | Tear in the aortic intima allowing blood to enter the vessel wall layers, causing sudden tearing chest or back pain.
Chuqur vena trombozi (deep vein thrombosis) | Chuqur venalarda, ko'pincha oyoqlarda tromb hosil bo'lishi; o'pka emboliyasiga olib kelishi mumkin. | Deep vein thrombosis | Formation of a blood clot in the deep veins, usually of the leg, which may embolize to the lungs.
Tromboflebit (thrombophlebitis) | Venaning yallig'lanishi va unda tromb hosil bo'lishi. | Thrombophlebitis | Inflammation of a vein with associated clot formation.
Varikoz kasalligi (varicose veins) | Oyoq yuza venalarining kengayishi, buralib qolishi va qopqoqlari yetishmovchiligi. | Varicose veins | Dilated, tortuous superficial leg veins with incompetent valves.
Surunkali vena yetishmovchiligi (chronic venous insufficiency) | Oyoq venalaridan qon qaytishi buzilishi; shish, teri o'zgarishi va trofik yaralar bilan kechadi. | Chronic venous insufficiency | Impaired venous return from the legs, producing edema, skin changes, and venous ulcers.
Oyoq arteriyalari obliteratsiyalovchi aterosklerozi (peripheral artery disease) | Oyoq arteriyalari torayishi tufayli yurganda oqsoqlanish (intermittirlovchi xromota) va og'riq bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Peripheral artery disease | Narrowing of the leg arteries causing pain on walking (intermittent claudication) that eases with rest.
Reyno sindromi (Raynaud phenomenon) | Sovuq yoki hayajonda barmoqlar arteriyalarining spazmi tufayli ularning oqarishi, ko'karishi va qizarishi. | Raynaud phenomenon | Episodic spasm of digital arteries causing finger pallor, cyanosis, and redness with cold or stress.
Vaskulit (vasculitis) | Qon tomir devorining yallig'lanishi bilan kechadigan kasalliklar guruhi. | Vasculitis | Group of diseases characterized by inflammation of blood vessel walls.
Takayasu arteriti (Takayasu arteritis) | Aorta va uning yirik tarmoqlarining surunkali yallig'lanishi; yosh ayollarda ko'proq uchraydi, qo'llarda puls susayishi bilan kechadi. | Takayasu arteritis | Chronic inflammation of the aorta and its major branches, more common in young women, causing diminished arm pulses.
Kavasaki kasalligi (Kawasaki disease) | Bolalarda o'rta o'lchamli arteriyalarning vaskuliti: uzoq isitma, toshma, shilliq pardalar qizarishi, koronar anevrizmalar xavfi. | Kawasaki disease | Vasculitis of medium-sized arteries in children with prolonged fever, rash, mucosal redness, and risk of coronary aneurysms.
Dislipidemiya (dyslipidemia) | Qonda lipidlar (xolesterin, triglitseridlar) miqdorining me'yordan og'ishi. | Dyslipidemia | Abnormal levels of blood lipids such as cholesterol and triglycerides.
Giperxolesterinemiya (hypercholesterolemia) | Qonda umumiy yoki LDL xolesterin miqdorining oshishi. | Hypercholesterolemia | Elevated total or LDL cholesterol in the blood.
Metabolik sindrom (metabolic syndrome) | Qorin semirishi, gipertenziya, qand almashinuvi buzilishi va dislipidemiya birikmasi; yurak-qon tomir xavfini oshiradi. | Metabolic syndrome | Cluster of abdominal obesity, hypertension, impaired glucose metabolism, and dyslipidemia that raises cardiovascular risk.
Shok (shock) | To'qimalarning qon bilan ta'minlanishi keskin buzilib, hayotiy a'zolar faoliyati izdan chiqadigan holat. | Shock | Life-threatening state of inadequate tissue perfusion leading to organ dysfunction.
Kollaps (collapse) | Tomir tonusining keskin pasayishi tufayli qon bosimining tushib ketishi. | Circulatory collapse | Acute fall in blood pressure due to a sharp drop in vascular tone.
Yurak shishi (cardiac edema) | Yurak yetishmovchiligi tufayli, ayniqsa oyoqlarda, to'qimalarda suyuqlik to'planishi. | Cardiac edema | Fluid accumulation in the tissues, especially the legs, caused by heart failure.
Assit (ascites) | Qorin bo'shlig'ida erkin suyuqlik to'planishi; jigar sirrozi, yurak yetishmovchiligi va boshqa kasalliklarda uchraydi. | Ascites | Free fluid accumulation in the peritoneal cavity, seen in cirrhosis, heart failure, and other diseases.
