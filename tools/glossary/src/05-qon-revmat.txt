## Qon kasalliklari | Blood diseases
Kamqonlik (anemiya) (anemia) | Qonda gemoglobin yoki eritrotsitlar miqdorining kamayishi (SGST bo'yicha kattalar erkaklarda Hb <130 g/L, ayollarda <120 g/L). | Anemia | Reduced hemoglobin or red cell mass; by WHO criteria hemoglobin below 130 g/L in men and 120 g/L in non-pregnant women.
Temir tanqisligi anemiyasi (iron deficiency anemia) | Temir yetishmasligi tufayli gemoglobin sintezi buzilishi; mikrotsitar, gipoxrom anemiya. | Iron deficiency anemia | Microcytic hypochromic anemia caused by insufficient iron for hemoglobin synthesis.
B12-tanqislik anemiyasi (vitamin B12 deficiency anemia) | B12 vitamini yetishmasligi tufayli makrotsitar (megaloblast) anemiya; nevrologik buzilishlar bilan birga kelishi mumkin. | Vitamin B12 deficiency anemia | Macrocytic megaloblastic anemia from vitamin B12 deficiency, possibly with neurologic deficits.
Zararli anemiya (pernicious anemia) | Ichki omilga autoimmun reaksiya tufayli B12 so'rilishining buzilishi natijasidagi anemiya. | Pernicious anemia | Autoimmune loss of intrinsic factor leading to vitamin B12 malabsorption and megaloblastic anemia.
Foliy tanqisligi anemiyasi (folate deficiency anemia) | Foliy kislotasi yetishmasligidan kelib chiqadigan megaloblast anemiya. | Folate deficiency anemia | Megaloblastic anemia caused by insufficient folic acid.
Gemolitik anemiya (hemolytic anemia) | Eritrotsitlarning muddatidan oldin parchalanishi tufayli yuzaga keladigan anemiya. | Hemolytic anemia | Anemia caused by premature destruction of red blood cells.
Aplastik anemiya (aplastic anemia) | Suyak ko'migi hujayralari yetishmasligi tufayli barcha qon hujayralari (pansitopeniya) kamayishi. | Aplastic anemia | Bone marrow failure causing reduction of all blood cell lines (pancytopenia).
O'roqsimon hujayrali anemiya (sickle cell disease) | Gemoglobin S tufayli eritrotsitlarning o'roqsimon shaklga kirishi, tomirlar to'silishi va og'riq krizlari bilan o'tadigan irsiy kasallik. | Sickle cell disease | Inherited hemoglobinopathy with hemoglobin S causing red cell sickling, vaso-occlusion, and pain crises.
Talassemiya (thalassemia) | Globin zanjirlari sintezi buzilishi bilan o'tadigan irsiy gemoglobinopatiya. | Thalassemia | Inherited disorder of reduced globin chain synthesis causing anemia.
Irsiy sferotsitoz (hereditary spherocytosis) | Eritrotsit membranasi oqsillari nuqsoni tufayli sharsimon eritrotsitlar va gemolitik anemiya. | Hereditary spherocytosis | Red cell membrane defect producing spherical erythrocytes and hemolytic anemia.
G6PD tanqisligi (G6PD deficiency) | Glyukoza-6-fosfatdegidrogenaza tanqisligi; ayrim dorilar yoki ovqat ta'sirida gemoliz paydo bo'ladi. | G6PD deficiency | X-linked enzyme deficiency that predisposes red cells to hemolysis triggered by certain drugs or foods.
Autoimmun gemolitik anemiya (autoimmune hemolytic anemia) | Eritrotsitlarga qarshi antitanalar tufayli ularning parchalanishi. | Autoimmune hemolytic anemia | Destruction of red cells by autoantibodies.
Polisitemiya (polycythemia vera) | Suyak ko'migi o'smasi tufayli eritrotsitlar miqdorining haddan tashqari oshishi (JAK2 mutatsiyasi bilan bog'liq). | Polycythemia vera | Myeloproliferative neoplasm with excessive red cell production, usually associated with a JAK2 mutation.
Leykoz (leukemia) | Qon hosil qiluvchi hujayralarning xavfli o'smasi, suyak ko'migi va qonning zararlanishi. | Leukemia | Malignant disease of blood-forming cells affecting bone marrow and blood.
O'tkir limfoblast leykoz (acute lymphoblastic leukemia) | Limfoid o'tmishdosh hujayralarning xavfli ko'payishi; bolalarda eng ko'p uchraydigan leykoz. | Acute lymphoblastic leukemia | Malignant proliferation of lymphoid precursors and the most common childhood leukemia.
O'tkir miyeloid leykoz (acute myeloid leukemia) | Miyeloid o'tmishdosh hujayralarning xavfli ko'payishi; kattalarda ko'proq uchraydi. | Acute myeloid leukemia | Malignant proliferation of myeloid precursors, more common in adults.
Surunkali miyeloid leykoz (chronic myeloid leukemia) | Filadelfiya xromosomasi (BCR-ABL) bilan bog'liq surunkali leykoz; tirozinkinaza inhibitorlari bilan davolanadi. | Chronic myeloid leukemia | Chronic leukemia associated with the Philadelphia chromosome (BCR-ABL), treated with tyrosine kinase inhibitors.
Surunkali limfotsitar leykoz (chronic lymphocytic leukemia) | Yetuk B-limfotsitlarning surunkali ko'payishi; keksa odamlarda uchraydi. | Chronic lymphocytic leukemia | Chronic accumulation of mature B lymphocytes, mainly in older adults.
Limfoma (lymphoma) | Limfa to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma. | Lymphoma | Malignant tumor of lymphoid tissue.
Xodjkin limfomasi (Hodgkin lymphoma) | Rid-Shternberg (Reed-Sternberg) hujayralari bilan tavsiflanadigan limfoma. | Hodgkin lymphoma | Lymphoma characterized by Reed-Sternberg cells.
Non-Xodjkin limfomalar (non-Hodgkin lymphoma) | Xodjkin limfomasidan boshqa limfoid o'smalar guruhi. | Non-Hodgkin lymphoma | Heterogeneous group of lymphoid malignancies other than Hodgkin lymphoma.
Ko'p miyeloma (multiple myeloma) | Plazmatik hujayralarning xavfli ko'payishi; suyak yemirilishi, anemiya, buyrak zararlanishi va paraproteinemiya bilan kechadi. | Multiple myeloma | Malignant plasma cell proliferation causing bone destruction, anemia, renal injury, and monoclonal protein.
Trombotsitopeniya (thrombocytopenia) | Qonda trombotsitlar miqdorining kamayishi (150×10⁹/L dan past). | Thrombocytopenia | Low platelet count, below 150×10⁹/L.
Immun trombotsitopenik purpura (immune thrombocytopenia) | Trombotsitlarga autoantitanalar tufayli ularning kamayishi va teri hamda shilliq pardalarda qon quyilishlar. | Immune thrombocytopenia (ITP) | Autoimmune platelet destruction causing low platelet counts and mucocutaneous bleeding.
Gemofiliya (hemophilia) | VIII (A) yoki IX (B) ivish omili tanqisligi bilan o'tadigan, X-bilan bog'liq irsiy qon ketish kasalligi. | Hemophilia | X-linked inherited bleeding disorder caused by deficiency of factor VIII (A) or IX (B).
Villebrand kasalligi (von Willebrand disease) | Villebrand omili tanqisligi yoki nuqsoni tufayli kelib chiqadigan eng ko'p uchraydigan irsiy qon ketish kasalligi. | Von Willebrand disease | The most common inherited bleeding disorder, caused by deficient or defective von Willebrand factor.
DVS-sindrom (disseminated intravascular coagulation) | Tarqalgan tomir ichi qon ivishi va keyinchalik ivish omillarining sarflanishi tufayli qon ketishi. | Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC) | Widespread intravascular clotting followed by consumption of clotting factors and bleeding.
Gemorragik vaskulit (Shenlein-Genox purpurasi) (IgA vasculitis) | Mayda tomirlarning IgA vositasidagi yallig'lanishi; toshma, qorin og'rig'i, artrit va nefrit bilan kechadi. | IgA vasculitis (Henoch-Schönlein purpura) | IgA-mediated small-vessel vasculitis with palpable purpura, abdominal pain, arthritis, and nephritis.
Neytropeniya (neutropenia) | Neytrofillar sonining kamayishi (1,5×10⁹/L dan past), infeksiya xavfini oshiradi. | Neutropenia | Reduced neutrophil count (below 1.5×10⁹/L), raising infection risk.
Agranulotsitoz (agranulocytosis) | Granulotsitlarning deyarli yo'qolishi; og'ir infeksiyalar xavfi. | Agranulocytosis | Near-total absence of granulocytes with high risk of severe infection.
Leykotsitoz (leukocytosis) | Qonda leykotsitlar sonining oshishi. | Leukocytosis | Elevated white blood cell count.
Eozinofiliya (eosinophilia) | Qonda eozinofillar sonining oshishi. | Eosinophilia | Elevated eosinophil count in the blood.
Pansitopeniya (pancytopenia) | Eritrotsitlar, leykotsitlar va trombotsitlarning uchala turi miqdorining kamayishi. | Pancytopenia | Reduction of red cells, white cells, and platelets.
Qon ketishi (gemorragik sindrom) (hemorrhagic syndrome) | Qon tomirlar, trombotsitlar yoki ivish tizimi buzilishi tufayli qon ketishga moyillik. | Bleeding diathesis | Tendency to bleed because of vascular, platelet, or coagulation abnormalities.
Tromboz (thrombosis) | Tomir ichida qon laxtasi (tromb) hosil bo'lishi. | Thrombosis | Formation of a blood clot (thrombus) within a vessel.
Emboliya (embolism) | Qon oqimi bilan keltirilgan jism (tromb, havo, yog' va h.k.) tomirni to'sib qo'yishi. | Embolism | Obstruction of a vessel by material carried in the bloodstream, such as a thrombus, air, or fat.
Trombofiliya (thrombophilia) | Tromboz xavfini oshiradigan irsiy yoki orttirilgan holat. | Thrombophilia | Inherited or acquired predisposition to thrombosis.
Sepsis (sepsis) | Infeksiyaga organizmning tartibsiz javobi tufayli hayot uchun xavfli a'zolar disfunksiyasi. | Sepsis | Life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection.
Septik shok (septic shock) | Sepsisda suyuqlik terapiyasiga qaramay davom etuvchi gipotenziya va to'qima gipoperfuziyasi. | Septic shock | Sepsis with persistent hypotension and tissue hypoperfusion despite fluid resuscitation.
Bakteriemiya (bacteremia) | Qonda bakteriyalarning bo'lishi. | Bacteremia | Presence of bacteria in the bloodstream.

## Revmatologiya va immunologiya | Rheumatology and immunology
Revmatoid artrit (rheumatoid arthritis) | Mayda bo'g'imlarning simmetrik yallig'lanishi va ertalabki qotishi bilan kechadigan autoimmun kasallik. | Rheumatoid arthritis | Autoimmune disease causing symmetric inflammation of small joints with prolonged morning stiffness.
Osteoartroz (osteoarthritis) | Bo'g'im tog'ayining degenerativ yemirilishi; yuklanishda og'riq va qisqa muddatli qotish. | Osteoarthritis | Degenerative joint disease with cartilage loss, pain on loading, and brief stiffness.
Ankilozlovchi spondilit (ankylosing spondylitis) | Umurtqa va dumg'aza-yonbosh bo'g'imlarining surunkali yallig'lanishi; ertalabki bel og'rig'i va umurtqa harakatchanligining kamayishi. | Ankylosing spondylitis | Chronic inflammation of the spine and sacroiliac joints with inflammatory back pain and reduced spinal mobility.
Psoriatik artrit (psoriatic arthritis) | Psoriaz bilan bog'liq bo'g'im yallig'lanishi. | Psoriatic arthritis | Inflammatory arthritis associated with psoriasis.
Reaktiv artrit (reactive arthritis) | Ichak yoki siydik-jinsiy infeksiyadan keyin rivojlanadigan asimmetrik artrit. | Reactive arthritis | Asymmetric arthritis that develops after gastrointestinal or genitourinary infection.
Yuvenil idiopatik artrit (juvenile idiopathic arthritis) | 16 yoshgacha boshlanib, kamida 6 hafta davom etadigan noma'lum sababli artrit. | Juvenile idiopathic arthritis | Arthritis of unknown cause beginning before age 16 and lasting at least 6 weeks.
Septik artrit (septic arthritis) | Bo'g'imning bakterial infeksiyasi; shoshilinch davolashni talab qiladi. | Septic arthritis | Bacterial joint infection requiring urgent treatment.
Tizimli qizil yugurik (systemic lupus erythematosus) | Ko'p a'zolarni zararlaydigan autoimmun kasallik; yuzda kapalaksimon toshma, artrit, nefrit va antiyadro antitanalar bilan kechadi. | Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) | Multisystem autoimmune disease with malar rash, arthritis, nephritis, and antinuclear antibodies.
Tizimli sklerodermiya (systemic sclerosis) | Teri va ichki a'zolar fibrozi, Reyno hodisasi va tomir zararlanishi bilan kechadigan autoimmun kasallik. | Systemic sclerosis | Autoimmune disease with skin and visceral fibrosis, Raynaud phenomenon, and vasculopathy.
Dermatomiozit (dermatomyositis) | Mushaklar yallig'lanishi va xarakterli teri toshmalari bilan kechadigan autoimmun kasallik. | Dermatomyositis | Autoimmune inflammatory myopathy with characteristic skin rash.
Polimiozit (polymyositis) | Proksimal mushaklar zaifligi bilan kechadigan mushaklarning autoimmun yallig'lanishi. | Polymyositis | Autoimmune inflammation of skeletal muscle causing proximal muscle weakness.
Sjogren sindromi (Sjögren syndrome) | Ko'z yosh va so'lak bezlarining autoimmun zararlanishi; quruq ko'z va quruq og'iz. | Sjögren syndrome | Autoimmune damage of lacrimal and salivary glands causing dry eyes and dry mouth.
Antifosfolipid sindrom (antiphospholipid syndrome) | Antifosfolipid antitanalar bilan bog'liq tromboz va homiladorlik asoratlari. | Antiphospholipid syndrome | Autoimmune disorder with antiphospholipid antibodies causing thrombosis and pregnancy complications.
Gigant hujayrali arteriit (giant cell arteritis) | Keksalarda bosh va bo'yin arteriyalari vaskuliti; bosh og'rig'i, ko'rlik xavfi. | Giant cell arteritis | Vasculitis of cranial arteries in older adults causing headache and risk of vision loss.
Polimialgiya revmatika (polymyalgia rheumatica) | Keksalarda yelka va tos kamari mushaklarida og'riq hamda ertalabki qotishi. | Polymyalgia rheumatica | Pain and morning stiffness of shoulder and hip girdles in older adults.
Vegener granulematozi (granulomatosis with polyangiitis) | O'rta va mayda tomirlarning ANCA bilan bog'liq vaskuliti; yuqori nafas yo'llari, o'pka va buyrak zararlanadi. | Granulomatosis with polyangiitis | ANCA-associated vasculitis affecting the upper airways, lungs, and kidneys.
Behchet kasalligi (Behçet disease) | Og'iz va jinsiy a'zolarda takroriy yaralar hamda uveit bilan kechadigan vaskulit. | Behçet disease | Vasculitis with recurrent oral and genital ulcers and uveitis.
Fibromialgiya (fibromyalgia) | Tarqalgan mushak-skelet og'rig'i, charchoq va uyqu buzilishi bilan kechadigan sindrom. | Fibromyalgia | Syndrome of widespread musculoskeletal pain, fatigue, and sleep disturbance.
Anafilaksiya (anaphylaxis) | Allergenga tez rivojlanadigan, hayot uchun xavfli tizimli allergik reaksiya; adrenalin bilan shoshilinch davolanadi. | Anaphylaxis | Rapid, life-threatening systemic allergic reaction requiring immediate epinephrine.
Anafilaktik shok (anaphylactic shock) | Anafilaksiyada qon bosimi keskin tushib ketishi. | Anaphylactic shock | Profound hypotension and circulatory collapse during anaphylaxis.
Quincke shishi (angioedema) | Teri osti va shilliq qavat chuqur qatlamlarining to'satdan shishishi, ayniqsa yuz va hiqildoqda. | Angioedema | Sudden swelling of deep dermal and mucosal tissues, especially of the face and larynx.
Ko'chma toshma (urtikariya) (urticaria) | Qichishuvchi, tez paydo bo'lib yo'qoladigan teri g'adir-budurlari (shish bo'rtmalari). | Urticaria | Itchy transient wheals on the skin.
Atopik dermatit (atopic dermatitis) | Qichishish va quruq teri bilan kechadigan surunkali, qaytalanuvchi allergik teri yallig'lanishi. | Atopic dermatitis | Chronic, relapsing itchy inflammatory skin disease associated with atopy.
Immunotanqislik (immunodeficiency) | Immun tizimi funksiyasining tug'ma yoki orttirilgan pasayishi, infeksiyalarga moyillik. | Immunodeficiency | Congenital or acquired impairment of immune function leading to increased susceptibility to infection.
OIV infeksiyasi (HIV infection) | CD4+ T-limfotsitlarni zararlaydigan immunitet tanqisligi virusi qo'zg'atadigan surunkali infeksiya. | HIV infection | Chronic infection by the human immunodeficiency virus, which depletes CD4+ T lymphocytes.
OITS (AIDS) | OIV infeksiyasining oxirgi bosqichi: og'ir immunotanqislik va opportunistik infeksiyalar yoki o'smalar. | Acquired immunodeficiency syndrome (AIDS) | Advanced stage of HIV infection with severe immunodeficiency and opportunistic infections or malignancies.
Transplantat-xo'jayinga qarshi reaksiya (graft-versus-host disease) | Donor immun hujayralarining qabul qiluvchi to'qimalarini zararlashi. | Graft-versus-host disease | Attack of donor immune cells on recipient tissues after transplantation.
