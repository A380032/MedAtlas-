## Nefrologiya va urologiya | Nephrology and urology
O'tkir buyrak yetishmovchiligi (acute kidney injury) | Buyrak funksiyasining tez pasayishi. | Acute kidney injury | Rapid decline of kidney function.
Nikturiya (nocturia) | Tunda siydik uchun turish. | Nocturia | Waking at night to urinate.
Buyrak-tosh kasalligi (urolithiasis) | Siydik yo'llarida tosh hosil bo'lishi. | Urolithiasis | Stone formation in the urinary tract.
## Pediatriya | Pediatrics
Chala tug'ilgan chaqaloq (premature infant) | 37 haftagacha tug'ilgan bola. | Premature infant | Infant born before 37 weeks.
Chaqaloq sarig'i (neonatal jaundice) | Yangi tug'ilganlarda bilirubin ko'tarilishi bilan teri sarg'ayishi. | Neonatal jaundice | Skin yellowing from raised bilirubin in newborns.
Diatez (diathesis) | Bolaning teri va shilliq qavat reaksiyalariga moyilligi. | Diathesis | A child's tendency to skin and mucosal reactions.
Tug'ma yurak nuqsoni (congenital heart defect) | Yurak tuzilishining tug'ilishdan nuqsonli bo'lishi. | Congenital heart defect | Structural heart abnormality present from birth.
Dauna sindromi (Down syndrome) | 21-xromosoma trisomiyasi, aqliy rivojlanishning orqada qolishi bilan. | Down syndrome | Trisomy 21 with intellectual disability.
Serebral falaj (cerebral palsy) | Bolalikda miya zararlanishidan kelib chiqqan harakat buzilishlari. | Cerebral palsy | Movement disorders from early brain injury.
BSJ emlashi (BCG vaccine) | Silga qarshi tug'ruqxonada qilinadigan emlash. | BCG vaccine | Anti-tuberculosis vaccine given in the maternity ward.
Ko'krak suti bilan boqish (breastfeeding) | Chaqaloqni ona suti bilan oziqlantirish. | Breastfeeding | Feeding an infant with mother's milk.
Qo'shimcha ovqat (complementary feeding) | Ona sutiga qo'shib beriladigan birinchi oziq, taxminan 6 oylikdan. | Complementary feeding | Foods added to breast milk, usually from about 6 months.
Bolalarda qorin og'rig'i (infantile colic) | Chaqaloqda uzoq yig'lash bilan kechuvchi qorin sanchig'i. | Infantile colic | Prolonged crying with abdominal discomfort in infants.
Bolalar psixomotor rivojlanishi (psychomotor development) | Bolaning harakat va aqliy ko'nikmalarining yoshga mos o'sishi. | Psychomotor development | Age-appropriate growth of a child's motor and mental skills.
Bolalarda boshning aylanasi (head circumference) | Bolaning bosh miya rivojlanishini baholovchi o'lcham. | Head circumference | Measure used to assess a child's brain growth.
Katta liqildoq (anterior fontanelle) | Chaqaloq bosh suyaklari orasidagi old yumshoq joy. | Anterior fontanelle | Soft spot between a newborn's skull bones at the front.
Bolalarda o'sish (growth) | Tana o'lchamlarining yoshga qarab ortishi. | Growth | Increase in body size with age.
Balog'at (puberty) | Jinsiy yetilish davri. | Puberty | Period of sexual maturation.
## Jarrohlik va anesteziologiya | Surgery and anesthesiology
Mastektomiya (mastectomy) | Sut bezini olib tashlash. | Mastectomy | Surgical removal of the breast.
Drenaj (drainage) | Yara yoki bo'shliqdan suyuqlikni chiqarish. | Drainage | Removal of fluid from a wound or cavity.
Tikuv (suture) | Yara chetlarini bog'lash usuli yoki ipi. | Suture | Method or thread used to close wound edges.
Reanimatsiya (resuscitation) | Hayotiy faoliyatni tiklash choralari. | Resuscitation | Measures to restore vital functions.
O'pkaning sun'iy ventilyatsiyasi (mechanical ventilation) | Nafasni apparat yordamida ta'minlash. | Mechanical ventilation | Machine-assisted breathing.
Kateterizatsiya (catheterization) | Naycha (kateter) kiritish, masalan tomir yoki siydik pufagiga. | Catheterization | Insertion of a catheter, e.g., into a vein or bladder.
Zond (probe) | A'zo ichiga kiritiladigan ingichka naycha. | Probe | Thin tube inserted into an organ.
Fibrogastroskopiya (gastroscopy) | Qizilo'ngach va oshqozonni endoskop bilan ko'rish. | Gastroscopy | Endoscopic inspection of the esophagus and stomach.
Kolonoskopiya (colonoscopy) | Yo'g'on ichakni endoskop bilan ko'rish. | Colonoscopy | Endoscopic inspection of the colon.
Sistoskopiya (cystoscopy) | Siydik pufagini endoskop bilan ko'rish. | Cystoscopy | Endoscopic inspection of the bladder.
Flyuorografiya (fluorography) | Ko'krak qafasining profilaktik rentgen tekshiruvi. | Fluorography | Screening chest X-ray examination.
Stsintigrafiya (scintigraphy) | Radioaktiv modda yordamida a'zo faoliyatini tasvirlash. | Scintigraphy | Imaging organ function with a radioactive tracer.
Pozitron-emission tomografiya (positron emission tomography) | Radioaktiv shakar yordamida to'qima almashinuvini tasvirlash. | Positron emission tomography | Imaging tissue metabolism using a radioactive glucose tracer.
Kontrast modda (contrast agent) | Tasvirda a'zolarni yaxshi ko'rsatish uchun yuboriladigan modda. | Contrast agent | Substance given to enhance organ visibility on imaging.
Doppler tekshiruvi (Doppler ultrasound) | Qon oqimini ultratovush bilan baholash. | Doppler ultrasound | Assessment of blood flow with ultrasound.
## Anatomiya | Anatomy
Skelet (skeleton) | Tanani qo'llab-quvvatlovchi suyaklar majmuasi. | Skeleton | The set of bones supporting the body.
Kalla suyaklari (cranial bones) | Bosh miyani o'rab turuvchi suyaklar. | Cranial bones | Bones enclosing the brain.
Umurtqalararo disk (intervertebral disc) | Umurtqalar orasidagi tog'ay yostiqcha. | Intervertebral disc | Cartilage pad between vertebrae.
Qovurg'alar (ribs) | Ko'krak qafasini hosil qiluvchi 12 juft yoysimon suyak. | Ribs | Twelve pairs of curved bones forming the thoracic cage.
Kuraksimon suyak (scapula) | Yelka kamarining yassi uchburchak suyagi. | Scapula | Flat triangular bone of the shoulder girdle.
Tizza qopqog'i (patella) | Tizza bo'g'imining old tomonidagi yassi suyak. | Patella | Flat bone at the front of the knee.
Paylar (tendon) | Mushakni suyakka biriktiruvchi zich tola. | Tendon | Dense fibrous cord attaching muscle to bone.
Silliq mushak (smooth muscle) | Ichki a'zolar devoridagi ixtiyorsiz mushak. | Smooth muscle | Involuntary muscle in the walls of internal organs.
Yurak qorinchalari (ventricles) | Yurakning qonni haydovchi pastki kameralari. | Ventricles | Lower pumping chambers of the heart.
Yurak bo'lmachalari (atria) | Yurakning qonni qabul qiluvchi yuqori kameralari. | Atria | Upper receiving chambers of the heart.
Kovak vena (vena cava) | Qonni tanadan o'ng bo'lmachaga olib keluvchi yirik vena. | Vena cava | Large vein returning blood from the body to the right atrium.
To'g'ri ichak (rectum) | Yo'g'on ichakning oxirgi qismi. | Rectum | The final part of the large intestine.
Siydik yo'li (ureter) | Buyrakdan siydik pufagiga siydik o'tkazuvchi nay. | Ureter | Tube carrying urine from kidney to bladder.
Tuxumdon (ovary) | Tuxum hujayra va ayol jinsiy gormonlarini ishlab chiqaruvchi bez. | Ovary | Gland producing eggs and female sex hormones.
Urug'don (testis) | Spermatozoid va testosteron ishlab chiqaruvchi erkak jinsiy bezi. | Testis | Male gland producing sperm and testosterone.
Miya po'stlog'i (cerebral cortex) | Bosh miya yarim sharlarining tashqi kulrang qavati. | Cerebral cortex | Outer gray layer of the cerebral hemispheres.
Uzunchoq miya (medulla oblongata) | Nafas va yurak faoliyati markazlari joylashgan miya tanasi qismi. | Medulla oblongata | Brainstem part with respiratory and cardiac centers.
Bosh miya pardalari (meninges) | Miyani o'rab turuvchi uch qavat parda. | Meninges | Three membranes enclosing the brain.
Likvor (cerebrospinal fluid) | Miya va orqa miya atrofidagi shaffof suyuqlik. | Cerebrospinal fluid | Clear fluid surrounding the brain and spinal cord.
Retikulyar formatsiya (reticular formation) | Uyg'oqlikni boshqaruvchi miya tanasi tuzilmasi. | Reticular formation | Brainstem network regulating wakefulness.
Talamus (thalamus) | Sezgi signallarini po'stloqqa uzatuvchi miya markazi. | Thalamus | Brain relay center sending sensory signals to the cortex.
Teri (skin) | Tanani qoplab turuvchi eng katta a'zo. | Skin | The largest organ, covering the body.
## Fiziologiya va biokimyo qo'shimcha | Physiology and biochemistry extras
Diffuziya (diffusion) | Moddalarning konsentratsiya past tomonga tarqalishi. | Diffusion | Spread of substances toward lower concentration.
Membrana potensiali (membrane potential) | Hujayra membranasi ikki tomoni orasidagi elektr farq. | Membrane potential | Electrical difference across a cell membrane.
Harakat potensiali (action potential) | Neyron yoki mushak hujayrasidagi tez elektr signal. | Action potential | Rapid electrical signal in a neuron or muscle cell.
Qon pH (blood pH) | Qonning kislota-ishqor ko'rsatkichi, me'yorda 7,35–7,45. | Blood pH | Acid–base measure of blood, normally 7.35–7.45.
Giponatremiya (hyponatremia) | Qonda natriy miqdorining kamayishi. | Hyponatremia | Low sodium level in the blood.
Gipokalsemiya (hypocalcemia) | Qonda kalsiy miqdorining kamayishi. | Hypocalcemia | Low calcium level in the blood.
Giperkalsemiya (hypercalcemia) | Qonda kalsiy miqdorining oshishi. | Hypercalcemia | Elevated calcium level in the blood.
Fermentlar (enzymes) | Kimyoviy reaksiyalarni tezlashtiruvchi oqsillar. | Enzymes | Proteins that speed up chemical reactions.
Albumin (albumin) | Qon plazmasidagi asosiy oqsil, onkotik bosimni saqlaydi. | Albumin | Main plasma protein maintaining oncotic pressure.
Trigliseridlar (triglycerides) | Organizmdagi asosiy yog' shakli. | Triglycerides | The main form of fat in the body.
## Jamoat salomatligi | Public health
Inkubatsiya davri (incubation period) | Zararlanishdan birinchi belgilargacha o'tgan vaqt. | Incubation period | Time from infection to first symptoms.
Sezgirlik (sensitivity) | Testning kasal odamlarni to'g'ri aniqlash qobiliyati. | Sensitivity | Ability of a test to correctly identify the sick.
Spetsifiklik (specificity) | Testning sog'lom odamlarni to'g'ri aniqlash qobiliyati. | Specificity | Ability of a test to correctly identify the healthy.
Isbotlangan tibbiyot (evidence-based medicine) | Qarorlarni ilmiy dalillarga asoslovchi tibbiyot yondashuvi. | Evidence-based medicine | Medical approach basing decisions on scientific evidence.
Tasodifiy nazoratli sinov (randomized controlled trial) | Ishtirokchilar tasodifiy guruhlarga bo'linadigan klinik tadqiqot. | Randomized controlled trial | Clinical study with random allocation to groups.
Tashxis (diagnosis) | Kasallikni aniqlash. | Diagnosis | Identification of a disease.
Differensial tashxis (differential diagnosis) | O'xshash kasalliklarni bir-biridan ajratish. | Differential diagnosis | Distinguishing similar diseases from each other.
Retsidiv (relapse) | Kasallikning qayta qo'zishi. | Relapse | Return of a disease after improvement.
