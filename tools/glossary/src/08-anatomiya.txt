## Anatomiya va fiziologiya | Anatomy and physiology
Yurak (heart) | Qon aylanishini ta'minlovchi, to'rt kamerali (ikki bo'lmacha va ikki qorincha) mushakli nasos a'zosi. | Heart | Four-chambered muscular pump (two atria, two ventricles) that drives blood circulation.
Miokard (myocardium) | Yurak devorining o'rta mushak qavati. | Myocardium | The middle muscular layer of the heart wall.
Endokard (endocardium) | Yurak bo'shliqlarini ichkaridan qoplab turuvchi yupqa ichki qavat. | Endocardium | The thin inner lining of the heart chambers.
Perikard (pericardium) | Yurakni o'rab turuvchi ikki varaqli seroz xalta. | Pericardium | The double-layered serous sac surrounding the heart.
Sinus tuguni (sinoatrial node) | O'ng bo'lmachadagi yurakning asosiy ritm yurituvchisi (pacemaker). | Sinoatrial node | The heart's natural pacemaker located in the right atrium.
Atrioventrikulyar tugun (atrioventricular node) | Impulsni bo'lmachalardan qorinchalarga o'tkazishni kechiktiruvchi o'tkazuvchi tizim tuguni. | Atrioventricular node | Conduction node that delays the impulse between the atria and ventricles.
Gis tutami (bundle of His) | Impulsni AV tugundan qorinchalarga yetkazuvchi o'tkazuvchi tolalar to'plami. | Bundle of His | Bundle of conducting fibers carrying impulses from the AV node into the ventricles.
Aorta (aorta) | Chap qorinchadan chiqadigan, organizmdagi eng yirik arteriya. | Aorta | The largest artery of the body, arising from the left ventricle.
Koronar arteriyalar (coronary arteries) | Yurak mushagini qon bilan ta'minlovchi arteriyalar. | Coronary arteries | Arteries that supply the heart muscle with blood.
Yuqori kovak vena (superior vena cava) | Boshdan, bo'yindan va qo'llardan qonni o'ng bo'lmachaga olib keluvchi yirik vena. | Superior vena cava | Large vein returning blood from the head, neck, and arms to the right atrium.
Mitral qopqoq (mitral valve) | Chap bo'lmacha va chap qorincha orasidagi ikki tavaqali qopqoq. | Mitral valve | Bicuspid valve between the left atrium and left ventricle.
Uch tavaqali qopqoq (tricuspid valve) | O'ng bo'lmacha va o'ng qorincha orasidagi qopqoq. | Tricuspid valve | Valve between the right atrium and right ventricle.
Aorta qopqog'i (aortic valve) | Chap qorincha va aorta orasidagi yarim oysimon qopqoq. | Aortic valve | Semilunar valve between the left ventricle and aorta.
Sistola (systole) | Yurak mushagining qisqarish va qonni haydash fazasi. | Systole | Phase of cardiac contraction and ejection of blood.
Diastola (diastole) | Yurak mushagining bo'shashishi va qon bilan to'lish fazasi. | Diastole | Phase of cardiac relaxation and filling with blood.
Zarba hajmi (stroke volume) | Yurak bir qisqarishda qorinchadan chiqaradigan qon miqdori. | Stroke volume | Volume of blood ejected by a ventricle in one contraction.
Yurak minut hajmi (cardiac output) | Yurak bir daqiqada haydaydigan qon miqdori: zarba hajmi va yurak urish chastotasi ko'paytmasi. | Cardiac output | Volume of blood pumped by the heart per minute: stroke volume multiplied by heart rate.
Preload (preload) | Diastola oxirida qorincha devorining cho'zilish darajasini belgilovchi hajm yuklamasi. | Preload | Ventricular wall stretch at the end of diastole determined by filling volume.
Afterload (afterload) | Qorincha qonni haydashda yengib o'tishi kerak bo'lgan qarshilik. | Afterload | Resistance the ventricle must overcome to eject blood.
Ejeksiya fraksiyasi (ejection fraction) | Chap qorincha diastolik hajmining qancha qismi sistolada haydalishi (me'yor taxminan 55-70%). | Ejection fraction | Fraction of end-diastolic volume ejected in systole (normally about 55-70%).
O'pka (lung) | Gaz almashinuvini amalga oshiruvchi juft nafas a'zosi. | Lung | Paired respiratory organ responsible for gas exchange.
Alveola (alveolus) | O'pkadagi gaz almashinuvi sodir bo'ladigan mayda havo pufakchasi. | Alveolus | Tiny air sac in the lung where gas exchange occurs.
Bronx (bronchus) | Traxeyaning o'pkaga kirishdan oldingi va undan keyingi tarmoqlari. | Bronchus | Branch of the trachea conducting air into the lungs.
Plevra (pleura) | O'pkani va ko'krak qafasi ichki yuzasini qoplovchi ikki varaqli seroz parda. | Pleura | Double-layered serous membrane covering the lungs and lining the chest wall.
Diafragma (diaphragm) | Ko'krak va qorin bo'shlig'ini ajratib turuvchi, asosiy nafas mushagi. | Diaphragm | Muscle separating the thoracic and abdominal cavities and the main muscle of respiration.
Hayotiy sig'im (vital capacity) | Maksimal nafas olishdan keyin chiqarish mumkin bo'lgan havoning eng katta hajmi. | Vital capacity | Maximum volume of air that can be exhaled after a maximal inhalation.
Oshqozon (stomach) | Ovqatni to'plovchi, kislota va pepsin bilan hazm qilishni boshlovchi kengaygan hazm trakti qismi. | Stomach | Expanded part of the digestive tract that stores food and begins digestion with acid and pepsin.
O'n ikki barmoqli ichak (duodenum) | Ingichka ichakning boshlang'ich qismi, bu yerga o't va oshqozon osti bezi shirasi quyiladi. | Duodenum | First part of the small intestine receiving bile and pancreatic juice.
Ingichka ichak (small intestine) | Ovqat hazm bo'lishi va so'rilishining asosiy qismi sodir bo'ladigan ichak. | Small intestine | Intestinal segment where most digestion and absorption occur.
Yo'g'on ichak (large intestine) | Suv va elektrolitlarni so'ruvchi va najas hosil qiluvchi ichak. | Large intestine | Bowel segment that absorbs water and electrolytes and forms stool.
Jigar (liver) | Safro ishlab chiqaruvchi, zararsizlantiruvchi va moddalar almashinuvida muhim rol o'ynovchi eng yirik bez. | Liver | The largest gland, producing bile, detoxifying substances, and central to metabolism.
O't pufagi (gallbladder) | Jigar ishlab chiqargan o'tni to'plovchi va quyultiruvchi xalta. | Gallbladder | Sac that stores and concentrates bile produced by the liver.
Oshqozon osti bezi (pancreas) | Hazm fermentlari va insulin, glyukagon ishlab chiqaruvchi aralash bez. | Pancreas | Mixed gland producing digestive enzymes and the hormones insulin and glucagon.
Taloq (spleen) | Qonni filtrlaydigan va immun javobda qatnashadigan limfoid a'zo. | Spleen | Lymphoid organ that filters blood and participates in immune responses.
Buyrak (kidney) | Qonni filtrlab siydik hosil qiluvchi, suv-tuz muvozanatini boshqaruvchi juft a'zo. | Kidney | Paired organ that filters blood to form urine and regulates fluid and electrolyte balance.
Nefron (nephron) | Buyrakning struktur-funksional birligi: koptokcha va naychalar tizimi. | Nephron | Structural and functional unit of the kidney consisting of a glomerulus and tubule system.
Siydik pufagi (urinary bladder) | Siydikni to'plovchi va chiqaruvchi mushakli xalta. | Urinary bladder | Muscular sac that stores and expels urine.
Gipofiz (pituitary gland) | Bosh miya asosida joylashgan, boshqa endokrin bezlar faoliyatini boshqaruvchi bez. | Pituitary gland | Gland at the base of the brain that controls many other endocrine glands.
Gipotalamus (hypothalamus) | Gipofiz faoliyati va ichki muhit doimiyligini boshqaruvchi miya bo'limi. | Hypothalamus | Brain region regulating pituitary function and internal homeostasis.
Qalqonsimon bez (thyroid gland) | Bo'yinda joylashgan, tiroksin va triyodtironin ishlab chiqaruvchi bez. | Thyroid gland | Neck gland producing thyroxine and triiodothyronine.
Buyrak usti bezlari (adrenal glands) | Buyraklar ustida joylashgan, kortizol, aldosteron va adrenalin ishlab chiqaruvchi bezlar. | Adrenal glands | Glands above the kidneys that produce cortisol, aldosterone, and adrenaline.
Insulin (insulin) | Oshqozon osti bezi beta-hujayralari ishlab chiqaradigan, qondagi glyukozani pasaytiruvchi gormon. | Insulin | Hormone from pancreatic beta cells that lowers blood glucose.
Kortizol (cortisol) | Buyrak usti bezi po'stlog'ida ishlab chiqariladigan stress gormoni. | Cortisol | Stress hormone produced by the adrenal cortex.
Adrenalin (epinephrine) | Buyrak usti bezi mag'izida ishlab chiqariladigan, yurak urishi va qon bosimini oshiruvchi gormon va mediator. | Epinephrine (adrenaline) | Hormone and neurotransmitter from the adrenal medulla that raises heart rate and blood pressure.
Tiroksin (thyroxine) | Qalqonsimon bez gormoni, moddalar almashinuvi tezligini boshqaradi. | Thyroxine | Thyroid hormone that regulates metabolic rate.
Bosh miya (brain) | Markaziy asab tizimining bosh suyagi ichida joylashgan bo'limi. | Brain | The part of the central nervous system inside the skull.
Orqa miya (spinal cord) | Umurtqa kanalida joylashgan, miya va tana o'rtasida impuls o'tkazuvchi markaziy asab tizimi qismi. | Spinal cord | Part of the central nervous system in the vertebral canal conducting impulses between brain and body.
Miyacha (cerebellum) | Harakatlar muvofiqlashuvi va muvozanatni boshqaruvchi miya bo'limi. | Cerebellum | Brain region controlling coordination of movement and balance.
Neyron (neuron) | Asab tizimining impuls hosil qiluvchi va o'tkazuvchi hujayrasi. | Neuron | Nerve cell that generates and conducts impulses.
Sinaps (synapse) | Neyronlar o'rtasida impuls uzatilishi sodir bo'ladigan aloqa joyi. | Synapse | Junction where signals are transmitted between neurons.
Neyromediator (neurotransmitter) | Sinapsda impulsni bir hujayradan boshqasiga uzatuvchi kimyoviy modda. | Neurotransmitter | Chemical messenger that transmits signals across a synapse.
Refleks yoyi (reflex arc) | Refleksning retseptordan ijrochi a'zogacha o'tadigan yo'li. | Reflex arc | Neural pathway of a reflex from receptor to effector.
Simpatik nerv tizimi (sympathetic nervous system) | "Jang yoki qoch" reaksiyasini yuzaga keltiruvchi vegetativ asab tizimi qismi. | Sympathetic nervous system | Division of the autonomic nervous system mediating the "fight or flight" response.
Parasimpatik nerv tizimi (parasympathetic nervous system) | Dam olish va hazm jarayonlarini ta'minlovchi vegetativ asab tizimi qismi. | Parasympathetic nervous system | Division of the autonomic nervous system promoting rest and digestion.
Qon (blood) | Tomirlarda aylanuvchi, plazma va shaklli elementlardan iborat suyuq biriktiruvchi to'qima. | Blood | Fluid connective tissue circulating in vessels, composed of plasma and formed elements.
Eritrotsit (erythrocyte) | Gemoglobin saqlovchi, kislorod tashuvchi qizil qon tanachasi. | Erythrocyte | Red blood cell containing hemoglobin that carries oxygen.
Gemoglobin (hemoglobin) | Eritrotsitlardagi temir saqlovchi, kislorod tashuvchi oqsil. | Hemoglobin | Iron-containing protein in red cells that transports oxygen.
Leykotsit (leukocyte) | Himoya funksiyasini bajaruvchi oq qon tanachasi. | Leukocyte | White blood cell involved in defense.
Trombotsit (platelet) | Qon ivishida ishtirok etuvchi mayda hujayra bo'lagi. | Platelet | Small cell fragment involved in blood clotting.
Plazma (plasma) | Qonning hujayralardan tashqari suyuq qismi. | Plasma | The fluid portion of blood excluding cells.
Gemostaz (hemostasis) | Qon ketishini to'xtatish jarayoni: tomir spazmi, trombotsit tiqini va qon ivishi. | Hemostasis | Process that stops bleeding through vasospasm, platelet plug formation, and coagulation.
Limfa (lymph) | Limfa tomirlarida oqadigan, to'qimalardan so'rilgan suyuqlik. | Lymph | Fluid collected from tissues and carried in lymphatic vessels.
Limfa tuguni (lymph node) | Limfani filtrlaydigan va immun javobda qatnashadigan mayda limfoid a'zo. | Lymph node | Small lymphoid organ that filters lymph and takes part in immune responses.
Timus (thymus) | T-limfotsitlar yetiladigan ko'krak qafasi ichidagi limfoid a'zo. | Thymus | Chest lymphoid organ where T lymphocytes mature.
Suyak ko'migi (bone marrow) | Qon hujayralari hosil bo'ladigan suyak ichidagi yumshoq to'qima. | Bone marrow | Soft tissue inside bones where blood cells are produced.
Bo'g'im (joint) | Ikki yoki undan ortiq suyakning birlashgan joyi. | Joint | Junction between two or more bones.
Tog'ay (cartilage) | Bo'g'im yuzalarini qoplovchi va tayanch vazifasini bajaruvchi elastik biriktiruvchi to'qima. | Cartilage | Elastic connective tissue that covers joint surfaces and provides support.
Pay (tendon) | Mushakni suyak bilan bog'lovchi zich biriktiruvchi to'qima. | Tendon | Dense connective tissue joining muscle to bone.
Boylam (ligament) | Suyaklarni bir-biriga bog'lovchi zich biriktiruvchi to'qima. | Ligament | Dense connective tissue connecting bones to each other.
Skelet mushagi (skeletal muscle) | Ixtiyoriy qisqaradigan, ko'ndalang-targ'il mushak to'qimasi. | Skeletal muscle | Voluntary striated muscle tissue.
Gomeostaz (homeostasis) | Organizm ichki muhitining nisbiy doimiyligini saqlash. | Homeostasis | Maintenance of relative constancy of the body's internal environment.
Metabolizm (metabolism) | Organizmda moddalar va energiya almashinuvi jarayonlari yig'indisi. | Metabolism | Sum of chemical processes of matter and energy exchange in the body.
Bazal metabolizm (basal metabolic rate) | Tinch holatda hayotiy funksiyalarni saqlash uchun sarflanadigan minimal energiya. | Basal metabolic rate | Minimum energy expended at rest to maintain vital functions.
Fagotsitoz (phagocytosis) | Hujayralarning zarrachalar yoki mikroblarni qamrab olib hazm qilishi. | Phagocytosis | Engulfment and digestion of particles or microbes by cells.
Antigen (antigen) | Immun tizimi tomonidan begona deb taniladigan va immun javob chaqiruvchi modda. | Antigen | Substance recognized as foreign that provokes an immune response.
Antitana (antibody) | B-hujayralar (plazmatik hujayralar) ishlab chiqaradigan, antigenga maxsus bog'lanuvchi oqsil (immunoglobulin). | Antibody | Immunoglobulin protein produced by plasma cells that binds a specific antigen.
Komplement (complement) | Antitanalar va fagotsitozga yordam beruvchi, mikroblarni parchalaydigan qon oqsillari tizimi. | Complement | System of plasma proteins that enhances antibody action and lyses microbes.
Immunitet (immunity) | Organizmning begona agent va o'zgargan hujayralardan himoyalanish qobiliyati. | Immunity | The body's ability to defend against foreign agents and altered cells.
Gen (gene) | Irsiy axborotni saqlovchi DNK bo'lagi. | Gene | Segment of DNA carrying hereditary information.
Mutatsiya (mutation) | Genetik materialdagi o'zgarish. | Mutation | Change in the genetic material.
Xromosoma (chromosome) | Gen saqlovchi, hujayra bo'linishida ko'rinadigan DNK-oqsil tuzilma; odamda 46 ta. | Chromosome | DNA-protein structure carrying genes; humans have 46.
Apoptoz (apoptosis) | Hujayraning genetik dasturlangan, yallig'lanishsiz o'limi. | Apoptosis | Genetically programmed, non-inflammatory form of cell death.
Nekroz (necrosis) | Shikastlanish natijasida to'qima hujayralarining nobud bo'lishi. | Necrosis | Death of tissue cells caused by injury.
Gipoksiya (hypoxia) | To'qimalarda kislorod yetishmovchiligi. | Hypoxia | Insufficient oxygen supply to tissues.
Gipoksemiya (hypoxemia) | Arterial qonda kislorod miqdorining pasayishi. | Hypoxemia | Low oxygen level in arterial blood.
Giperkapniya (hypercapnia) | Qonda karbonat angidrid (CO2) miqdorining oshishi. | Hypercapnia | Elevated carbon dioxide level in the blood.
