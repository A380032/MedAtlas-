## Qo'shimcha atamalar (A–B) | Additional terms (A–B)
Abaziya (abasia) | Oyoq mushaklari kuchi saqlangan bo'lsa ham yura olmaslik; harakatlar muvofiqlashuvi buzilishidan. | Abasia | Inability to walk despite preserved leg muscle strength, due to impaired coordination.
Abdominal jarrohlik (abdominal surgery) | Qorin bo'shlig'i a'zolarida o'tkaziladigan jarrohlik amaliyotlari bo'limi. | Abdominal surgery | Branch of surgery dealing with operations on the abdominal organs.
Abdominoskopiya (abdominoscopy) | Qorin bo'shlig'ini maxsus optik asbob bilan ko'rib tekshirish. | Abdominoscopy | Visual examination of the abdominal cavity with an optical instrument.
Abduksiya (abduction) | Qo'l yoki oyoqni tananing o'rta chizig'idan uzoqlashtirish harakati. | Abduction | Movement of a limb away from the midline of the body.
Adduksiya (adduction) | Qo'l yoki oyoqni tananing o'rta chizig'iga yaqinlashtirish harakati. | Adduction | Movement of a limb toward the midline of the body.
Aberratsiya (aberration) | Me'yordan chetlashish: optikada tasvir buzilishi, biologiyada xromosoma o'zgarishi. | Aberration | Deviation from the normal, such as image distortion in optics or a chromosomal change in biology.
Abiotrofiya (abiotrophy) | A'zo yoki to'qimaning irsiy sabablarga ko'ra vaqtidan oldin tushkunlikka uchrashi. | Abiotrophy | Premature degeneration of a tissue or organ due to inherited causes.
Ablefariya (ablepharia) | Bir yoki ikkala ko'z qovog'ining tug'ma yo'qligi. | Ablepharia | Congenital absence of one or both eyelids.
Ablyatsiya (ablation) | To'qima yoki a'zo qismini olib tashlash yoki yo'q qilish. | Ablation | Removal or destruction of tissue or part of an organ.
Abraziya (abrasion) | Teri yoki shilliq pardaning sirtqi qatlamini ishqalanish natijasida shilinishi. | Abrasion | Scraping off of the superficial layer of skin or mucosa by friction.
Absans (absence seizure) | Bir necha soniya davom etadigan, ongning qisqa muddatli o'chishi bilan kechadigan kichik tutqanoq xuruji. | Absence seizure | Brief seizure with a few seconds of impaired awareness, typical of childhood epilepsy.
Absorbsiya (absorption) | Moddalarning to'qima, shilliq parda yoki ichak devori orqali so'rilishi. | Absorption | Uptake of substances through tissue, mucosa, or the intestinal wall.
Abstinensiya (withdrawal) | Alkogol yoki giyohvand moddani to'xtatganda paydo bo'ladigan og'ir jismoniy va ruhiy buzilishlar. | Withdrawal (abstinence syndrome) | Physical and psychological disturbances following cessation of alcohol or drugs.
Abuzus (abuse) | Dori yoki psixofaol moddani me'yordan ortiq, zararli tarzda qo'llash. | Substance abuse | Harmful excessive use of a drug or psychoactive substance.
Abuliya (abulia) | Irodaning susayishi, harakat qilishga intilishning yo'qolishi. | Abulia | Reduction or loss of will and motivation to act.
Aviatsiya tibbiyoti (aviation medicine) | Parvoz sharoitining odam organizmiga ta'sirini va uchuvchilar salomatligini o'rganuvchi tibbiyot sohasi. | Aviation medicine | Branch of medicine studying the effects of flight on the body and the health of aviators.
Avitaminoz (avitaminosis) | Organizmda biror vitaminning mutlaqo yetishmasligidan kelib chiqadigan kasallik. | Avitaminosis | Disease caused by the complete deficiency of a vitamin.
Avtofagiya (autophagy) | Hujayraning o'z tarkibiy qismlarini lizosomalarda parchalab, qayta ishlatishi. | Autophagy | Process by which a cell digests and recycles its own components in lysosomes.
Avulsiya (avulsion) | To'qima, nerv yoki a'zoning kuch bilan yulib olinishi yoki uzilishi. | Avulsion | Forcible tearing away of tissue, a nerve, or an organ.
Agalaktiya (agalactia) | Tug'ruqdan keyin sut ajralishining butunlay yo'qligi. | Agalactia | Absence of milk secretion after childbirth.
Agglyutinatsiya (agglutination) | Hujayralar yoki zarrachalarning antitanalar ta'sirida bir-biriga yopishib yig'ilishi. | Agglutination | Clumping of cells or particles caused by antibodies.
Aggravatsiya (aggravation) | Bemorning o'z kasalligi belgilarini ataylab bo'rttirib ko'rsatishi. | Aggravation | Deliberate exaggeration of existing symptoms by a patient.
Agnatiya (agnathia) | Pastki jag'ning tug'ma yo'qligi. | Agnathia | Congenital absence of the lower jaw.
Agnoziya (agnosia) | Sezgi a'zolari saqlangan holda ob'ektlarni tanish qobiliyatining yo'qolishi. | Agnosia | Loss of ability to recognize objects despite intact sensory organs.
Agrafiya (agraphia) | Yozish qobiliyatining buzilishi, qo'l harakati saqlangan bo'lsa ham. | Agraphia | Loss of the ability to write despite intact motor function.
Adaktiliya (adactyly) | Qo'l yoki oyoq barmoqlarining tug'ma yo'qligi. | Adactyly | Congenital absence of fingers or toes.
Adaptometr (adaptometer) | Ko'zning qorong'ilikka moslashuvini o'lchaydigan asbob. | Adaptometer | Instrument measuring the eye's dark adaptation.
Adventitsiya (adventitia) | Qon tomirlar va ichi kovak a'zolar devorining tashqi biriktiruvchi to'qima qavati. | Adventitia | Outer connective tissue layer of vessels and hollow organs.
Adenit (adenitis) | Bez yoki limfa tugunining yallig'lanishi. | Adenitis | Inflammation of a gland or lymph node.
Adenoid (adenoid) | Burun-halqum bodomsimon bezining limfoid to'qimasi; kattalashganda burun nafasini buzadi. | Adenoid | Lymphoid tissue of the pharyngeal tonsil that obstructs nasal breathing when enlarged.
Adenoidit (adenoiditis) | Burun-halqum bodomsimon bezi, ya'ni adenoidlarning yallig'lanishi. | Adenoiditis | Inflammation of the adenoids.
Adenopatiya (adenopathy) | Bezlar yoki limfa tugunlarining kattalashishi bilan kechadigan har qanday kasallik. | Adenopathy | Any disease with enlargement of glands or lymph nodes.
Adentiya (adentia) | Tishlarning tug'ma yoki orttirilgan to'liq yo'qligi. | Adentia | Complete absence of teeth, congenital or acquired.
Adinamiya (adynamia) | Mushak kuchi va harakat faolligining keskin pasayishi, qattiq holsizlik. | Adynamia | Marked loss of muscular strength and activity with profound weakness.
Adneksit (adnexitis) | Bachadon ortiqlari, ya'ni naylar va tuxumdonlarning yallig'lanishi. | Adnexitis | Inflammation of the uterine adnexa: fallopian tubes and ovaries.
Akineziya (akinesia) | Ixtiyoriy harakatlarning boshlanishi va bajarilishining qiyinlashuvi yoki yo'qligi. | Akinesia | Absence or difficulty of initiating voluntary movements.
Akklimatizatsiya (acclimatization) | Organizmning yangi iqlim yoki balandlik sharoitiga moslashishi. | Acclimatization | Adaptation of the body to a new climate or altitude.
Akkumulyatsiya (accumulation) | Dori yoki zaharli moddaning organizmda ketma-ket qabuldan to'planib borishi. | Accumulation | Build-up of a drug or toxic substance in the body with repeated intake.
Akupunktura (acupuncture) | Teridagi maxsus nuqtalarga ingichka ignalar kiritish orqali davolash usuli. | Acupuncture | Treatment method inserting thin needles into specific skin points.
Akustik travma (acoustic trauma) | Juda kuchli tovush ta'sirida ichki quloq va eshitishning shikastlanishi. | Acoustic trauma | Damage to the inner ear and hearing from extremely loud sound.
Akusherlik (obstetrics) | Homiladorlik, tug'ruq va tug'ruqdan keyingi davrni o'rganuvchi tibbiyot sohasi. | Obstetrics | Branch of medicine concerned with pregnancy, childbirth, and the postpartum period.
Akseleratsiya (acceleration) | Bolalar jismoniy va jinsiy rivojlanishining avvalgi avlodlarga nisbatan tezlashishi. | Acceleration (developmental) | Faster physical and sexual maturation of children compared with previous generations.
Alaliya (alalia) | Bolada nutq a'zolari va eshitish saqlangan bo'lsa ham nutqning rivojlanmasligi. | Alalia | Failure of speech development in a child with intact speech organs and hearing.
Aleksiya (alexia) | Miya zararlanishi tufayli o'qish qobiliyatining yo'qolishi. | Alexia | Loss of the ability to read due to brain damage.
Alkogolizm (alcoholism) | Alkogolga bog'liqlik va uni iste'mol qilishni nazorat qila olmaslik bilan kechadigan kasallik. | Alcoholism | Disease of dependence on alcohol with loss of control over drinking.
Allopatiya (allopathy) | Kasallikka qarshi ta'sir qiluvchi dorilar bilan davolashga asoslangan an'anaviy tibbiyot usuli. | Allopathy | Conventional medicine treating disease with agents that produce opposing effects.
Alopetsiya (alopecia) | Sochning to'kilishi yoki kal bo'lib qolish. | Alopecia | Loss of hair or baldness.
Albinizm (albinism) | Melanin sintezi buzilishi tufayli teri, soch va ko'z rangsizligi bilan kechadigan irsiy kasallik. | Albinism | Inherited disorder of melanin synthesis causing lack of pigment in skin, hair, and eyes.
Albuminuriya (albuminuria) | Siydikda albumin ajralishining oshishi. | Albuminuria | Increased excretion of albumin in the urine.
Alveolit (alveolitis) | O'pka alveolalari yoki tish katakchasining yallig'lanishi. | Alveolitis | Inflammation of the pulmonary alveoli or of a tooth socket.
Algodismenoreya (algodysmenorrhea) | Hayz paytida kuchli og'riq bilan kechadigan hayz buzilishi. | Algodysmenorrhea | Menstrual disorder with severe pain during menstruation.
Amavroz (amaurosis) | Ko'z yoki ko'rish nervi kasalligi tufayli ko'rishning to'liq yo'qolishi. | Amaurosis | Complete loss of vision caused by disease of the eye or optic nerve.

Ambliopiya (amblyopia) | Ko'z tuzilishi sog'lom bo'lsa ham ko'rish o'tkirligining pasayishi ("dangasa ko'z"). | Amblyopia | Reduced visual acuity in an otherwise structurally normal eye ("lazy eye").
Amebiaz (amoebiasis) | Entamoeba histolytica qo'zg'atadigan, yo'g'on ichak yarasi va jigar abssessi bilan kechadigan parazitar kasallik. | Amoebiasis | Parasitic disease caused by Entamoeba histolytica, causing colitis and liver abscess.

Amneziya (amnesia) | Xotira yo'qolishi: voqealarni eslab qolish yoki eslay olishning buzilishi. | Amnesia | Loss of memory, with impaired ability to form or retrieve memories.
