## Mikrobiologiya va patologiya | Microbiology and pathology
Bakteriya (bacterium) | Yadrosiz, bir hujayrali prokariot mikroorganizm. | Bacterium | Single-celled prokaryotic microorganism without a nucleus.
Virus (virus) | Hujayradan tashqarida ko'payolmaydigan, nuklein kislota va oqsil qobig'idan iborat infeksion agent. | Virus | Infectious agent of nucleic acid in a protein coat that replicates only inside host cells.
Zamburug' (fungus) | Hujayra devori xitindan iborat bo'lgan yadroli mikroorganizm. | Fungus | Eukaryotic organism with a chitin-containing cell wall.
Parazit (parasite) | Boshqa organizm hisobiga yashovchi tirik mavjudot. | Parasite | Organism living on or in another organism at its expense.
Gram usulida bo'yash (Gram stain) | Bakteriyalarni hujayra devori tuzilishiga ko'ra grammusbat (binafsha) va gramnegativ (pushti) guruhlarga ajratuvchi bo'yash usuli. | Gram stain | Staining method separating bacteria into gram-positive (purple) and gram-negative (pink) by cell wall structure.
Stafilokokk (Staphylococcus) | Uzum shaklida joylashadigan grammusbat kokklar; S. aureus eng muhim vakili. | Staphylococcus | Gram-positive cocci arranged in clusters; S. aureus is the most important species.
Streptokokk (Streptococcus) | Zanjir shaklida joylashadigan grammusbat kokklar. | Streptococcus | Gram-positive cocci arranged in chains.
Pnevmokokk (Streptococcus pneumoniae) | Pnevmoniya, meningit va otit qo'zg'atuvchi kapsulali streptokokk. | Streptococcus pneumoniae | Encapsulated streptococcus causing pneumonia, meningitis, and otitis media.
Ichak tayoqchasi (Escherichia coli) | Ichak mikroflorasining normal vakili; ayrim shtammlari ich ketishi va siydik yo'llari infeksiyasini keltirib chiqaradi. | Escherichia coli | Normal intestinal commensal whose pathogenic strains cause diarrhea and urinary tract infection.
Zangori yiring tayoqchasi (Pseudomonas aeruginosa) | Gramnegativ tayoqcha, kasalxona infeksiyalari va kuyishdan keyingi infeksiyalarni qo'zg'atadi, ko'k-yashil pigment hosil qiladi. | Pseudomonas aeruginosa | Gram-negative rod causing hospital-acquired and burn infections and producing blue-green pigment.
Mikobakteriya (Mycobacterium) | Kislotaga chidamli tayoqchalar turkumi, sil va moxov qo'zg'atuvchilarini o'z ichiga oladi. | Mycobacterium | Genus of acid-fast bacilli including the agents of tuberculosis and leprosy.
Moxov (leprosy) | Mycobacterium leprae qo'zg'atadigan teri va periferik nervlarni zararlaydigan surunkali infeksiya. | Leprosy | Chronic infection by Mycobacterium leprae affecting the skin and peripheral nerves.
Spora (spore) | Noqulay sharoitga chidamli, bakteriya yoki zamburug'ning tinch shakli. | Spore | Resistant dormant form of certain bacteria or fungi.
Toksin (toxin) | Mikroblar yoki boshqa manbalar ishlab chiqaradigan zaharli modda. | Toxin | Poisonous substance produced by microbes or other sources.
Endotoksin (endotoxin) | Gramnegativ bakteriyalar hujayra devoridagi lipopolisaxarid; shok chaqirishi mumkin. | Endotoxin | Lipopolysaccharide of gram-negative bacterial cell walls that can trigger shock.
Virulentlik (virulence) | Mikrobning kasallik qo'zg'atish qobiliyati darajasi. | Virulence | Degree of a microbe's ability to cause disease.
Sterilizatsiya (sterilization) | Barcha mikroorganizmlar va sporalarni to'liq yo'q qilish. | Sterilization | Complete elimination of all microorganisms including spores.
Dezinfeksiya (disinfection) | Ob'ektlardagi patogen mikroblarni yo'q qilish. | Disinfection | Elimination of pathogenic microbes from objects.
Antiseptika (antisepsis) | Teri, yara va shilliq pardalardagi mikroblarni kamaytirish chora-tadbirlari. | Antisepsis | Measures to reduce microbes on skin, wounds, and mucous membranes.
Aseptika (asepsis) | Jarrohlik va boshqa muolajalarda mikroblar tushishining oldini olish tadbirlari. | Asepsis | Measures preventing microbial contamination during surgery and procedures.
PZR (PCR) | Polimeraza zanjirli reaksiya: nuklein kislotaning aniq bo'lagini ko'paytirib aniqlovchi molekulyar usul. | Polymerase chain reaction (PCR) | Molecular technique that amplifies a specific nucleic acid segment for detection.
IFA (ELISA) | Immunoferment tahlili: antigen yoki antitanani ferment bilan belgilangan reaksiya orqali aniqlovchi usul. | ELISA | Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay detecting antigens or antibodies via enzyme-labeled reactions.
Antibiogramma (antibiogram) | Mikrobning turli antibiotiklarga sezgirligini aniqlash natijasi. | Antibiogram | Result of testing a microbe's susceptibility to antibiotics.
Yallig'lanish (inflammation) | To'qima shikastlanishiga qon tomir va hujayra reaksiyalarini o'z ichiga olgan himoya javob; qizarish, issiqlik, shish, og'riq bilan namoyon bo'ladi. | Inflammation | Protective vascular and cellular response to tissue injury, showing redness, heat, swelling, and pain.
Eksudat (exudate) | Yallig'lanishda tomirlardan to'qimaga chiqadigan oqsilga boy suyuqlik. | Exudate | Protein-rich fluid that leaves vessels into tissue during inflammation.
Transsudat (transudate) | Yallig'lanishsiz sabablarga ko'ra to'planadigan oqsili kam suyuqlik. | Transudate | Protein-poor fluid accumulating from non-inflammatory causes.
Abssess (abscess) | To'qimada yiring to'planib, kapsula bilan o'ralgan o'choq. | Abscess | Localized collection of pus walled off in tissue.
Flegmona (phlegmon) | Yumshoq to'qimalarning chegarasiz tarqaluvchi yiringli yallig'lanishi. | Phlegmon | Diffuse spreading purulent inflammation of soft tissue.
Furunkul (furuncle) | Soch follikulasi va uni o'rab turgan to'qimaning o'tkir yiringli yallig'lanishi. | Furuncle | Acute purulent inflammation of a hair follicle and surrounding tissue.
Granuloma (granuloma) | Makrofaglar to'plamidan iborat surunkali yallig'lanish o'chog'i. | Granuloma | Focus of chronic inflammation made up of aggregated macrophages.
Giperplaziya (hyperplasia) | Hujayralar sonining ko'payishi. | Hyperplasia | Increase in the number of cells.
Gipertrofiya (hypertrophy) | Hujayralar hajmining kattalashishi, hujayralar soni o'zgarmagan holda. | Hypertrophy | Increase in cell size without an increase in cell number.
Atrofiya (atrophy) | A'zo yoki to'qima hajmining kamayishi. | Atrophy | Decrease in the size of an organ or tissue.
Metaplaziya (metaplasia) | Bir turdagi yetilgan to'qimaning boshqa turga aylanishi. | Metaplasia | Replacement of one mature cell type by another.
Displaziya (dysplasia) | Hujayralarning tartibsiz o'sishi va atipiyasi; saratondan oldingi o'zgarish bo'lishi mumkin. | Dysplasia | Disordered cell growth with atypia that may precede cancer.
Neoplaziya (neoplasia) | Nazoratsiz hujayra o'sishi natijasida o'sma hosil bo'lishi. | Neoplasia | Formation of a tumor through uncontrolled cell growth.
Xavfsiz o'sma (benign tumor) | Mahalliy o'sadigan, metastaz bermaydigan o'sma. | Benign tumor | Tumor that grows locally and does not metastasize.
Xavfli o'sma (malignant tumor) | Atrofdagi to'qimalarga bostirib kiradigan va metastaz beradigan o'sma. | Malignant tumor | Tumor that invades surrounding tissue and can metastasize.
Metastaz (metastasis) | O'smaning boshlang'ich o'choqdan boshqa a'zolarga tarqalishi. | Metastasis | Spread of a tumor from its primary site to distant organs.
Karsinoma (carcinoma) | Epiteliy to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma. | Carcinoma | Malignant tumor arising from epithelial tissue.
Sarkoma (sarcoma) | Biriktiruvchi to'qimadan chiqadigan xavfli o'sma. | Sarcoma | Malignant tumor arising from connective tissue.
Adenoma (adenoma) | Bez epiteliyidan chiqadigan xavfsiz o'sma. | Adenoma | Benign tumor of glandular epithelium.
Lipoma (lipoma) | Yog' to'qimasidan chiqadigan xavfsiz o'sma. | Lipoma | Benign tumor of adipose tissue.
Melanoma (melanoma) | Melanotsitlardan chiqadigan xavfli teri o'smasi. | Melanoma | Malignant tumor derived from melanocytes.
Tugun (nodule) | Teri yoki a'zoda paypaslanadigan zich dumaloq hosila. | Nodule | Small firm rounded lesion palpable in skin or an organ.
Kist (cyst) | Suyuqlik yoki yarim suyuq massa bilan to'lgan, devori bor bo'shliq. | Cyst | Walled cavity filled with fluid or semi-solid material.
Infarkt (infarction) | Qon ta'minoti to'xtashi tufayli to'qima qismining nekrozi. | Infarction | Tissue necrosis caused by interruption of blood supply.
Ishemiya (ischemia) | A'zo yoki to'qimaning qon bilan ta'minlanishi kamayishi. | Ischemia | Reduced blood supply to an organ or tissue.
Dimlanish (congestion) | Venoz qon oqimining sekinlashishi tufayli a'zoda qon to'planishi. | Congestion | Accumulation of blood in an organ due to impaired venous outflow.
Qon quyilish (hemorrhage) | Qonning tomirdan tashqariga chiqishi. | Hemorrhage | Escape of blood from a vessel.
Gematoma (hematoma) | To'qimada to'plangan qon ivigan massasi. | Hematoma | Localized collection of clotted blood in tissue.
Tromb (thrombus) | Tomir ichida qon elementlaridan hosil bo'lgan zich massa. | Thrombus | Solid mass formed from blood components inside a vessel.
Aterom (atheroma) | Arteriya intimasida xolesterin va hujayralar to'planishidan hosil bo'lgan blyashka. | Atheroma | Plaque of cholesterol and cells in the arterial intima.
Fibroz (fibrosis) | To'qimada biriktiruvchi to'qima ortiqcha hosil bo'lishi. | Fibrosis | Excess formation of connective tissue in an organ.
Skleroz (sclerosis) | To'qima qattiqlashuvi, ko'pincha fibroz tufayli. | Sclerosis | Hardening of tissue, often due to fibrosis.
Kazeoz nekroz (caseous necrosis) | Sil uchun xos, pishloqsimon massaga o'xshash nekroz. | Caseous necrosis | Cheese-like necrosis characteristic of tuberculosis.
Koagulyatsion nekroz (coagulative necrosis) | Hujayra konturlari saqlangan holda oqsillar ivishi bilan kechadigan nekroz; infarktlarda uchraydi. | Coagulative necrosis | Necrosis with preserved cell outlines due to protein coagulation, as in infarcts.
Gangrena (gangrene) | To'qimaning nekrozi va ikkilamchi mikroblar bilan zararlanishi. | Gangrene | Tissue necrosis with secondary bacterial infection.
Distrofiya (dystrophy) | Hujayra va to'qimalarda modda almashinuvi buzilishi bilan o'tuvchi tuzilmaviy o'zgarishlar. | Dystrophy | Structural changes in cells and tissues caused by metabolic disorders.
Yog'li distrofiya (fatty change) | Hujayralarda, ko'pincha jigarda triglitseridlar to'planishi. | Fatty change (steatosis) | Accumulation of triglycerides in cells, most often hepatocytes.
Kaxeksiya (cachexia) | Organizmning keskin ozishi va holsizlanishi. | Cachexia | Severe wasting with loss of weight and muscle.
Regeneratsiya (regeneration) | Shikastlangan to'qima o'rnida yangi hujayralar hosil bo'lishi. | Regeneration | Replacement of damaged tissue by new cells.
Yara bitishi (wound healing) | Yara o'rnida granulyatsion to'qima orqali nuqson qoplanishi. | Wound healing | Closure of a tissue defect through granulation tissue formation.
Autopsiya (autopsy) | O'lim sababini aniqlash uchun jasadni tekshirish. | Autopsy | Postmortem examination to determine cause of death.
Gistologiya (histology) | To'qimalarning mikroskopik tuzilishi haqidagi fan. | Histology | Science of the microscopic structure of tissues.
Sitologiya (cytology) | Hujayralarning tuzilishi va faoliyatini o'rganuvchi fan. | Cytology | Study of the structure and function of cells.
