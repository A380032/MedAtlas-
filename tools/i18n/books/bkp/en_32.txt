s6644 1) Presumptive diagnosis and today's condition? 2) Name the four components of the defect. 3) What is the meaning of squatting? 4) What X-ray changes are expected? 5) First aid in the spell?
s6645 1) Tetralogy of Fallot; today — a hypercyanotic spell. 2) Pulmonary stenosis, a large VSD, right ventricular hypertrophy, dextroposition of the aorta. 3) Squatting compresses the systemic arteries and increases peripheral resistance — the right-to-left shunt decreases, more blood goes to the lungs, and cyanosis decreases. 4) A “wooden shoe”-shaped heart, reduced lung markings. 5) Calming, the knee-chest position, humidified oxygen, on the doctor's order morphine, sodium bicarbonate, propranolol; cardiac glycosides must not be given; urgent hospitalization.
