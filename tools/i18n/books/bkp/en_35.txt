s7004 **osteoarticular** syndrome — pain in the tubular bones and joints (ossalgia), limping;
s7005 **infectious complications** (neutropenia), neuroleukemia (meningeal signs).
s7006 **Hemogram:** normochromic anemia, reticulocytopenia, **thrombocytopenia**, leukocytosis (or leukopenia), **blast cells**, **“leukemic hiatus”** (no intermediate forms between the blasts and the mature granulocytes), raised ESR. **Myelogram:** blasts **≥20–25%** (the diagnostic criterion), the normal hematopoietic lineages depressed.
s7007 11.8. Clinical section
s7008 A child with anemia — diagnostic algorithm
s7009 **Confirm the anemia** and determine its severity (Hb by the age cut-off).
s7010 **Color index and MCV**: hypochromic-microcytic → iron deficiency (ferritin), thalassemia (Hb electrophoresis); hyperchromic-macrocytic → B12 or folate deficiency; normochromic → continue.
s7011 **Reticulocytes**: high → hemolysis (bilirubin, Coombs, erythrocyte morphology) or acute blood loss; low → bone marrow failure.
s7012 **Other lineages**: leukocytes and platelets are also reduced (pancytopenia) or blasts are present → aplastic anemia or **leukemia** — myelogram.
s7013 Determine **the cause**: feeding, blood loss (gastrointestinal, helminthiasis), malabsorption, prematurity.
s7014 **Prevention of iron deficiency anemia:** iron preparations for pregnant and breastfeeding mothers, exclusive breastfeeding up to 6 months, iron-rich complementary foods from 6 months (meat, fortified cereals), no whole cow's milk before 1 year, prophylactic iron preparations for preterm babies from 2–4 weeks.

s7022 Skill 2. Determining the bleeding time (Duke)
s7023 Wipe the earlobe or fingertip with alcohol and let it dry.
s7024 Prick to a depth of 3–4 mm with a sterile lancet; start the stopwatch.
s7025 Every 15–30 seconds touch the emerging drop of blood with filter paper (without touching the skin).
s7026 Record the time when no more blood mark remains on the paper.
s7027 Assessment: normally 2–4 minutes; prolongation — disturbance of the platelet component.
s7028 Skill 3. Determining the clotting time (Morawitz or Sitkovsky method)
s7029 Place a drop of blood from the finger on a dry, clean glass slide; record the time.
s7030 Every 30 seconds pass a glass rod (or needle) through the drop.
s7031 The appearance of the first fibrin thread — the beginning of clotting; complete clotting of the drop — the end.
s7032 Compare with the norm for the method (Morawitz: 3–5 minutes).
s7033 Skill 4. Examining a child with a hemorrhagic syndrome

s7035 Inspection: type of rash (petechiae, ecchymoses, hematoma), location, symmetry, color; joints (hemarthrosis); mucous membranes.
s7036 Perform the endothelial tests (tourniquet, pinch).
s7037 Palpate the lymph nodes, liver and spleen.
s7038 Determine the type of bleeding (hematoma, petechial-spotted, mixed, vasculitic-purpuric, angiomatous).
s7039 Draw up an investigation plan: CBC and platelets, bleeding and clotting time, coagulogram, urinalysis (hematuria), stool for occult blood.
