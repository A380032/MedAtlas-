s7003 11.8. Clinical section
s7004 A child with anemia — diagnostic algorithm
s7005 **Confirm the anemia** and determine its severity (Hb by the age cut-off).
s7006 **Color index and MCV**: hypochromic-microcytic → iron deficiency (ferritin), thalassemia (Hb electrophoresis); hyperchromic-macrocytic → B12 or folate deficiency; normochromic → continue.
s7007 **Reticulocytes**: high → hemolysis (bilirubin, Coombs, erythrocyte morphology) or acute blood loss; low → bone marrow failure.
s7008 **Other lineages**: leukocytes and platelets are also reduced (pancytopenia) or blasts are present → aplastic anemia or **leukemia** — myelogram.
s7009 Determine **the cause**: feeding, blood loss (gastrointestinal, helminthiasis), malabsorption, prematurity.
s7010 **Prevention of iron deficiency anemia:** iron preparations for pregnant and breastfeeding mothers, exclusive breastfeeding up to 6 months, iron-rich complementary foods from 6 months (meat, fortified cereals), no whole cow's milk before 1 year, prophylactic iron preparations for preterm babies from 2–4 weeks.

s7018 Skill 2. Determining the bleeding time (Duke)
s7019 Wipe the earlobe or fingertip with alcohol and let it dry.
s7020 Prick to a depth of 3–4 mm with a sterile lancet; start the stopwatch.
s7021 Every 15–30 seconds touch the emerging drop of blood with filter paper (without touching the skin).
s7022 Record the time when no more blood mark remains on the paper.
s7023 Assessment: normally 2–4 minutes; prolongation — disturbance of the platelet component.
s7024 Skill 3. Determining the clotting time (Morawitz or Sitkovsky method)
s7025 Place a drop of blood from the finger on a dry, clean glass slide; record the time.
s7026 Every 30 seconds pass a glass rod (or needle) through the drop.
s7027 The appearance of the first fibrin thread — the beginning of clotting; complete clotting of the drop — the end.
s7028 Compare with the norm for the method (Morawitz: 3–5 minutes).
s7029 Skill 4. Examining a child with a hemorrhagic syndrome
s7030 History: onset of bleeding, relation to injury, delayed bleeding, bleeding in the family (the mother's male relatives), preceding infection or vaccination.
s7031 Inspection: type of rash (petechiae, ecchymoses, hematoma), location, symmetry, color; joints (hemarthrosis); mucous membranes.
s7032 Perform the endothelial tests (tourniquet, pinch).
s7033 Palpate the lymph nodes, liver and spleen.
s7034 Determine the type of bleeding (hematoma, petechial-spotted, mixed, vasculitic-purpuric, angiomatous).
s7035 Draw up an investigation plan: CBC and platelets, bleeding and clotting time, coagulogram, urinalysis (hematuria), stool for occult blood.
