s3306 5.7. Clinical section
s3307 A child suspected of rickets
s3308 Rickets is diagnosed on the basis of clinical signs (sweating, bald nape, hypotonia, bone changes) and confirmed by **biochemistry** (reduced calcium and phosphorus, **raised alkaline phosphatase**, reduced 25(OH)D) and **X-ray** (wrist: cup-shaped widening of the metaphyses, a “fringed” border of the growth zone, osteoporosis).
s3309 **Prevention of rickets:** during pregnancy — fresh air, proper nutrition and vitamin D for the mother; after birth — **breast milk**, walks, massage and gymnastics; for term babies from 1 month of age **vitamin D 400–500 IU/day** (according to Uzbekistan and WHO recommendations, doses are set by the local protocol), more for preterm babies.
s3310 Joint syndrome — diagnostic approach
s3311 **How many joints?** Mono- (1), oligo- (2–4), polyarthritis (≥5).
s3312 **Is it symmetric?** Symmetric — JRA, SLE; asymmetric — reactive, septic.
s3313 **What is the course?** Migratory — rheumatic fever; persistent, progressive — JRA.
s3314 **Additional signs:** fever, rash (SLE, Still's disease, vasculitis), conjunctivitis (Reiter), heart murmur (rheumatic fever), preceding infection.
s3315 **Laboratory:** CBC, CRP, ESR, ASO, rheumatoid factor, ANA, HLA-B27, synovial fluid.
s3316 A newborn with muscle hypotonia
s3317 Hypotonia is “not a disease but a sign”. Its causes are **central** (CNS damage, hypoxia, syndromes) and **peripheral** (spinal muscular atrophy, myopathies, myasthenia). In central hypotonia the reflexes are preserved or increased; in peripheral hypotonia they are **reduced or absent**, and muscle weakness is pronounced.

s3325 Skill 2. Inspection of the chest and spine
s3326 The child is undressed and stands (arms along the body).

s3332 Skill 3. Examination of the joints
s3333 Observe the gait.
s3334 Inspect each joint symmetrically: shape, swelling, defiguration, deformity, skin color.
s3335 Palpation: temperature (comparing with the back of the hand), pain, swelling, fluid (ballottement of the patella), crepitus.
s3336 Assess the range of active, then passive movements with functional tests; record pain and restriction.
s3337 Mobility of the spine (finger-to-floor distance, Ott's and Schober's tests).
s3338 Conclusion: the shape of the joints is unchanged, there are no signs of inflammation, movements are in full range and painless (or the changes found).
s3339 Skill 4. Testing for developmental dysplasia of the hip (newborn)
s3340 The child lies on the back on a warm table, calm.
s3341 Assess the symmetry of the thigh, inguinal and gluteal folds (gluteal folds — with the child prone).
s3342 Bend the legs at the hips and knees and compare the levels of the knees (relative shortening).
s3343 Flex the thighs to 90° and slowly spread them apart; assess the abduction angle.
s3344 Marx–Ortolani test: determine whether a “click” is felt while the thighs are spread.
s3345 If there is any suspicion — orthopedic consultation and hip ultrasound.
s3346 Skill 5. Assessment of the muscular system
s3347 Inspection: degree of development, relief, symmetry; measure the limb circumference if necessary.
s3348 Palpation: consistency, pain, indurations.
s3349 Tone: the child's posture; resistance to passive flexion-extension; in the newborn, the recoil, traction and “loose shoulders” tests.
s3350 Strength: movements against resistance; dynamometry in an older child.
s3351 Range of active and passive movements.
s3352 Conclusion: “The muscles are moderately developed, symmetric, tone normal, strength appropriate for age, painless, movements in full range.”
s3353 Skill 6. Testing for the signs of latent tetany
s3354 **Chvostek:** tap with a hammer or finger below the zygomatic arch, in front of the ear, where the facial nerve emerges; watch for contraction of the corner of the mouth, the nasal ala and the eyelid.
s3355 **Trousseau:** place a sphygmomanometer cuff on the upper arm, inflate it slightly above systolic pressure and hold for 2–3 minutes; watch for the “obstetrician's hand”.
s3356 **Lust:** tap with a hammer below the head of the fibula; watch for outward turning and dorsal flexion of the foot.
s3357 Record the result; if positive — refer for a blood calcium test.
