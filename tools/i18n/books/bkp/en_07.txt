s1351 3.1. Anatomical and physiological features of the nervous system
s1352 The nervous system connects the body with the external environment and maintains the internal balance (homeostasis). At birth the nervous system is not fully formed anatomically and functionally — this determines the specific features of the child's motor, sensory and mental life.
s1353 **The fetal period.** The nervous system begins to form from the ectoderm as the neural tube in the 3rd–4th week of intrauterine development. **The critical period of brain development is weeks 10–18 of pregnancy** (nerve cells divide most intensively). At birth the child has 25% of the cells of the CNS, and at 1 year 90–95%.
