s6825 Inspection and palpation
s6826 **General condition:** impaired consciousness (with a rapid fall in Hb or blood volume — anemic coma, hypovolemic shock), ongoing bleeding.
s6827 **Color of the skin and mucous membranes: pallor** (anemia; marked pallor — acute blood loss); pallor + **a lemon-yellow tint** (hemolytic anemia, B12 deficiency); waxy pallor (leukemia); **cherry-red** (polycythemia); perioral cyanosis (hypoxia in severe anemia).
s6828 **Hemorrhagic rash:** type (petechiae, purpura, ecchymoses, hematoma), location, symmetry, color (different colors — different ages), circumstances of appearance.
s6829 **Skin appendages and mucous membranes:** dry skin, brittle hair and nails, **koilonychia**, angular stomatitis (“cheilitis”), **a “lacquered” or “geographic” tongue**, glossitis, gum hyperplasia and bleeding (leukemia).
s6830 **Lymph nodes, liver and spleen** (by the methods of topic 4): enlargement, consistency, adhesion; **hepatosplenomegaly** — systemic blood diseases.
s6831 **Joints:** hemarthrosis (hemophilia), joint syndrome (hemorrhagic vasculitis, leukemia).
s6832 **Bones:** pain on tapping (sternum, ribs, spine, tibia — leukemia).
s6833 **Cardiovascular:** tachycardia, a functional systolic murmur at the apex (anemia).
s6834 **Endothelial tests** (tourniquet, pinch, hammer — topic 4) — the state of the vascular wall and platelets.
s6835 Laboratory investigations
s6836 **Complete (clinical) blood count** — from a finger (from the heel in the newborn) or a vein; hemoglobin, erythrocytes, color index, reticulocytes, leukocytes and **the leukocyte differential**, platelets, ESR, hematocrit; on an automated analyzer — the erythrocyte indices (MCV, MCH, MCHC, RDW). **ESR** by the Panchenkov method: the column of plasma (mm) above the erythrocytes that have settled in a capillary in 1 hour.

s6839 Assessment of hemostasis:
s6840 **Bleeding time (Duke)** — the finger or earlobe is pricked with a lancet and the drops of blood are blotted onto filter paper every 15–30 seconds; normally **2–4 minutes**. Prolongation — **reduced platelet count or impaired platelet function** (platelet component).
s6841 **Clotting time** — assesses the plasma component; the norm depends on the method: **Morawitz** — beginning 3, end 5 minutes; **Bürker** — 2.5 and 5.5 minutes; **Lee–White** (venous blood) — 5–10 minutes; **Sitkovsky** — 1.5 and 4 minutes. Prolongation — **deficiency of coagulation factors** (hemophilia).
s6842 **Coagulogram:** activated partial thromboplastin time (**APTT**, 25–55 s), prothrombin index (70–110%) or **INR**, thrombin time (14–16 s), **fibrinogen** (1.7–3.5 g/l), antithrombin III, D-dimers, individual factors (VIII, IX), clot retraction (60–75%), platelet aggregation.
s6843 **Other investigations:** iron metabolism parameters, bilirubin and its fractions, hemoglobin electrophoresis (thalassemia), osmotic resistance of erythrocytes (spherocytosis), Coombs test (immune hemolysis), vitamin B12 and folate; **myelogram** (bone marrow aspiration) and trephine biopsy; lymph node biopsy; cytochemical, immunophenotypic, cytogenetic and molecular methods (leukemias).

s6854 Changes in erythrocytes

s6929 Changes in ESR
s6930 **Increase:** increased fibrinogen and globulins (inflammation, infections, sepsis), anemia, connective tissue diseases, kidney diseases (nephrotic syndrome), leukemias, lymphomas, tumors, after vaccination.
s6931 **Decrease:** the neonatal period, erythrocytosis and hemoconcentration (hematocrit >50%), hyperbilirubinemia, acidosis.
s6932 Changes in hemostasis
s6933 **Hypocoagulation** (tendency to bleed) — reduced platelets or impaired platelet function, deficiency of clotting factors (hemophilia, liver diseases, vitamin K deficiency, obstructive jaundice), an excess of anticoagulants.
s6934 **Hypercoagulation** (tendency to thrombosis) — hereditary thrombophilias (deficiency of antithrombin III, proteins C and S), stage 1 of DIC syndrome, antiphospholipid syndrome, burns, major injuries, after surgery, nephrotic syndrome.
s6935 **DIC syndrome** (disseminated intravascular coagulation) — in severe infections (sepsis, meningococcemia), shock, extensive injuries, tumors, hemolysis, exsicosis: first the clotting factors are sharply activated, with **disseminated thromboses** in the small vessels (impaired microcirculation, organ failure), then the factors and platelets are **consumed** (“consumption coagulopathy”) and fibrinolysis is activated, resulting in **bleeding from many sites**.
s6936 11.7. Main syndromes of blood system involvement
s6937 Anemic syndrome
s6938 Signs related to tissue hypoxia due to reduced hemoglobin:
s6939 **pallor** of the skin and mucous membranes (conjunctiva, lips, nail beds, palms);
s6940 weakness, rapid fatigue, lethargy, reduced appetite, dizziness, headache, darkening before the eyes, tinnitus, fainting;
s6941 dyspnea on exertion, **tachycardia**, a **functional systolic murmur** at the apex, a “humming top” over the neck veins, reduced BP;
s6942 in severe anemia — enlarged cardiac borders, heart failure, anemic coma.
s6943 Sideropenic syndrome
s6944 Signs of tissue iron deficiency in iron deficiency anemia:
s6945 dystrophy of the skin and appendages: **dry skin**, **hair loss and brittleness**, **brittle nails, transverse striations, koilonychia** (spoon-shaped nails);
s6946 atrophy of the mucous membranes: nose, esophagus (difficulty swallowing), stomach (dyspepsia, malabsorption); **angular stomatitis**, glossitis, “lacquered” tongue, gingivitis;
s6947 **distorted taste and smell** — eating chalk, soil, ice, raw meat, dough (**pica**), liking the smell of gasoline, acetone, paint;
s6948 muscle hypotonia and pain (myoglobin deficiency), including of the bladder sphincter — **nocturnal enuresis**, urine leakage on laughing and coughing;
s6949 reduced memory and attention, emotional lability, drowsiness; delay in the child's psychomotor development;
s6950 frequent infections (immunodeficiency).
s6951 **Hemolytic syndrome:** anemia + **jaundice** (lemon-yellow tint, raised indirect bilirubin) + **splenomegaly** + dark urine (urobilin) + reticulocytosis. In hemolytic crises — fever, weakness, abdominal pain, marked jaundice, deepening anemia.
s6952 Hemorrhagic syndrome
s6953 **Hemorrhagic syndrome** — a tendency to bleed: bleeding into cavities and/or hemorrhages into the skin, joints and internal organs. According to which component of hemostasis is disturbed: **coagulopathies** (plasma factors), **thrombocytopenias and thrombocytopathies** (platelet component), **vasopathies** (vascular component).

s6993 Lymphoproliferative (hyperplastic) syndrome
s6994 **Enlargement of the lymph nodes, liver and spleen** (one or several groups of nodes): leukemias, lymphomas, lymphogranulomatosis, histiocytoses, infectious mononucleosis and other infections (see topic 4).
