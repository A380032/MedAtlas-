s8900 14.7. Examination of the immune system
s8901 **History and inspection:** frequent and severe infections, recurrences, opportunistic infections, reactions to vaccination (complications after BCG), allergic diseases, family history (death from infection at an early age); examination of the lymph nodes, tonsils and spleen (see topic 4); **absence or hypoplasia** of the tonsils and lymph nodes — a sign of humoral immunodeficiency.
s8902 Immunogram (laboratory methods):
s8903 **Cell-mediated immunity:** the number of lymphocytes and T lymphocytes (at 7–14 years T cells make up **≈70%** of lymphocytes, ≈1400/µl), the **CD3, CD4, CD8** subpopulations and **the CD4/CD8 ratio** (normal 1.5–2), the blast transformation reaction with phytohemagglutinin, cytokines; **skin tests** (Mantoux); chest X-ray (thymic hypoplasia or thymomegaly).
s8904 **Humoral immunity:** the number of B lymphocytes (CD19, CD20; ≈25%, ≈500/µl), **serum IgG, IgA, IgM, IgE**, secretory IgA (in saliva), circulating immune complexes, specific post-vaccination antibodies.
s8905 **Complement system:** total hemolytic activity (CH50), C3, C4, C1 inhibitor.
s8906 **Phagocytosis:** the neutrophil count (**neutropenia** — absolute count <1500/µl), the phagocytic index and number, **the NBT test** (nitroblue tetrazolium — bactericidal activity), chemotaxis, adhesion molecules (CD11/CD18).
s8907 14.8. Semiotics of immune system involvement
s8908 **3 types** of immune system involvement: 1) quantitative or functional insufficiency of a component of the immune system (**primary and secondary immunodeficiencies**); 2) impaired recognition of self antigens (**autoimmune diseases**); 3) a hyperreactive immune response (**allergic reactions**).
s8909 The 10 warning signs of primary immunodeficiency
s8910 **6–8 or more** episodes of otitis a year.
s8911 **4–6** severe sinusitis episodes a year.
s8912 **More than 2** severe pneumonias a year.
s8913 Recurrent **deep abscesses** of the skin or internal organs.
s8914 **More than 2** severe infections (osteomyelitis, sepsis, meningitis).
s8915 **Persistent oral candidiasis** or fungal infection of the skin in a child over 1 year.
s8916 The need for **2 months or more** of antibiotic treatment (ineffective).
s8917 The need for **intravenous antibiotics** to clear an infection.
s8918 **Primary immunodeficiency** or deaths from infection at an early age in the family history.
s8919 **Failure to gain weight and growth retardation** in an infant.
s8920 If there are two or more signs — an immunology consultation and an immunogram are needed. **Live vaccines** (BCG, oral poliomyelitis vaccine, measles) are **contraindicated** in cell-mediated and combined immunodeficiency — risk of generalized vaccine infection.

s8943 14.9. Clinical section
s8944 **1–3 weeks after tonsillitis, scarlet fever or pyoderma** — the “**nephritic triad**”: **edema** (face and eyelids in the morning), **arterial hypertension** (headache, vomiting; in a severe case — **eclampsia**: seizures, loss of consciousness), **urine the color of “meat washings”** (macrohematuria) and oliguria. Laboratory: proteinuria 1–2 g/l, erythrocyte casts; in the blood **raised ASO**, **reduced complement C3** (recovers in 6–8 weeks), raised ESR. Treatment: bed rest, restriction of salt and fluid, penicillin, antihypertensive drugs; the main dangers are hypertensive encephalopathy, acute heart failure and acute kidney injury.

s8952 Skill 2. Bimanual palpation of the kidneys (Obraztsov–Strazhesko)
s8953 The child lies on the back with the legs slightly bent; the doctor on the right; warm hands.
s8954 Place the left hand under the lower back, in the angle between rib XII and the long muscles.
s8955 Place the right hand outside the rectus muscle below the costal arch and plunge it deep during expiration.
s8956 Bring the hands together and try to feel the lower pole of the kidney; on deep inspiration the kidney slides under the fingers.
