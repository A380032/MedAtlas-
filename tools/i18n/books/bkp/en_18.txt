s3635 6.1. Anatomical and physiological features of the respiratory organs
s3636 **Main idea:** in the child all the airways are **narrow, soft, rich in blood and lymph vessels and poor in elastic fibers**. For this reason the mucosa swells quickly, the airways become obstructed quickly, and atelectasis and pneumonia are common.
s3637 The airways are divided into three parts: **upper** (nose, paranasal sinuses, pharynx), **middle** (larynx, trachea, main, lobar and segmental bronchi), **lower** (bronchioles, alveoli).

s3692 6.2. Mechanism of the first breath
s3693 **Main idea:** clamping of the cord → **hypoxia + hypercapnia + acidosis** → chemoreceptors → respiratory center → **sharp contraction of the diaphragm** → the lungs expand, and surfactant keeps them open.
s3694 **The chemical (humoral) factor is the most important.** When the cord is clamped, pO2 falls from 80 to ≈15 mm Hg, pCO2 rises from 40 to ≈70 mm, and pH falls below 7.35. The central chemoreceptors are sensitive to pH, the peripheral (carotid, aortic) chemoreceptors to O2 and CO2.
s3695 **Physical (external) stimuli:** cold, light, stimulation of skin receptors, gravity → reticular formation → the excitability of the respiratory center increases.
s3696 **Removing inhibiting factors:** suctioning mucus and amniotic fluid from the mouth and nose.
s3697 **Mechanics:** the diaphragm contracts and creates negative intrathoracic pressure (≈80 mm H2O); the volume of the first breath exceeds 80 ml. Lung fluid is absorbed into the lymphatics and capillaries. A healthy baby takes the first breath with **a loud cry**.

s3700 **Surfactant** — a surface-active substance covering the alveolar surface (90% lipid, mainly phosphatidylcholine; 10% protein). It is synthesized by **type II alveolocytes**. It prevents the alveoli from collapsing on expiration and has a bactericidal effect. Synthesis enzymes: methyltransferase — active **from week 22–24**, phosphocholine transferase — **from week 35**. Surfactant deficiency causes **respiratory distress syndrome** (hyaline membrane disease), mainly in preterm babies.
s3701 If there is a risk of preterm birth (before 34 weeks), the mother is given **glucocorticoids** (dexamethasone or betamethasone) — they accelerate surfactant synthesis in the fetal lungs. Exogenous surfactant is given intratracheally to the preterm newborn.
s3702 **The respiratory center** (medulla oblongata and pons): the medullary part (alternation of inspiration and expiration), the apneustic part (inspiratory spasm), the pneumotaxic part (inhibits the apneustic part). The pneumotaxic part matures **by the end of the 1st year** — at an early age breathing is **arrhythmic**; **apnea** occurs in preterm babies. Up to 3 months, pauses in breathing of **up to 8–10 seconds** are physiological; apnea longer than 20 seconds or accompanied by bradycardia and cyanosis is pathological.
