s5181 **Cardiometry** — measuring the dimensions of the heart on an X-ray. The most widely used parameter is **the cardiothoracic index (CTI)**:
s5182 **CTI = (CE + ED) / AB**, where CE is the distance from the midline to the outermost point of the right cardiac contour, ED — from the midline to the outermost point of the left contour, AB — the inner transverse diameter of the chest at the level of the diaphragmatic dome.
s5183 **Normal:** **≤0.55** in young children, **≤0.5** in older children. An increased index means **cardiomegaly**.

s5186 Pathological configurations:
s5187 **mitral** — the “cardiac waist” is flattened (the arcs of the pulmonary artery and the left atrial appendage bulge): mitral stenosis and insufficiency;
s5188 **aortic** — the “cardiac waist” is deepened, the left ventricle enlarged, the apex displaced downward and to the left (“sitting duck”): aortic defects, arterial hypertension;
s5189 **spherical or triangular (“trapezoid”)** — exudative pericarditis;
s5190 **“wooden shoe” (“Dutch clog”)** — **tetralogy of Fallot** (raised apex, deep “cardiac waist”, reduced lung markings);

s5198 8.9. ECG and echocardiography — basic concepts
s5199 **Electrocardiography (ECG)** — recording of the electrophysiological processes in the heart; it gives information on the rhythm, HR, conduction, hypertrophy of the heart chambers and the state of the myocardium. 12 standard leads: 3 standard (I, II, III), 3 augmented (aVR, aVL, aVF) and 6 chest (V~1~–V~6~) leads.
s5200 **P wave** — atrial excitation;
s5201 **PQ interval** — conduction of the impulse from the atria to the ventricles;
s5202 **QRS complex** — ventricular excitation (depolarization);
s5203 **ST segment and T wave** — ventricular repolarization;
s5204 **QT interval** — electrical systole of the ventricles.
s5205 HR = 60 / RR (in seconds). The age-related features of the ECG in children (right axis deviation, short waves and intervals, negative T waves) are given in detail in topic 9.
s5206 **Echocardiography** — ultrasound examination of the structure and function of the heart (2.5–7.5 MHz probe); **the main method in pediatric cardiology**. Its possibilities:
s5207 the size of the heart chambers, wall thickness, **contractility** (ejection fraction, normally ≥55–60%);
s5208 the structure and movement of the valves, **regurgitation** and **stenosis** (Doppler echocardiography);
s5209 **congenital defects** (septal defects, patent ductus arteriosus, tetralogy of Fallot, etc.) — the direction and volume of the shunt;
s5210 pulmonary artery pressure;
s5211 the state of the pericardium and **fluid in the pericardial cavity**;
s5212 intracardiac formations (thrombus, tumor, vegetations — infective endocarditis, false chords).
s5213 For early detection of congenital heart defects in newborns, **pulse oximetry screening** is carried out: SpO~2~ is measured on the right hand and a foot at 24–48 hours of life. If **SpO~2~ <95%** or the difference between the hand and the foot is **>3%**, the baby is referred for echocardiography.

s5226 The child stands or lies, the doctor on the right.

s5232 Skill 3. Examining the pulse in the peripheral arteries

s5237 Skill 4. Calculating the cardiothoracic index
s5238 On a frontal X-ray draw the midline (along the spinous processes).
