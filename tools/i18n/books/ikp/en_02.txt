s0334 9. Special situations in analyzing complaints
s0335 9.1. Features related to age, sex and occupation
s0336 The same complaint is interpreted differently in different patients:
s0337 **Age:** chest pain in a 20-year-old man is most often due to a musculoskeletal or pleural cause, whereas in a 60-year-old male smoker it is due to angina pectoris; constipation or weight loss appearing for the first time in the elderly raises suspicion of a tumor.
s0338 **Sex:** in women, angina pectoris often runs an atypical course (weakness, dyspnea, nausea); iron deficiency anemia, thyroid diseases and systemic lupus erythematosus are more common in women.
s0339 **Occupation:** silicosis in miners and stonecutters; byssinosis and the effects of pesticides in cotton growing; diseases of the vocal apparatus in teachers and singers; low back pain, hemorrhoids and ischemic heart disease in drivers; viral hepatitis and tuberculosis in health workers.

s0400 A modern model of the consultation
s0401 According to the **Calgary–Cambridge model**, widely used in international practice, the consultation consists of the following stages:
s0402 **Initiating the session** — introductions, identifying the reason for the patient’s visit, agreeing on the plan of the conversation.
s0403 **Gathering information** — moving from open to closed questions, identifying the patient’s ideas, concerns and expectations.
