s2187 A 63-year-old man has had chronic glomerulonephritis for 15 years. In recent months he has had nausea, weakness and itching of the skin; blood creatinine and urea are sharply increased. On auscultation a friction rub is heard over the heart. The patient died.

s2244 6.1. The concept of chronic inflammation
s2245 **Chronic inflammation** is inflammation of prolonged duration (weeks, months, years) in which **active inflammation, tissue damage** and **attempts at healing (repair)** proceed simultaneously. It may follow acute inflammation or begin as chronic from the start (insidious, without symptoms).
s2246 Causes:
s2247 **Persistent infections** — microorganisms that are difficult to eradicate: Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Treponema pallidum (syphilis), the leprosy bacillus, fungi, some viruses (hepatitis B and C), parasites. They often elicit a **delayed-type hypersensitivity** (type IV) reaction and lead to **granulomatous** inflammation.
s2248 **Immune-mediated inflammatory diseases** — autoimmune diseases (rheumatoid arthritis, systemic lupus erythematosus, multiple sclerosis), allergic diseases (bronchial asthma), inflammatory bowel diseases (Crohn disease, ulcerative colitis).
s2249 **Prolonged exposure to endogenous or exogenous toxic agents** — silicon dioxide (silicosis), asbestos, atherosclerosis (lipids).
s2250 It has been established that **latent chronic inflammation** also underlies some **chronic diseases** (atherosclerosis, type 2 diabetes mellitus, obesity, Alzheimer disease).
