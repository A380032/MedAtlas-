s2182 A 63-year-old man has had chronic glomerulonephritis for 15 years. In recent months he has had nausea, weakness and itching of the skin; blood creatinine and urea are sharply increased. On auscultation a friction rub is heard over the heart. The patient died.

s2184 1) Fibrinous pericarditis (“hairy heart”) — the epicardium is dull, rough and covered with hairlike fibrin threads; microscopically — a pink fibrin network and leukocytes. 2) Uremia (the final stage of chronic kidney disease): nitrogenous wastes accumulated in the blood are excreted through the serous membranes, damage them and increase vascular permeability — a fibrinogen-rich exudate escapes and fibrin forms. s2239 6.1. The concept of chronic inflammation
s2240 **Chronic inflammation** is inflammation of prolonged duration (weeks, months, years) in which **active inflammation, tissue damage** and **attempts at healing (repair)** proceed simultaneously. It may follow acute inflammation or begin as chronic from the start (insidious, without symptoms).
s2241 Causes:
s2242 **Persistent infections** — microorganisms that are difficult to eradicate: Mycobacterium tuberculosis, Treponema pallidum (syphilis), the leprosy bacillus, fungi, some viruses (hepatitis B and C), parasites. They often elicit a **delayed-type hypersensitivity** (type IV) reaction and lead to **granulomatous** inflammation.
s2243 **Immune-mediated inflammatory diseases** — autoimmune diseases (rheumatoid arthritis, systemic lupus erythematosus, multiple sclerosis), allergic diseases (bronchial asthma), inflammatory bowel diseases (Crohn disease, ulcerative colitis).
s2244 **Prolonged exposure to endogenous or exogenous toxic agents** — silicon dioxide (silicosis), asbestos, atherosclerosis (lipids).
s2245 It has been established that **latent chronic inflammation** also underlies some **chronic diseases** (atherosclerosis, type 2 diabetes mellitus, obesity, Alzheimer disease). **Morphologic features of chronic inflammation** (Robbins):
s2246 **Infiltration by mononuclear cells** — macrophages, lymphocytes, plasma cells.
s2247 **Tissue destruction** — by the causative agent or inflammatory cells.
s2248 **Attempts at healing** — formation of new vessels (**angiogenesis**) and **fibrosis** (growth of connective tissue).
